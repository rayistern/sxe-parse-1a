from a friend or picked it up on their own, to
women with artistic flair looking to make a quick
buck, there are all types of people writing Stam.
However, in order for Stam to be kosher, the
f
person writing it must be thoroughly familiar
with all the relevant halochos. The Chasam Sofer,
however, says that knowledge is not enough. The
person must be tested by rabbonim and awarded
a ksav kabolo before being permitted to write
Stam. A sofer without kabolo, the Chasam Sofer
says, is “possul may’umnoso” and not allowed to
write Stam. This psak is universally accepted.
