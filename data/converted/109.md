An enclosed courtyard used for leisure or
storage requires a mezuzah.
A doorway must consist of a minimum of a right doorpost
and a lintel. An open layout in which the entrance between
rooms does not have a descending lintel, is considered like
one big room and the "doorways" do not require a mezuzah.
If besides support or décor they also separate between areas,
they do constitute a doorway and require a mezuzah.
An assessment is needed in each individual situation as to
whether the posts serve a function of distinguishing two areas,
and thus require a mezuzah, or if they are there just for other
reasons, such as in the photo below:
Posts which are installed for support or
for décor are not considered doorposts
and do not require a mezuzah.
While we have tried to cover some of the more common scenarios, there are countless more questions that can arise
when placing mezuzos. We hope that this article will bring the much needed awareness to the complexity of the matter,
and efforts will be made to inquire and verify that mezuzos are placed properly—allowing for the most precise observance
of the mitzvah and the greatest protection of the dwellers of the home.
