# Merkos 302 Moisdos & Programs - Complete Catalog

**Document Type**: Comprehensive Program Catalog
**Organization**: Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch, Suite 302
**Pages**: 48 pages
**Purpose**: Resource guide for Shluchim showcasing all available programs and support services

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive catalog details over 100 programs and resources offered by Merkos 302 to support Shluchim (Chabad emissaries) worldwide in their mission. The organization's stated goal is singular: "to help shluchim complete the last phase of their shlichus by preparing their makom hashlichus to be mekabel pnei Moshiach Tzidkeinu."

## Letter from Rabbi Mendy Kotlarsky

**Key Message**: Merkos 302 began with a single Kinus hachlatah 15 years ago and has grown to over 100 programs responding to Kinus hachlatos and pressing needs of Shluchim. The mission is to serve the Shluchim's mission until the shlichus is complete with the hisgalus of Melech HaMoshiach.

## MAJOR PROGRAM DIVISIONS

### 1. CKIDS - CHABAD CHILDREN'S NETWORK
**Established**: 2011

**Mission**: Help Shluchim reach every Jewish child through expansion of educational institutions by creating advanced curricula, experiential programming, teacher training, professional branding, competitions, trips, and shabbatons.

**Programs**:

#### CKids Hebrew School
Child-centered, innovative educational program teaching Jewish life events, holidays, reading Hebrew, and developing pride in Jewish identity.

#### JewQ
International Torah competition encouraging children to learn about, review, and take tests on the fundamentals of Yiddishkeit.

#### JewQ's GameZone
Online game site where children play fun and educational Jewish games, gaining Torah knowledge and excitement for Yiddishkeit.

#### CKids Shabbaton
Unforgettable three-day extravaganza in Crown Heights bringing together Jewish children from around the country to inspire pride in their Yiddishkeit.

#### CKids CGI Overnight
Three CKids Gan Israel Overnight Camps providing life-changing summer experiences. Hundreds of children from CKids Hebrew Schools have joined. Camps are independently run and fundraised by individual directors.

#### Staff Training
Empowering training for camp counselors, head counselors, and directors.

#### CKids CGI Day Camp
New cutting-edge customized curricula, creative programs and activities, staff training and placements, and networking so children wait all year to return to Camp Gan Israel.

#### CKids Tzivos Hashem Brigade
Network for Chabad House children encouraging every child to be an active member in Hashem's army through motivational point system, rewards, and rank promotions.

#### CKids Club
Educational, family-style programs that can be run independently or during Hebrew school or after-school programs with individualized boxes that help Shluchim take programs to the next level.

#### Advanced Curriculum Development
Skillful curriculum development for camps, schools, and programs to teach about Yiddishkeit in a meaningful and engaging way.

#### Kinusim
Yearly kinnus for youth directors to participate in training and enlightening lectures to improve their schools and camps.

#### Jewish Birthdays
Customizable, digital birthday packages sent to hundreds of children worldwide.

#### CKids Mezuzah Bank
In partnership with Yad Menachem: For every mezuzah a shliach puts up on a family home, CKids provides an additional mezuzah for a child's bedroom doorpost.

#### Holiday Tool Boxes
Specially created kit with everything students need to create a fun, exciting, and unforgettable family yom tov experience in their homes.

#### Tefillah Publications
Specialized siddurim and machzorim where students can write their own meditations and thoughts in invisible ink.

**For New Shluchim**:

**Programs**:

Course granting hospital recognized certification, giving tools to work with those coping with life-altering situations.

Resources guide through every step of journey within medical community. Many downloadable for free or minimal cost. Popular items include Tehillim and pop-up pushka.

#### New Shluchim Placement
Helping Shluchim whose main focus is servicing hospitals to maximize potential and provide seed grants to expand this area of shlichus.

#### Seder To Go
Complete seder experience in a box for Jewish patients.

#### Website
ChabadOnCall.com provides online hospital directory, educational courses, and variety of practical resources for medical professionals and patients.

#### Hospitality Suite
Creating series of Bikkur Cholim lounges and apartments near major medical centers.

#### Chicken Soup Conference
Professional training and opportunities to brainstorm, discuss, and develop ideas with other chaplains.

#### Shluchim Refuah
Confidential rofeh yedid network for Shluchim to help through medical and mental health challenges according to Rebbe's guidance.

### 5. JNET - JEWISH LEARNING NETWORK
**Established**: 2006

**Mission**: Provide business people, students, and homemakers with opportunity to take time from busy work week to study Torah. Volunteers bring warmth of Torah and Judaism to people whose situations restrict them from acquiring it in other ways.

**Programs**:

#### Chavrusa Program
JNet carefully matches baalei batim with members of Anash for weekly Torah study sessions on topic of their choice. Sessions typically half hour once a week. Many chavrusas have continued learning weekly for many years, guiding one another to true spiritual growth.

#### Learning Material
JNet partners with JLI, Kehot, and private individuals to produce and subsidize books and online content helping chavrusas easily find something meaningful to study. Extensive online resource page with links to websites.

#### JNet Connection Magazine
Monthly pamphlet describing incredible impact weekly learning has had on individual's journey in life. Distributed in Chabad houses and shuls worldwide and sent digitally to all JNet members.

#### Annual Volunteer Appreciation Dinner
JNet hosts annual appreciation dinner for volunteers with great food and entertainment, celebrating accomplishments of chavrusas over past year.

### 16. YALDEI SHLUCHEI HAREBBE (YSH)
**Established**: 2009

**Mission**: Shluchim who experience having child who is different often feel very alone. YSH provides financial support and appropriate programming for families of Shluchim raising children with different needs. By offering opportunities for service coordination, advocacy, financial assistance, and family and respite support, helps Shluchim continue serving Jews in their community.

**Programs**:

#### Family Care Coordination
Maintain ongoing contact with families and assist in identifying long- and short-term needs of child and family.

#### School and Camp Placement and Grants
Yaldei advises parents regarding school and camp placement for child and provides special grant subsidies for special-ed tuitions.

#### Therapy and Equipment Grants
YSH provides and assists with finding and funding expensive therapies and medical and home-accessibility equipment.

#### Mental Health Grants
Makes referrals and absorbs some costs involved in obtaining professional therapy for family members of special needs children.

#### Project Care and Project Smile
Design and send birthday gifts to parents and every child in family, letting them know they are cared for and not alone on challenging journey.

#### Respite Retreats and Grants
Offers and promotes various respite programs providing regular and at-will opportunities for parents to take break and recharge batteries, as well as covering costs during parents' break.

#### Networking
Coordinates peer-to-peer support contact network between parents facing similar challenges.

#### Yom Tov Packages
Sends care packages for yomim tovim to help keep kids entertained and deliver joy to entire family.

## For Shluchim

**Key Takeaways**:

1. **Comprehensive Support**: Merkos 302 offers 100+ programs covering every aspect of shlichus from children's education to young professionals, from hospital chaplaincy to legacy planning.

2. **Flexible Solutions**: Programs are designed to be flexible for every Chabad House with dedicated team standing by to help find right programs for each community.

3. **Continuous Development**: Merkos 302 constantly striving to advance programs. Shluchim feedback is critical in this process.

4. **New Shluchim Resources**: Each major program division has specific resources marked "For New Shluchim" to help those just starting their shlichus journey.

5. **Financial Support**: Keren Hashluchim provides various grants and loans for simchas, emergencies, building projects, and ongoing operations.

6. **Professional Development**: Training seminars, courses, farbrengens, and coaching available across multiple programs.

7. **Network Building**: Multiple initiatives facilitate connections between Shluchim for sharing best practices, support, and collaboration.

## Bottom Line for Shluchim

This catalog represents the infrastructure supporting global Chabad shlichus. From the moment a young couple considers going on shlichus through decades of building and expanding their community, Merkos 302 provides tools, funding, training, networking, and support. The breadth of offerings demonstrates that Shluchim are not alone in their mission - they have an entire organization backing them with resources specifically designed to help them succeed in preparing their communities to greet Moshiach.

Whether a Shliach needs curriculum for Hebrew school, grants for building projects, respite support for special needs children, training for hospital chaplaincy, or simply connection with fellow Shluchim, Merkos 302 has established a program to meet that need. The organization's commitment to responding to each year's Kinus hachlatos ensures that the support continues to evolve based on real needs in the field.
