- **1/4 teaspoon salt**

## Recipe 2: Vegetable Soup

### Ingredients

- **2 tablespoons oil**
- **1 onions, chopped**
- **1 small butternut squash,** peeled and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- **6 zucchinis**
- **6 yellow squash**
- **8 carrots**
- **4 sweet potatoes**
- **Coarse salt and ground pepper**

### Directions

1. **Sauté onions** until fragrant, about 2 minutes

2. **Add garlic,** let brown

3. **Add all vegetables** and almost cover with water

4. **Cook, stirring occasionally,** bring to a boil; reduce heat

5. **Simmer** until vegetables are tender

6. **Blend soup** until smooth

### Matzah Sorting ("Shlaimos etc.")

**What "Shlaimos" Means:**
- Whole, unbroken matzahs
- For special uses (Afikoman, etc.)
- Quality check

**Sorting Process:**
1. Open all matzah boxes
2. Separate broken from whole
3. Reserve best "shlaimos" for Seder plate
4. Portion 3/4 per person into ziplocs
5. Label bags if for specific tables
6. Keep extra for seconds

**Why Ziplocs:**
- Keeps fresh
- Easy distribution
- Pre-portioned
- Can label for tables

### Common Questions

**Q: "3/4 matzah per person" - what does this mean?**
A: Three-quarters of one machine matzah per person for entire Seder. May seem like not much, but accounts for other eating during meal.

**Q: "Check lettuce" - what am I checking for?**
A: Bugs! Romaine lettuce requires careful checking. Also checking for wilting, freshness, and that you have enough.

**Q: "Sort Matzos - shlaimos etc." - why?**
A: Need whole (shlaimos) matzahs for specific Seder uses. Broken ones fine for general eating.

**Q: Why "grind" maror?**
A: Makes it easier to eat and distribute. If using horseradish root, must grind. Romaine lettuce just needs to be cut/torn.

### Safety Considerations

**Sternos:**
- Never leave unattended
- Keep away from children
- Stable surface essential
- Have fire extinguisher nearby
- Know how to safely extinguish

**Hot Liquids:**
- Soup in chafing dish very hot
- Warn guests
- Serve carefully
- Keep children at safe distance

**Candles:**
- Stable holders
- Away from decorations
- Not near curtains/tablecloths
- Extinguish before leaving room

---

### Pre-Made vs. Fresh

**What Can Be Made Ahead:**
- Vegetable soup (2 days before)
- Salad dressing (1 week before)
- Charoses (2 days before)
- Boiled potatoes (day before)
- Salt water (day before)

**What Must Be Fresh:**
- Lettuce (check day-of)
- Eggs (hard boil day-of or night before)
- Zeroa (broil erev Pesach)

**Storage:**
- Soup: Refrigerate in covered container
- Dressing: Jar with tight lid in fridge
- Charoses: Covered container in fridge
- Potatoes: Refrigerate in water (prevents browning)
- Salt water: Covered container in fridge
