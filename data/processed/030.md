## Content Summary

Forum discussion on police escort challenges and solutions, including:

**Challenge:**
Police department declined to lead parade citing "mixing church and state" concerns

**Solutions Discussed:**

**1. Reframe the Request:**
- "You don't need them to lead, you need them to secure the parade"
- Protecting free speech, not engaging religiously

**2. Hire Off-Duty Officers:**
- $35-$100 per hour per officer
- Varies by location

**3. Apply for Parade Permit:**
- Official approval may include escort
- Pay required fee

**4. Conversation with Manager:**
- Point out other holiday parades
- Sit-down meeting
- Request equal treatment
