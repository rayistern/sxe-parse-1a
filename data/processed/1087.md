- Not complex partnership

**Concerns:**

**Complex Issue:**
- Requires individual psak
- Cannot give blanket answer
- Factors to consider:
  - Sale procedure used
  - Access to premises
  - Payment structure
  - Post-Pesach timing

**Proper Response:**
- "This requires specific guidance"
- "Please consult with the rabbi who handled sale"
- "Different factors affect the answer"
- Not simple yes/no
