# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Mechiras Chametz Email Template

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Community email template

**Purpose:** Promote mechiras chametz (selling chametz to non-Jew)

**Timing:** Less than a week before Passover

**Format:** Ready-to-send community communication

---

## Email Content

### Opening

**Greeting:**
> "BH
Passover is less than a week away!"

**Sets Urgency:**
- Timeline clear
- Action needed soon
- Timely reminder

---

### Educational Introduction

**Multiple Mitzvot:**
> "In addition to having a Seder, asking the Four Questions, and eating Matzah, a very important Mitzvah on Passover is the prohibition of possessing Chametz (leavened bread)."

**Key Teaching:**
- Not just eating chametz
- **Possessing** chametz also prohibited
- Separate mitzvah

---

### Critical Distinction

**Two Different Prohibitions:**
> "This should not be confused with the prohibition of eating Chametz. Each Mitzvah is independent of the other."

**Why Important:**
- People often confuse them
- Think if they don't eat it, enough
- Need to understand possession prohibition separately
- Both must be addressed

---

### The Solution

**Customary Practice:**
> "It is therefore customary to legally (and Halachiclly) sell all our Chametz to a non-Jew for the duration of Passover."

**Key Points:**
- Legally valid sale
- Halachically proper
- To non-Jew
- Duration of Passover only
- Solves possession problem

---

### Easy Process

**Simplicity Emphasized:**
> "We have made this easy for everyone. All you need to do is click here, complete the simple form, and we will 'take care' of your Chametz for you."

**Features:**
- Online form
- Simple to complete
- Rabbi handles details
- No complicated procedures

---

### The Pitch

**Three-Part Close:**

**1. Simplicity:**
> "It's as simple as that"

**2. Mitzvah Fulfillment:**
> "and you fulfill the Mitzvah of not possessing Chametz on Passover."

**3. Holiday Wishes:**
> "Best wishes for a happy and kosher Passover,"

---

## For Shluchim: Using This Template

### Timing Strategy

**Send This Email:**
- 5-7 days before Passover
- After most planning emails sent
- Before last-minute rush
- Urgent but not panicked

**Why This Timing:**
- Passover preparations in full swing
- People receptive to reminders
- Still have time to complete form
- Creates urgency without pressure

---

### Customization Options

**Add Your Details:**
- Rabbi's name at signature
- Specific link to your form
- Shul/organization name
- Contact information

**Optional Additions:**
- Deadline for submission
- Office hours for questions
- Phone number for help
- Link to FAQs

---

### Educational Value

**Keep It Simple:**
- This template doesn't overexplain
- Just core information needed
- Link to form is call to action
- More details available elsewhere

**What It Accomplishes:**
- Clarifies possession vs eating
- Shows independence of mitzvot
- Presents solution
- Makes participation easy

---

### Follow-Up Emails

**Day Before Deadline:**
- "Last chance to sell chametz"
- Emphasize urgency
- Same link
- Shorter message

**After Passover:**
- Chametz "bought back"
- Process complete
- Thank you for participation

---

### Common Questions to Anticipate

**After Sending This:**

**"What if I forgot?"**
- Have backup plan
- Can still participate until deadline
- Rabbi available for questions

**"Do I need to be there?"**
- No, form is sufficient
- Appointing rabbi as agent
- Legally valid

**"What about my business?"**
- Separate business chametz form
- More complex situations
- Personal consultation available

---

### Link Management

**The Form Link:**
- Should go to online form
- Mobile-friendly essential
- Clear instructions
- Confirmation page after submission

**Form Should Collect:**
- Name and address
- Types of chametz
- Location of chametz
- Contact information
- Signature/agreement

### Legal and Halachic Notes

**"Legally (and Halachiclly)"**
- Important qualification

- Quotes indicate agency
- Rabbi acts as agent
- Community member appoints rabbi
- Rabbi conducts actual sale
