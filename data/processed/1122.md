## For Shluchim: Pesach Masterchef Program

### Program Overview

**From These Materials:**

**Program Name:** Pesach Masterchef

**Target Audience:** Children/families

**Format:** Cooking competition or activity

**Materials Provided:**
- Marketing flyer
- Completion diplomas
- Branded water bottles
- Event backdrop
- Educational Kahoot game

---

### Implementation Guide

**Pre-Event:**

**Marketing:**
- Download and customize flyer from Canva link
- Add your Chabad House details
- Include date, time, location
- Registration information
- Print and distribute or share digitally

**Preparation:**
- Print diplomas (one per participant)
- Order water bottle labels (or print on sticker paper)
- Print backdrop (large format at copy shop like FedEx)
- Test Kahoot link and familiarize with questions

---

**During Event:**

**Setup:**
- Display backdrop prominently
- Arrange cooking stations
- Distribute branded water bottles
- Have diplomas ready

**Activities:**
- Cooking competition/demonstration
- Passover learning (via Kahoot)
- Photo opportunities with backdrop
- Diploma ceremony at end

---

**Post-Event:**
- Photos with backdrop for social media
- Follow-up with participants
- Collect feedback for next year

---

### Customization Options

**Canva Links Are Shareable:**
- These links allow viewing
- To edit: May need Canva account
- Can duplicate and customize
- Maintain branding consistency

**What to Customize:**
- Your Chabad House name and logo
- Event date and time
- Location details
- Contact information
- Any specific program details

**Keep Standard:**
- "Pesach Masterchef" branding
- Overall design aesthetic
- Educational content (Kahoot)

---

### Program Variations

**Competition Format:**
- Teams compete to make best dish
- Judging by panel or attendees
- Categories: Presentation, taste, creativity
- Awards for different categories

**Workshop Format:**
- Demonstration by chef/Shlucha
- Hands-on participation
- Everyone makes same recipe
- Educational component about Passover

**Family Format:**
- Parent-child teams
- Age-appropriate tasks
- Focus on fun and learning
- Everyone wins diploma

---

### Integration with Passover Programming

**Timing Options:**

**Pre-Pesach:**
- Week before Passover
- Learn about Passover foods
- Practice recipes for Seder
- Build excitement

**During Pesach:**
- Chol Hamoed activity
- Day program for families
- Camp activity
- Community event

**Educational Integration:**
- Kahoot teaches Passover facts
- While cooking, discuss symbolism
- Why matzah? Why maror?
- Connect food to freedom story

---

### Recipe Selection

**For Children's Program:**
- Simple, age-appropriate recipes
- Passover-friendly (obviously)
- Limited sharp knives
- Mostly no-cook or simple baking
- Visible results quickly

**Popular Options:**
- Charoset variations
- Matzah pizza
- Fruit salads
- Simple desserts (see File 1128 for recipes)
- Decorated matzah

**Safety:**
- Adult supervision for all stations
- Pre-chop ingredients if needed
- Electric mixers supervised
- Oven use by adults only

---

### Budget Considerations

**Design Materials:**
- Canva designs: Free (already created)
- Printing flyer: Minimal cost
- Printing diplomas: $1-2 each
- Water bottle labels: $0.50-1 each
- Backdrop: $50-150 (reusable)

**Event Costs:**
- Ingredients for cooking
- Disposable supplies
- Prizes (optional)
- Marketing
- Venue (if off-site)

**Revenue:**
- Registration fees (optional)
- Sponsor the event
- Free to community

---

### Marketing Strategy

**Using the Flyer:**

**Digital:**
- Social media posts
- Email blast
- WhatsApp groups
- Website/blog
- Online registration link

**Physical:**
- Post in Jewish businesses
- Hand out at Hebrew school
- Include in Purim packages
- Mail to families with children

**Messaging:**
- Fun, hands-on activity
- Learn while cooking
- Passover preparation
- Family bonding
- Certificate of completion

---

### Diploma Ceremony

**End Event on High Note:**

**Presentation:**
- Call each child by name
- Brief comment about their work
- Hand diploma with handshake
- Photo opportunity
- Applause from group

**What Diplomas Can Say:**
- "Certified Pesach Masterchef"
- Child's name
- Date of completion
- Signature line (Rabbi/Rebbetzin)
- Official-looking design

**Added Value:**
- Children feel accomplished
- Parents appreciate recognition
- Nice memento to keep
- Incentive for participation

---

### Kahoot Game Tips

**If Unfamiliar with Kahoot:**
- Free online quiz platform
- Students answer on their devices
- Questions appear on main screen
- Competitive and engaging
- Tracks scores automatically

**Using Pesach Kahoot:**
- Access link before event
- Review questions
- Ensure WiFi adequate
- Test on your devices
- Prepare prizes for winners

**When to Use:**
- Opening icebreaker
- Between cooking activities
- While waiting for baking/cooking
- Educational break
- Closing activity

**Age Considerations:**
- Very young: May need parent help
- Elementary: Perfect age
- Teens: May need more challenging version
- Mixed ages: Team format

---

### Photo Opportunities

**Using the Backdrop:**

**Setup:**
- Well-lit area
- Stable mounting
- Clear space in front
- Props available (chef hats, utensils)

**Photo Types:**
- Individual with diploma
- Group shot of all participants
- Action shots while cooking
- Display of finished dishes
- Candid moments

**Sharing:**
- Social media (with permission)
- Email to parents
- Community newsletter
- Website gallery
- Next year's marketing
