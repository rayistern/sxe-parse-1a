## Women's Obligation in Chanukah

### 1. The Talmudic Source

**Talmud Shabbat 23a:**
> "Rabbi Yehoshua Ben Levi said; Women are obligated in the kindling of the Chanukah light, for they, too, were involved in that miracle."

---

### 2. The Exceptional Nature

**Tosfot to Pesachim 29a:**
> "Even though this is a positive commandment that is bound by time and women are generally exempt from performing such Mitzvot-even those that are of Rabbinic origin."

**Significance:**
This obligation is unusual - women are typically exempt from time-bound mitzvot, yet here they are obligated.

---

## Why Are Women Obligated?

### Reason #1: Women Were Specifically Targeted

**The Greek Decrees:**
- Many decrees against all Jews:
  - No Kosher
  - No Shabbos
  - No Circumcision
- **But even more specifically targeted Jewish women**

**The Jus Primae Noctis Decree:**
> "The Greeks decreed that every Jewish bride would be violated on her wedding night by first submitting to the local Greek commander."

**The Salvation:**
> "So when the salvation and the miracles of Chanukah finally came about, it was a salvation for all Jews but the women in particular experienced a very personal freedom from being assaulted by the Greeks."

---

### Reason #2: The Miracle Happened Through a Woman

**Yehudis's Story:**

**Who She Was:**
- Daughter of Yochanan the Kohain Gadol (High Priest)
- Extremely beautiful

**What She Did:**
1. Greek general greatly desired her
2. She pretended to give herself to him
3. Fed him cheese dishes until he became very thirsty
4. Gave him wine to quench his thirst
5. He drank until drunk and fell asleep
6. She beheaded him
7. Brought his head to Jerusalem
8. Enemy commanders saw what happened, got scared, and fled

**The Impact:**
> "This victory was the turning point for the Jews in the battle against the Greek army."

**Side Note:**
> "(Side note: This is why many have the custom of eating dairy foods on Chanukah.)"

---

### Reason #3: Another Heroine - Matityahu's Daughter

**The Story:**

**The Wedding:**
At her own wedding, she ripped off her gown.

**Brothers' Reaction:**
- Macabees completely embarrassed
- Became outraged
- Wanted to kill her for immodesty

**Her Challenge:**
> "She then reprimanded them by asking them why they were so zealous that they would kill her for violating her modesty, yet they weren't so zealous enough to protect her from the hands of the governor who would come to assault and violate her that same night."

**The Result:**
> "Her words were what aroused the 5 brothers to go to war and to battle the Greeks."

**Source:** Midrash Maaseh Chanukah, Otzar Meforshim Vol.1

---

### Additional Heroism: Women During Circumcisions

**The Context:**
Circumcision was outlawed under penalty of death. Many men fled into hiding.

**What the Women Did:**
> "If a child was born to the wife of one of those who had fled into hiding, the mother would circumcise the child on the 8th day. She would go upon the wall of Jerusalem carrying her child. She would hurl herself and her child from the wall-thereby saying to her husband, and all her brothers who had gone into hiding to escape the war:"

**Her Message:**
> "If you will not go out to wage war against your enemies, you will have neither children nor wives, and your end will be total annihilation. We will observe what is holy to us, not in hiding, but publicly, before the eyes of all. If you intend to save us, emerge from your caves, and wage war against the enemy till you destroy him. G-d will be with you!"

**The Response:**
> "Thereupon Matityahu and his 5 sons arose like lions."

## Women's Deeper Connection to Chanukah

### The Summary

> "Women thus have a stronger connection to Chanukah than the men since a) women were singled out in the Greek prosecution and b) since the miracle happened through her."

---

### The Personal Nature of the Victory

**The Attack:**
> "Through the violation of Jewish women, the Greeks were personally attacking the Jewish woman's purity and integrity."

**The Victory:**
> "Furthermore, the salvation came about through a woman and its victory is all the more meaningful for her."

---

## Halachic Expression of Women's Connection

### Women's Custom Regarding Work

**General Rule:**
> "Work is permitted on Chanukah."

**Women's Exception:**
> "Women, however, follow the custom of refraining from work during the time the Chanukah candles are burning at home. (They should continue this custom) without any leniency."

**Source:** Code of Jewish Law

---

### Why Women Are More Stringent

**The Explanation:**
> "Women's refraining from work reflects their ability to absorb the spiritual light and holiness of the holiday, thus causing them to react in a similar manner as Shabbat and Yom Tov during which work is forbidden due to the immense degree of spirituality."

**The Implication:**
Women's souls are so attuned to the spiritual light of Chanukah that they naturally respond as if it were Shabbat or Yom Tov.

## Life's Lessons

### The Pattern of Jewish History

**The Principle:**
> "Women are the heroines of each of the major holidays. They were the ones to take action and bring about salvation for the entire Jewish People."

**Examples:**
- **Pesach:** Miriam, Yocheved, Serach bat Asher, midwives
- **Purim:** Esther
- **Chanukah:** Yehudis, Matityahu's daughter, circumcising mothers

---

### Our Responsibility

**The Challenge:**
> "We must follow their lead and do the same!"

**What This Means:**
1. Take initiative (don't wait for others)
2. Act courageously (even when it's risky)
3. Prioritize holiness (over comfort or safety)
4. Challenge men to rise to occasion (when necessary)
5. Bring salvation (be the change-maker)
