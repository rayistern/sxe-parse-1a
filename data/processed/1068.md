# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] "Haman's Gallows" - Ki Sisa Sermon

## Note: Purim Content (Not Chanukah)

**Title:** Ki Sisa Sermon: "Haman's Gallows"

**Parshat Connection:** Ki Sisa (contains account of Golden Calf and Abraham's tree)

**Theme:** Mystical significance of fifty cubits and Abraham's tree

---

## The Story: Haman's Growing Hatred

### Initial Plan

**Original Intention:**
- Have Mordechai killed with rest of Jewish people
- 11 months later
- After his plan executed

---

### Venom Grows

**Haman's Complaint to Zeresh:**
> "And Haman said, 'Esther did not even bring [anyone] to the party that she made, except me, and tomorrow, too, I am invited to her with the king. But all this is worth nothing to me, every time I see Mordechai the Jew sitting in the king's gate.'"

**Analysis:**
- Honored by queen exclusively
- Invited again tomorrow
- Yet all feels worthless
- Because sees Mordechai not bowing
- One thorn spoils entire bouquet

---

### Zeresh's Advice

**The Proposal:**
> "Let them make a gallows fifty cubits high, and in the morning say to the king that they should hang Mordechai on it, and go to the king to the banquet joyfully."

**Key Details:**
- Specific height: fifty cubits
- Act in morning (urgent)
- Then go joyfully to banquet
- Remove irritant immediately

---

## G-d's Response

### The Divine Reaction

**G-d's Statement:**
> "Said the Holy One, blessed be he, 'the tree [wood; gallows] shall not be, for Abraham already was first [with the tree] and said [to his guest, the three angels] "and you shall rest under the tree."'"

**Question:**
- "Under the tree?"
- Which tree?
- What's the reference?

---

## History of Abraham's Tree

### Abraham's Original Use

**At Beer Sheba:**
- Abraham brought tree with him
- Built guest house from it
- Called it "Eshel"

---

### Meaning of "Eshel"

**Acronym in Hebrew:**
- **Aleph:** Achila [a place to eat]
- **Shin:** Shtiya [a place to drink]
- **Lamed:** Linah [a place to sleep]

**Purpose:**
- Guest hospitality house
- Food, drink, lodging
- Service to travelers
- Acts of kindness

---

### Journey Through Generations

**Abraham to Jacob:**
- Abraham's grandson Jacob
- Brought tree to Egypt with him
- Told Jewish people
- Should bring tree when leave Egypt

**From Egypt to Wilderness:**
- Jews brought tree out of Egypt
- From this tree of Abraham
- Walls of Holy Temple built
- The Tabernacle in desert
- Mishkan structure

**Spiritual Continuity:**
- Abraham's kindness tree
- Becomes walls of G-d's dwelling
- Physical structure with spiritual heritage
- Hospitality becomes holiness

---

## G-d's Scoffing at Haman

### The Divine Argument

**G-d's Point:**
> "Thus, G-d scoffs at Haman, that long before Haman came to create a gallows with mystical powers, in order to be able to hang Mordechai, Abraham already utilized this mystical tree to serve G-d, with kindness to people, and from that Abraham's descendants built a House for G-d."

**Priority:**
- Abraham got there first
- Used tree for good
- Established pattern
- Kindness and holiness
- Haman too late

---

## Mystical Powers of Fifty Cubits

### The Question

**Why Fifty Cubits?**
- About seventy-five feet high!
- Absurdly tall for gallows
- Why does Zeresh advise this?
- Why does Haman listen?
- Why specifically fifty cubits?

---

### The Mystical Number Fifty

**Counting of Omer:**
- Jews commanded to prepare
- From when left Egypt
- For forty-nine days
- After which, on fiftieth day
- G-d revealed Himself
- Gave Ten Commandments
- Gave holy Torah

---

### Forty-Nine vs Fifty

**Forty-Nine:**
- 7 x 7
- Full measure of Finite Linear Light
- Manifesting through nature
- Of human psyche
- Clear description of right and wrong
- Good and evil defined

**On This Level:**
- Haman knows cannot obtain Heavenly permission
- For wicked to conquer righteous
- Clear moral categories
- Justice prevails

---

### The Fiftieth Gateway

**Transcendence:**
- Infinite Circular Light
- "G-d is everything and everything is G-d"
- All are equal
- No distinctions

**Haman's Strategy:**
> "Here, Haman provokes an Essence Light, beyond light and darkness, good and evil, and hopes to be able to conquer Mordechai."

**Haman's Hope:**
- Reach level beyond morality
- Where distinctions don't matter
- Where wicked equal to righteous
- Exploit divine transcendence
- Against divine justice

---

## Abraham's Preemptive Action

### G-d's Counter to Haman

**The Message:**
> "Thus, G-d tells Haman, that Haman is too late! For Abraham already evoked the magical Essence Light, and made sure to draw this light as well, into only Light and Goodness, cutting it off from Darkness and Evil."

**What Abraham Did:**
- Reached Fiftieth Gateway first
- Evoked Essence Light
- But channeled it exclusively
- Into Light and Goodness
- Cut off from Darkness and Evil
- Established precedent

**Implication:**
- Even transcendent level
- Serves only good
- Not available for evil
- Abraham's merit protects
- Haman cannot exploit it

---

## The Deepest Level

### Purim's Ultimate Lesson

**Summary:**
> "This is the story of Purim on its deepest level."

**The Challenge:**
> "We must reach into our depths, and even there where nothing matters anymore, we must make everything matter, with a commitment and conviction to Goodness, Kindness, and Light!"

---

### Understanding the Message

**Two Levels of Service:**

**Level of Forty-Nine:**
- Clear right and wrong
- Defined morality
- Obvious choices
- Natural service

**Level of Fifty:**
- Beyond definitions
- Transcendent realm
- Where distinctions blur
- "Nothing matters"

**Our Task:**
- Even at level where "nothing matters"
- Make everything matter
- Commitment remains
- Conviction to goodness
- Even in transcendence
- Choose light over darkness
