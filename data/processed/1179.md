# Forum: Using Regular Electric Oven on Broil for Model Matzah Bakery

**Source**: http://www.shluchimexchange.com/en/forum/viewtopic.php?t=443204
**Topic**: Can regular electric oven on broil be used instead of pizza oven?
**Date**: April 2014

## Question
"Has anyone ever used a regular electric oven on broil to bake the matzohs? Will it pass? I just decided to do a model bakery last minute and would like to see if I can get away without getting a pizza oven."

## Responses

**Response 1 (Yahoo Mail on Android)**:
"I have done many matzah bakeries where the oven did not have time to reach full heat and it worked fine."

**Response 2 (Nosson Potash, S. Francisco, CA - April 1, 2014)**:
"I have done it and it works. Takes longer to bake so make sure the matzah is very thin."

**Response 3 (Rabbi Mendel Hendel, Chabad of Greece)**:
"We did it and it works. Put it on maximum heat, and also have some Matza prepared in advance because it takes longer to bake. Hatzlocho"

## Bottom Line
Consensus: Regular electric oven on broil works for model matzah bakery though takes longer than pizza oven, requiring very thin matzah and preparing batches in advance to manage longer baking time, useful for last-minute programs without pizza oven access.
