### Chanukah Explanation

```
Chanukah is the eight-day Jewish "Festival of Lights," this year beginning on the evening of November 29th. Each evening at sunset an additional candle is lit to commemorate the rededication of the Temple in Jerusalem some 2000 years ago. The holiday also celebrates the miracle of one day's worth of pure ritual oil burning for eight days until a new supply could be obtained.
```

**What This Paragraph Does:**

**Basic Facts:**
- Eight-day holiday
- "Festival of Lights" (poetic name)
- When it starts this year
- Nightly candle lighting
- Historical background (Temple, Jerusalem, 2000 years)
- The oil miracle

**Tone:**
- Educational
- Assumes no knowledge
- Brief but complete
- Respectful to tradition
- Accessible language

**Why Include:**
- General newspaper audience
- Many don't know basics
- Provides context
- Makes it interesting
- Shows substance behind celebration
