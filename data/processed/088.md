## How the Program Works

### Official Program Description

**From Walmart Website:**
(http://walmartstores.com/GlobalWMStoresWeb/navigate.do?catg=759)

---

### Community Grant Program Overview

> "The Community Grant Program is the largest program funded by the Wal-Mart Foundation and includes both matching and direct grants."

**Two Types of Grants:**
1. **Matching Grants** (fundraiser-based)
2. **Direct Grants** (no fundraiser needed)

---

### Matching Grant Program

**How It Works:**

**Option A: On-Premises Fundraiser**
- Local nonprofit holds fundraiser outside Walmart or Sam's Club
- Walmart/Sam's Club can match portion of funds raised
- **Up to $1,000 match**

**Option B: Off-Premises with Associate Involvement**
- Event held off Walmart/Sam's Club premises
- BUT: Walmart or Sam's Club associate actively involved
- Also eligible for funding
- Still up to $1,000

---

### Direct Grants

**After Meeting Criteria:**
- Once store/club meets certain criteria in Matching Grant Program each year
- Second source of funding unlocked: Direct Grants
- **Do not require fundraiser**
- Funds awarded directly to deserving organization
- No event needed

**This is likely what forum posters received**

---

## Eligible Organizations

### Who Qualifies

**501(c)(3) Non-Profit Organizations:**
- Standard non-profits
- With IRS tax-exempt status

**OR Organizations Exempt from Needing 501(c)(3):**
- **Public schools**
- **Faith-based institutions** (churches, synagogues, etc.)
  - Must be conducting project that benefits community at large
  - Not just internal congregation activities
- **Government agencies**

---

### Faith-Based Institution Requirement

**Key Phrase:**
> "Faith-based institutions such as churches (must be conducting a project that benefits the community at large)"

**What This Means:**
- Chabad qualifies as faith-based institution
- Project must benefit broader community
- Not just for members/attendees
- Public menorah lighting = community benefit
- Chanukah celebration = open to all
- Educational programs = community service

---

## How to Apply

### Simple Process

**Step 1: Go to Local Walmart**
- Visit your nearest Walmart store
- Or Sam's Club if you prefer

**Step 2: Ask for Form**
> "Asked for a community grant application"

- Ask at customer service
- Or ask for Community Involvement Coordinator
- They'll provide the form

**Step 3: Fill Out Application**
> "Filled it out by hand inside the store"

- Can fill out right there
- Simple form
- Hand-written fine
- Don't need fancy proposal

**Step 4: Submit**
- Give to store staff
- They process internally

**Step 5: Wait**
> "A few weeks later they called"

- Takes a few weeks
- They'll call if approved
- Pick up check at store
