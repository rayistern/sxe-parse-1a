## Planning Your Own Jewish Heritage Night

### NFL Chanukah Collaboration

**What to Request from Shluchim Who Have Done This:**

If you've done any collaboration with an NFL team for Chanukah and/or Jewish themed events, please share:
- Details of the collaboration
- Proposal that outlines the logistics
- Request framework
- Contacts and relationships built

**Applicable to Other Sports:**
Experience with other pro sports (NBA, NHL, MLB, MLS) is also helpful.

---

### Key Success Factors

**1. Venue Relationship Building:**
- Start early (6-12 months in advance)
- Build relationship with group sales manager
- Identify Jewish staff members who can champion the event
- Present professional proposal

**2. Community Engagement:**
- Partner with other Jewish organizations
- Create buzz on social media
- Offer group ticket packages
- Leverage CTeen and youth programs

**3. Event Elements:**
- High-visibility Menorah lighting (halftime or between quarters)
- Pre-game or post-game party
- Branded giveaways (T-shirts with Hebrew)
- Photo opportunities
- Meet and greet with Jewish athletes (if available)

**4. Financial Model:**
- Negotiate ticket minimums
- Explore sponsorship opportunities
- CTeen discount codes to drive youth attendance
- Group sales packages

**5. Marketing:**
- Professional press releases
- Social media campaign
- Video highlights from previous years
- Partner with venue's marketing team
- Community calendars and Jewish media

## Benefits of Jewish Heritage Night

### For the Community

**Accessibility:**
- Reaches unaffiliated Jews in comfortable setting
- Neutral venue (not religious institution)
- Fun, family-friendly environment
- Draws Jews from wide geographic area

**Pirsumei Nisa (Publicizing the Miracle):**
- Menorah lit before thousands (sometimes 20,000+)
- Maximum publicity of Chanukah message
- Media coverage
- Social media reach

**Community Pride:**
- Jewish presence in mainstream culture
- Celebration of Jewish identity
- Brings community together

### For Your Chabad House

**Outreach:**
- Contact information from thousands of attendees
- Follow-up opportunities
- Database building
- Program awareness

**Visibility:**
- Branding on T-shirts, signage
- Logo recognition
- Media mentions
- Community awareness

**Engagement:**
- CTeen recruitment
- Hebrew school interest
- Holiday program participation
- Relationship building

## Final Thoughts

Jewish Heritage Night at professional sports venues represents one of the most powerful platforms for Chanukah outreach, combining:
- Mass attendance (thousands)
- Neutral, accessible venue
- Media coverage potential
- Community building
- Maximum Pirsumei Nisa

With proper planning, relationship building, and community engagement, this event can become an annual highlight of your Chanukah programming.
