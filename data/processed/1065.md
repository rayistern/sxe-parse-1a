## Sermon 1: Hadassah, She is Esther

### Biblical Source

**Verse:**
> "And he [Mordechai] brought up Hadassah, that is Esther." -Esther 2:7

**Date of Rebbe's Maamar:**
- Purim 1953 (5713)
- First delivered this teaching
- Explored two dimensions

---

### Talmudic Question

**Which Was Her Real Name?**
- Hadassah or Esther?
- Which was nickname?
- Talmud explores this

**Spiritual Significance:**
- Both quoted in Book of Esther
- Both exist in spiritual dimension
- Both within soul of every Jew
- Not just historical names
- Archetypal qualities

---

### Kabbalistic Framework: Ten Emanations

**World of Atzilut:**
- Ten Emanations (Sefirot)
- Made up of Light and Vessels
- Both absolutely transparent
- Unified with G-d
- No separation

**Tenth Emanation - Kingship (Malchut):**
- Has two dimensions
- Unlike other nine emanations

---

### Two Dimensions of Kingship

**Haddasah (Myrtle; Righteous):**
- As it is within its world of Atzilut
- Higher world
- Revealed G-dliness
- Times of Divine Revelation

**Esther (Hidden):**
- As it descends
- Hidden within lower world of Beriah (Creation; Ego)
- Concealment of G-d's presence
- Times of Exile

---

### Application to Soul

**Two Names for Jewish People:**

**Children of Israel:**
- Like Hadassah
- Soul in revelation
- Intellectual and emotional revelation
- Living within perception of G-d
- Living within love for G-d

**Word Analysis:**
- Yisroel (Hebrew for Israel)
- Letters form: "Lee Rosh"
- Means: "Unto Me a head"
- Head = higher level

**Children of Jacob:**
- Like Esther
- Soul in exile and concealment
- State of darkness

**Word Analysis:**
- Yaakov (Hebrew for Jacob)
- Named after holding Esau's heel at birth
- Akeiv = heel
- Heel = lower level

---

### Two Types of Exile/Concealment

**1. World's State:**
- Darkness and exile at large
- External circumstances
- Historical conditions

**2. Personal Exile:**
- "When we draw our power of desire and love into the indulgence of self-pleasuring egocentric desire"
- We deny soul its expression
- Love for G-d concealed
- Internal self-imposed exile

---

### Central Message of Verse

> "The verse in the Book of Esther is telling us that even the dimension of the soul that is in a state of Esther exile is still connected and contains within it all the divinity of the Hadassah dimension of the soul."

**Even More:**
> "When the Haddasdah dimension of the soul is extinguished, it is the Esther dimension of the soul that resurrects it!"

---

### Haddasah Dimension of Soul

**Characteristics:**
- All levels of soul's love
- Depends on being fueled by:
  - Soul's understanding
  - Concentrating on G-d
  - Meditating upon G-d's relationship with us
  - Meditating upon G-d's greatness

**In Exile:**
- G-d is concealed
- Higher sensitivities drowned
- Indulgence in lower egocentric pleasures
- Hadassah experience extinguished

---

### Esther Dimension of Soul - The Potency

**Remains Unaffected by Exile:**
- Potency of the Soul
- Power of self-sacrifice
- NOT based on perception
- NOT based on feelings
- Outcome of soul's nucleus being "Spark of Creator!"

**Greater Than Hadassah:**
- Esther (Hidden) Potency > Hadassah (Revealed) Love
- In essence, deeper level
- More fundamental

**In Darkest Times:**
- When Haddasah Love extinguished
- Esther (Hidden) Potency resurrects her
- Concealment produces revelation
- Darkness births light

---

### Activating the Potency

**The Seed:**
> "The seed that draws forth the Esther (Hidden) Potency of the soul are the words of Torah-study."

**Torah's Power:**
- Empowers Potency of Soul
- Reveals potency into action
- Transforms latent into active
- Brings forth essence

## Sermon 2: To Remember or To Not Know?!

### The Contradiction

**Shabbat Zachor:**
- Shabbat before Purim
- Called "Zachor" - Remember
- Read commandment to remember Amalek
- What Amalek did leaving Egypt
- First nation to attack Jews in desert
- Clear memory and identification

**Haman Connection:**
- Haman called "Haman H'Agogi"
- Agog = king of Amalek in times of Saul
- Agog impregnated woman night before death
- Haman = offspring of Agog
- Therefore: Amalekite

**Purpose:**
- Shabbat Zachor: Remember wickedness
- Remember commandment to eradicate
- Clear identification
- Intellectual understanding

**Then Comes Purim:**
- Completely different approach
- New directive

---

### Purim's Law

**Must Drink:**
> "Until he becomes too intoxicated to know the difference between, 'Blessed be Mordechai and Cursed be Haman!'"

**Apparent Contradiction:**
- Zachor: Clearly remember who Haman (Amalek) is
- Purim: Too intoxicated to know difference
- Which is it?
- Remember or forget?

---

### The Key: "Ad D'loh Yodah"

**Wording Analysis:**
- Does NOT use word "Shikor" (drunk)
- States: "Ad d'loh yodah"
- Means: "Until [he] doesn't know"
- Focus on "know" vs "not know"

---

### Parable: The Sinking Ship

**Scenario 1 - Forgot Friend:**
- Man on sinking ship
- Panic
- Forgot about friend
- Jumped ship to save himself

**Decent World's Reaction:**
- Eventually understand
- Would forgive
- Panic overrides logic

**Scenario 2 - Forgot Children:**
- Same man
- Same panic
- Forgot about children
- Jumped ship to save himself

**Decent World's Reaction:**
- Very difficult to understand
- Very difficult to forgive
- Never acceptable

**Why the Difference?**
- Panic overrides logic
- But panic does NOT override what lies deeper than logic
- In depths of heart
- Core essence
- No justification for forgetting children
- No matter how powerful the panic

---

### Two Levels of Remembrance

**Shabbat Zachor - "Know" Level:**
- Sitting in synagogue holiness
- Intellectually reading
- Understanding
- Absorbing who Amalek is
- What he did to us
- Logical relationship
- Between us and Amalekian War

**Problem:**
- Logic-based remembrance
- Won't necessarily withstand panic
- Won't withstand sinking ship
- Intellectual understanding insufficient

**Purim - "Not Know" Level:**
> "The deeper dimension of the words, 'Ad d'loh yodah -Until [he] doesn't know.' is to take the depths of, 'Blessed be Mordechai and Cursed be Haman!' beyond the 'Know' (logic) into the deeper essence of the heart and soul, transcending into the 'Not Know!'"

---

### Deeper Than Logic

**Some Concepts Require More:**
- Logical relationship not enough
- Must touch essence of soul
- Beyond intellectual understanding
- Core identity level

**Amalek:**
> "A people who stand up against Israel, bent on annihilating our people, G-d forbid, must touch the very essence of our soul!"

**In Deepest Intoxication:**
- Way beyond "Know"
- Into depths of soul's "Not Know"
- Jew must cry out:
- "Blessed be Mordechai and Cursed be Haman!"

---

### Contemporary Application

**Dangerous Approach:**
> "To negotiate within our minds with resolutions of the United Nation, Foreign Policy, and World Opinion about an outright Haman sworn to destroy our land and wipeout our people, is to not have celebrated Purim!"

**What It Means:**
- Not truly "Zachor" - Remembered
- Not remembered history
- Not remembered people's enemies
- Intellectual negotiation insufficient

---

### The Cry from Soul's Essence

**Must Passionately:**
- Remember
- Cry out
- To point of intoxication
- From depths of soul

**The Declaration:**
> "Blessed be Mordechai! Blessed be our people! Blessed be our Holy Land! Cursed be Haman! Cursed be those who want nothing but to destroy our People and destroy our Holy Land! Blessed be Mordechai and Cursed be Haman!"

**Not Intellectual:**
- Not debate
- Not negotiation
- Not analysis
- Soul-level certainty

---

### Conclusion

**Message:**
- Two levels of knowing
- Intellectual (Zachor)
- Essential ("Not Know" - beyond intellect)
- Both necessary
- But essence deeper
- Must reach soul's core
- Especially regarding enemies of Jewish people

**Closing:**
> "Have a happy Purim!"
