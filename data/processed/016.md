# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > Chanukah Events > Recipes] Trader Joe's Latkes

## Sample Request Letter to Trader Joe's

**Date:** November 22, 2022

**To whom it may concern,**

In honor of the holiday of Chanukah - the Jewish festival of lights, [REDACTED-ORGANIZATION] is having a community-wide celebration. We would like to ask you to donate the latkes for this event.

We are requesting **200 potato pancakes** and jars of apple sauce.

[REDACTED-ORGANIZATION] is an educational outreach organization, providing an array of programs, classes and events for adults and children. With this in mind, we are hosting the above-mentioned Chanukah event to help educate and spread a message of hope and togetherness in our communities.

Our 501(c)(3) Tax ID # is [REDACTED-TAX-ID]

Thank you for your generous sponsorship of educational community holiday events!

Sincerely,

Rabbi [REDACTED-NAME]

## How to Use This Template

### Customize for Your Organization

**Replace the following:**
- Organization name
- Your 501(c)(3) Tax ID number
- Quantity of latkes needed (based on your expected attendance)
- Rabbi/Director signature
- Date

---

### Approach Strategy

**Step 1: Identify Your Contact**
- Visit your local Trader Joe's
- Ask to speak with the store manager
- Best times: Mid-morning or early afternoon (avoid peak hours)

**Step 2: Make Initial Contact**
- Introduce yourself and your organization
- Explain the Chanukah celebration
- Express interest in partnership

**Step 3: Submit Written Request**
- Follow up conversation with this letter
- Bring 501(c)(3) documentation
- Provide event details
- Specify donation needs

**Step 4: Follow Up**
- Give manager time to review (1 week)
- Call or visit to check status
- Be flexible on quantities
- Express gratitude

---

### Tips for Success

**Build Relationships:**
- Start conversations early (1-2 months before Chanukah)
- Visit the store regularly throughout the year
- Thank them publicly (social media, event signage)
- Invite manager to the event

**Be Professional:**
- Typed, formal letter on letterhead (if available)
- Include all contact information
- Bring proper documentation
- Follow through on commitments

**Be Flexible:**
- Accept what they can offer
- Be understanding if they can't fulfill full request
- Consider multiple stores if you have large needs
- Express appreciation regardless of amount

---

### What to Expect

**Typical Response:**

From experienced Shluchim:
> "Walk in and ask to speak to the manager. If it's a busy store they should give you at least two boxes and sometimes more. Just bring your 501(c)(3)."

**Usual Donations:**
- 2+ boxes of frozen latkes
- May include applesauce
- Varies by store size and budget
- Manager has discretion

**Timeline:**
- Response within 1-2 weeks
- Pickup usually scheduled few days before event
- Some stores may require advance notice

---

### Alternative Approaches

**If Written Request Doesn't Work:**

**In-Person Request:**
- Some managers prefer face-to-face conversation
- Bring event flyer and 501(c)(3) documentation
- Explain community impact

**Partnership Proposal:**
- Offer to mention Trader Joe's sponsorship
- Social media recognition
- Banner at event: "Latkes generously donated by Trader Joe's"
- Photo opportunities

**Multiple Stores:**
- If you have several Trader Joe's locations nearby
- Each store has its own budget
- Can approach multiple locations
- Combine donations

---

### Quantity Guidelines

**How Many Latkes to Request:**

**For 50 people:**
- Request 100-125 latkes
- 2 per person, plus extras

**For 100 people:**
- Request 200-250 latkes
- Account for seconds

**For 200+ people:**
- Request 400-500 latkes
- Consider multiple stores
- Start planning 2+ months in advance

**General Rule:**
Request 2 latkes per person plus 20% buffer

---

### Don't Forget Applesauce!

**Applesauce Quantities:**

**Small Event (50 people):**
- 3-4 large jars

**Medium Event (100 people):**
- 6-8 large jars

**Large Event (200+ people):**
- 12-16 large jars

**Tip:** Request applesauce in your letter, or purchase separately to ensure adequate supply.

---

### After the Event

**Say Thank You:**

**Follow-Up Letter:**
Send thank you letter with:
- Photos from the event
- Number of attendees
- Impact on community
- Invitation to next year's event

**Social Media:**
- Post photos
- Tag Trader Joe's
- Public recognition
- Community appreciation

**In Person:**
- Visit store after event
- Thank manager personally
- Bring small gift or card
- Maintain relationship

---

### Building Long-Term Partnership

**Annual Tradition:**

**Year 1:**
- Establish relationship
- Prove you're reliable
- Show community impact

**Year 2 and Beyond:**
- Earlier contact
- Easier approval process
- May increase donation amount
- Manager becomes advocate

**Best Practices:**
- Same contact person each year
- Consistent event timing
- Reliable pickup
- Always express gratitude
- Keep them informed of impact

## Additional Considerations

### 501(c)(3) Documentation

**What to Bring:**
- Copy of IRS determination letter
- Your organization's EIN
- Business card
- Event flyer

**If You Don't Have:**
- Work under parent Chabad organization
- Use their 501(c)(3)
- Get proper authorization

---

### Event Details to Share

**Include Information About:**
- Expected attendance
- Date, time, location
- Community served
- Educational component
- Previous events (if applicable)

**Why This Matters:**
Trader Joe's wants to support meaningful community events. Show them the impact.

---

## Final Thoughts

Trader Joe's has been generous to many Chabad houses for Chanukah events. The key to success is:

1. **Professional presentation** - Formal letter, proper documentation
2. **Early timing** - Give them notice to budget
3. **Clear request** - Specific quantities
4. **Community impact** - Show how this helps people
5. **Gratitude** - Always thank them publicly and privately

This partnership can become an annual tradition that saves your budget while building community relationships.
