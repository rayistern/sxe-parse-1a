# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Passover 5775 - Announcements for Table Leaders

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Script for community Seder table leaders

**Year:** Passover 5775

**Purpose:** Sequential instructions for guiding Seder participants through all steps

**Format:** A through J announcements with specific directions

**Audience:** Table leaders at community Seder

---

## A - First Cup and Kiddush

> "We are now going to fill our cups with either wine or grape juice. This will be the first of the four cups tonight."

> "We will turn to page 21, and the rabbi will lead the kiddush momentarily. After the 'shehechiyanu' (which is at the end of the kiddush), we will sit down and drink down the whole cup, or at least most it (not sipping though)."

> "Don't forget, that when we are drinking the cup down, we recline on our left side."

**Key Points:**
- First of four cups
- Page 21
- Whole cup or most of it
- Recline on left side

---

## B - First Hand Washing (Urchatz)

> "We are now going to wash our hands. There are a few washing stations in the room. Make your way to the nearest one."

> "You'll see a washing cup there. Take it, fill it up."

> "We'll wash in the same way how a kohen ('priest') would wash when they entered the Holy Temple in Jerusalem."

**Washing Instructions:**
1. "Pour the water to cover your right hand. Repeat 2 more times."
2. "Then do the same for your left hand."
3. "Dry hands."

**Important Note:**
> "Usually, we would recite a blessing at this point, but not now. (When we wash the second time before eating the matzah, we'll say it then)."

---

## C - Karpas

> "We take a piece of potato from one of the bowls on the table."

> "Then dip it into the saltwater, and turn to page 24. Wait a we will say the blessing together with the rabbi."

**Key Points:**
- Potato from bowls on table
- Dip in saltwater
- Page 24
- Wait for rabbi to lead blessing together

---

## D - Yachatz (Breaking the Matzah)

> "Under the seder plate is a box with 3 matzahs. One person should open the box, take out the middle Matzah, and break it in half."

**The Smaller Piece:**
> "The smaller piece should be put back into the box in between the two other matzahs."

**The Bigger Piece:**
> "The bigger piece should be put into a bag (which can be found inside of the Matzah box). It should be set aside to be used later as the afikoman."

---

## E - Ten Plagues

> "On page 43 (in the bigger font) there are listed the ten plagues. As you say each one, spill a drop of wine out of your glass into the designated bowl. Take turns and pass the bowl around."

**Key Points:**
- Page 43, bigger font
- One drop per plague
- Spill into designated bowl
- Pass bowl around table

---

## F - Second Hand Washing (Rochtzah) and Matzah

> "We will now wash our hands again the same way that we did before. 3 times on the right hand, and then three times on the left hand."

**New Detail:**
> "You'll find bags of Shmurah Matzah waiting near the washing stations. Take one with you."

**After Washing:**
> "When you get back to your seat, we'll wait quietly until the rest of the group has washed."

**Blessing and Eating:**
> "Rabbi will lead us in the three blessings on pages 54 and 55, and then we'll eat the Matzah. Don't forget to recline."

**Key Points:**
- Same washing as before
- Take matzah bag from washing station
- Wait quietly for everyone
- Three blessings (pages 54-55)
- Eat while reclining

---

## G - Maror

> "Maror. Take some lettuce leaves, and put some of the horseradish on it. Dip it onto the charoses (only a bit so we don't ruin the harshness!)."

**Procedure:**
> "Don't wait for the group. Turn to page 56, make the blessing, and eat it. We do not lean for the eating of the maror."

**Key Points:**
- Lettuce leaves with horseradish
- Dip in charoses (only a bit!)
- Don't wait for group
- Page 56
- Do NOT recline

---

## H - Korech (Sandwich)

> "The table leader should distribute some more Matzah from the box. Take some more maror exactly as we did before. Make a sandwich by putting the maror inside of two pieces of Matzah. Dip it onto charoses. Then read from page 57, and eat it while reclining. Don't wait for the whole group."

**Key Points:**
- Table leader distributes matzah
- Maror same as before
- Sandwich: maror between two matzahs
- Dip in charoses
- Page 57
- Recline
- Don't wait for group

---

## I - Start of Meal

> "We start the meal by dipping the egg into the saltwater. Pass the saltwater around so everyone has a chance to dip."

---

## J - Afikoman

> "The table leader should take out the afikoman bag and give everyone a piece. Supplement from the box in the center so that everyone can get a decent sized piece. Eat it while reclining."

**Key Points:**
- Table leader distributes
- Supplement from center box if needed
- Everyone gets decent sized piece
- Recline while eating

---

## For Shluchim: Using Table Leader Scripts

### Why This Document Works

**Clear Structure:**
- Alphabetical progression (A-J)
- Matches Seder order
- Easy to reference quickly

**Complete Information:**
- What to do
- Where to find it (page numbers)
- How to do it (specific instructions)
- When to wait vs. proceed

**Addresses Common Confusions:**
- "Don't wait for the group" (specified when needed)
- Reclining clarified each time
- "Only a bit" of charoses on maror
- Which washing has blessing, which doesn't

---

### Adapting for Your Community

**Customization Needed:**

**Page Numbers:**
- This references specific Haggadah
- Update to match your Haggadah's pages
- Or: Remove page numbers, use section names
- Or: Provide your own Haggadah with marked sections

**Language Level:**
- This assumes some familiarity
- May need more explanation for total newcomers
- May need less detail for experienced participants
- Consider audience demographics

**Logistics:**
- "Washing stations in the room" - plan accordingly
- "Bags of Shmurah Matzah" - specify type
- "Designated bowl" for spilling wine drops
- "Box in the center" - each table needs this

---

### Training Table Leaders

**Before the Seder:**

**Rehearsal:**
- Walk through all 10 announcements
- Practice reading aloud
- Clarify any questions
- Understand why each step

**Materials:**
- Give each table leader this script
- Highlight their specific responsibilities
- Ensure they know page numbers
- Review timing and pacing

**Special Instructions:**
- Explain "don't wait for group" vs. "wait quietly"
- Demonstrate reclining
- Show how to distribute items
- Practice washing instructions

---

### Key Transitions Handled

**From This Script:**

**Building Suspense:**
- "Wait a we will say the blessing together" (karpas)
- "Wait quietly until rest of group has washed" (before matzah)

**Independent Action:**
- "Don't wait for the group" (maror and korech)
- Empowers participants to proceed

**Table Leader Responsibilities:**
- Break middle matzah (D)
- Distribute extra matzah (H)
- Distribute afikoman (J)

---

### Page Number Cross-Reference

**From This Script:**
- Page 21: Kiddush
- Page 24: Karpas blessing
- Page 43: Ten plagues (bigger font)
- Pages 54-55: Three blessings for matzah
- Page 56: Maror blessing
- Page 57: Korech

**For Your Haggadah:**
- Create similar reference sheet
- Or mark pages in advance
- Or print page numbers on cards
- Or use colored tabs

---

### Equipment Checklist (Implied from Script)

**From These Instructions:**

**Per Table:**
- Seder plate
- Box with 3 matzahs underneath
- Bag inside matzah box (for afikoman)
- Bowls of potato
- Saltwater
- Lettuce leaves
- Horseradish
- Charoses
- Designated bowl (for spilling wine)
- Extra matzah box in center
- Hard boiled eggs
- Wine/grape juice (enough for 4 cups per person)

**In Room:**
- Multiple washing stations
- Washing cups at each station
- Paper towels
- Bags of Shmurah Matzah at washing stations

---

### Timing Considerations

**From Script Structure:**

**Slow Points (Everyone Together):**
- A: Filling cups, kiddush
- B: All washing hands
- C: Blessing together
- F: All washing again, waiting for group

**Fast Points (Don't Wait):**
- G: Maror individual
- H: Korech individual

**Estimated Timeline:**
- A through F: ~30-45 minutes
- F (washing/matzah): 15-20 minutes alone
- G through J: Quick succession
- Then: Full meal
- After meal: Rest of Seder

---

### Common Questions Addressed

**This Script Answers:**

**Q: Do we say blessing at first washing?**
A: No. "Usually we would... but not now."

**Q: Do we recline for maror?**
A: No. "We do not lean for the eating of the maror."

**Q: How much charoses on maror?**
A: "Only a bit so we don't ruin the harshness!"

**Q: Wait for everyone before eating matzah?**
A: Yes. "Wait quietly until rest of group has washed."

**Q: Wait for everyone before maror/korech?**
A: No. "Don't wait for the group."

**Q: Where do I get the afikoman?**
A: "Table leader should take out the afikoman bag"

### Denominations and Variations

**This Script Accommodates:**

**Language:**
- Uses some Hebrew terms (Shehechiyanu)
- Explains others (kohen = priest)
- Balances accessibility

**Practice:**
- References kohanim washing
- Explains spiritual significance
- Connects to Temple service

**Kashrut:**
- Specifies "Shmurah Matzah"
- Implies higher standard
- Or: Use term your community uses

### Creating Your Own Table Leader Script

**Based on This Model:**

**Structure:**
1. Use letters or numbers for progression
2. Match actual Seder order
3. Include all transitions
4. Specify page numbers
5. Clarify recline/no recline
6. Note wait/don't wait
7. Assign table leader tasks

**Content:**
- Quote what to say aloud
- Instructions in different formatting
- Parenthetical notes for clarity
- Page references
- Procedural details

**Length:**
- This one: 10 announcements (A-J)
- Could expand to 15 (full Seder)
- Or condense to key transitions only
- Adjust to your needs
