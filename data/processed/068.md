### Establish Track Record

**Opening Paragraph:**
> "For the past four years, the Jewish Federation of Brevard and Chabad of the Space & Treasure Coasts have provided a fascinating project for the Jewish Community of Brevard - CHANUKAH AT THE MALL. CHANUKAH AT THE MALL has proven to be a highly successful program drawing an average of 500 people a year from all segments of the Jewish community in a true manifestation of Jewish unity. Indeed, CATM is directly responsible for connecting countless 'closet Jews' to our Jewish community and its affiliations."

**Key Points:**
- **4-year track record** (proven success)
- **Co-sponsors** (Federation + Chabad = legitimacy)
- **Quantifiable results** (500 people/year average)
- **Diverse attendance** (all segments)
- **Social impact** ("closet Jews" now connected)
- **Acronym** (CATM - shows it's established)

---

### This Year's Event

**Event Details:**
> "This year, we are happy to announce that on Sunday, December 17, Brevard will once again enjoy the Chanukah program at the Melbourne Square Mall highlighted by Israeli Star & Entertainer, Avi Albrecht, in concert."

**Event Features:**
1. **Israeli entertainer** in concert (star power)
2. **Satellite link-up** of world Chanukah celebrations (tech + global)
3. **Menorah lighting ceremony** (traditional)
4. **Congressman Dave Weldon** and dignitaries (VIP participation)
5. **Children's song presentation**
6. **Hot potato latkes** distribution (food!)

**Additional:**
- **Full room of hands-on activities** for children
- **Beautiful Chanukah exhibit** explaining origin and symbols
- **Displayed in mall all through Chanukah**

---

### The Vision Statement

**Stated Objective:**
> "Our objective of CHANUKAH AT THE MALL is to bring the joy of Chanukah and the pride of Judaism to every Jew in our community in an expression of Jewish warmth, and unity. We look forward to your participation with us to recreate the Chanukah miracle of Jewish solidarity."

**Key Themes:**
- Joy and pride
- Reaching "every Jew"
- Warmth and unity
- "Miracle of Jewish solidarity"

---

### Closing

## Additional Tips

### For Mall/Public Space

**Make It Easy:**
- Provide floor plan
- List equipment needs
- Offer to handle cleanup
- Show insurance certificate
- Propose dates with flexibility

**Make It Beneficial:**
- Foot traffic for stores
- Positive PR for mall
- Family-friendly reputation
- Community goodwill
- No cost to venue

---

### For Dignitaries

**Make It Appealing:**
- Significant crowd
- Media opportunity
- Universal message
- Non-controversial
- Constituent engagement
- Brief commitment

**Make It Clear:**
- Exact time/location
- Parking information
- What's expected (5-min greeting?)
- Who else attending
- Media present
- Easy yes/no response

---

**Bottom Line:**
These letters succeed by establishing credibility (track record, co-sponsors, attendance figures), making clear asks (specific dates, participation), and showing mutual benefit (venue gets traffic, dignitary gets constituent engagement). The mall letter emphasizes partnership and proven success; the dignitary letter would emphasize community significance and universal themes. Adapt these templates but keep the professional tone, specific details, and clear benefit propositions. Always follow up after events with gratitude and results - building relationships for future years.
