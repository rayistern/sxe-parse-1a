## Safety & Logistics

### Health & Safety

**Considerations:**
- Headset sanitization between users
- Hand sanitizer available
- Adequate space to prevent collisions
- Age restrictions (typically 13+, check VR guidelines)
- Motion sickness warnings
- Parental consent for minors

### Technical Requirements

**Setup Needs:**
- Wi-Fi connectivity (check requirements)
- Charging stations
- Backup batteries
- Technical troubleshooting plan
- Test run before event

### Equipment Management

**Care & Handling:**
- Protective storage case
- Cleaning supplies
- Instruction manual
- Spare parts if needed
- Insurance consideration

## Questions to Ask

### Before Committing

**Key Questions:**
1. What exactly is included in the VR experience?
2. How long is the VR content?
3. Is it age-appropriate for all children?
4. What is the final cost based on current participation?
5. When will the content be delivered?
6. What format/platform is it on?
7. Can we use it multiple times?
8. Are there any licensing restrictions?
9. Do we need specific technical specs?
10. What support is provided?
