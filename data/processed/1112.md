## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Learning booklet reference

**Title:** "Learning Booklet Geared for the Whole Family"

**Occasion:** Acharon Shel Pesach (Last Day of Passover / 8th day)

**Purpose:** Farbrengen and Seudas Moshiach material

**URL:** https://merkos302.com/learning-booklet-geared-for-the-whole-family/

**Dedication:** Lzchus Refuah Shleima of Chaim Shneur Zalman Ben Hinda Yocheved Shlit"a, JNet's Director

---

## Context

**The Setting:**
> "This Acharon Shel Pesach, as we remain in our homes, let us realize that we have a unique opportunity to farbreng and unite with our family and celebrate Seudas Moshiach with them."

**Purpose:**
> "To assist in bringing a Moshiach'dike discussion to the table, we present here excerpts of the Rebbe's farbrengen of Acharon Shel Pesach 5743."

## Contents

**What's Included:**
- Excerpts of Rebbe's farbrengen (Acharon Shel Pesach 5743)
- Summary of points the Rebbe discusses
- Points to ponder
- Practical applications
- History of minhag to have four cups of wine by Moshiach seudah
