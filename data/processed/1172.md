# Matzah Gift Letter - Chabad of Maple, Ontario

**Source**: Chabad of Maple
**Location**: Maple, Ontario
**Date**: Nissan 3, 5776 (April 11, 2016)
**Authors**: Rabbi Chezky & Shaina Deren
**Type**: Personalized matzah gift letter template

## Full Letter Text

Nissan 3, 5776
April 11, 2016
Maple, Ontario

Dear Friends,

Greetings & Blessings,

One of the beautiful Mitzvahs of Pesach is eating the Matzah commemorating the unleavened bread that our ancestors ate when leaving Egypt. The Zohar teaches us that Matzah on Pesach is a "food of faith" as well as a "food of good health". This precious Mitzvah is one that has been cherished by our people throughout history; even under the most difficult circumstances.

Matzah – The Poor Man's Bread- teaches us an important lesson in humility. As high as we rise and as far as we reach we must never forget our humble beginnings. In every generation and every day we must remember that we in fact were slaves in Egypt and never take for granted G-d's gift of 'Freedom'. The simplicity of the Matzahs remind us never to forget about those less fortunate than we are.

Although this Mitzvah can be performed by eating any plain Kosher for Pesach Matzah, this cherished Mitzvah is best done at the Seder by eating handmade "Matzot Shmurot". While a machine can make a perfect Matzah; it lacks 'soul-Kavanah'. When it comes to a Mitzvah intentions absolutely do count! The ingredients of these Matzahs are only flour, water and three and half thousand years of human heart and soul.

These delicious handmade in Israel Matzahs are our gift to you. In order to do the Mitzvah properly please be sure to eat at least one half of a Round Shmurah Matzah during each of your Pesach Seders (after dark).

Enjoy!

Our best wishes to you and your family for a Kosher, Joyous, and stress free Passover!

Rabbi Chezky & Shaina Deren
Chabad of Maple
