## Response #1: Daniel Sanoff, Poughkeepsie, NY (March 8, 2017)

### First Class Mail Solution

**Weight Limit**: If less than 13 ounces, can send First Class mail

**Commercial Rate Advantage**:
- Using stamps.com or similar: $3.47 for 13oz (commercial rate)
- Retail rate: $4.11
- **Savings**: $0.64 per package (14% discount)

### Packaging Selection

**Tested Multiple Envelopes**, settled on:
- **Product**: Bubble mailer from Uline
- **Model**: S-7550
- **Description**: Uline Bubble-Lined Polyolefin Mailers 7 1/4 x 20
- **Link**: http://www.uline.com/Product/Detail/S-7550/Poly-Mailers/Uline-Bubble-Lined-Polyolefin-Mailers-7-14-1-4-x-20?keywords=s-7550
- **Weight**: Just over 1 oz

**Total Package Weight**:
- Matzah + bubble mailer + letter/brochure = 11 oz

**Strategy**: "Because of slight variations in the weight of the matzo, and not wanting to individually weigh each piece, we just paid the 13oz rate for each package."

**Rationale**: Easier to pay slightly more and guarantee First Class eligibility than risk packages going over 13oz limit

### Label Recommendation

**Strong Preference**: "I would HIGHLY recommend paying the extra for AVERY products as they peel off easily while the cheaper versions can be a hassle to deal with."

**Product**: amazon.com/Avery-Shipping (specific product link truncated in document)

**Reasoning**: Time savings and frustration reduction worth the premium when processing 500 packages

### Bottom Line Costs

**Per Package**:
- Packaging (Uline bubble mailer): ~$0.83 (estimated based on typical Uline pricing for this product)
- Postage (13oz First Class with tracking): $3.47
- **Total**: ~$4.30 + cost of actual matzah

**Benefits**:
- Includes tracking
- Works anywhere in the country (no zone pricing)
- First Class speed (2-5 business days typically)

### Important Warning

**Weight Considerations for Printed Materials**:

"Keep in mind: If you're going to add printed material to the package, many pesach guides and other brochures can weigh a lot, and push you over the 13 oz limit, which forces you to pay priority mail rates."

**Implication**: If adding heavy brochures/Haggadahs, may need to:
- Use lighter paper stock
- Reduce number of pages
- Switch to Priority Mail pricing
- Weigh samples before committing to full run

## For Shluchim: Comprehensive Shipping Decision Framework

### Method Comparison

**Option 1: First Class Mail (Sanoff Method)**
- **Best for**: 50-1000 packages, quality presentation, tracking needed
- **Cost**: ~$4.30 + matzah
- **Pros**: Tracking, reasonable speed, national flat rate, professional appearance
- **Cons**: More expensive than bulk, weight limits restrictive
- **Prep time**: Moderate (labels, stuffing envelopes)

**Option 2: Priority Mail Flat Rate (Hein Method)**
- **Best for**: Heavier packages, including substantial printed materials
- **Cost**: ~$13.65 per medium flat rate box (2017 rate)
- **Pros**: No weight concerns up to 70 lbs, tracking included, faster delivery
- **Cons**: Significantly more expensive, box size may be excessive for just matzah
- **Prep time**: Low (just seal boxes)

**Option 3: Non-Profit Bulk Mail**
- **Best for**: 500+ packages, advance planning, budget-conscious
- **Cost**: ~$0.50-1.00 per piece
- **Pros**: Dramatically cheaper at scale, non-profit rates
- **Cons**: Requires permit ($240/year), slow (2-3 weeks), complex preparation, minimum quantities
- **Prep time**: High (sorting, bundling, paperwork)

**Option 4: Commercial Matzah Box Solutions (Eisenbach/MatzahBox.org)**
- **Best for**: Simplicity, protective packaging, professional appearance
- **Cost**: Variable (boxes + chosen shipping method)
- **Pros**: Purpose-designed protection, professional, may offer turnkey solutions
- **Cons**: Box cost adds to expense
- **Prep time**: Low to moderate depending on service level

### Cost Analysis for 500 Packages

**First Class Mail (Sanoff Method)**:
- Packaging: $415 (500 × $0.83)
- Postage: $1,735 (500 × $3.47)
- Labels: $20 (Avery shipping labels)
- Matzah: $1,000-2,500 (500 × $2-5 per matzah depending on source/size)
- **Total**: $3,170-4,670
- **Per package**: $6.34-9.34

**Priority Flat Rate (Hein Method)**:
- Boxes: Free (USPS supplies)
- Postage: $6,825 (500 × $13.65)
- Matzah: $1,000-2,500
- **Total**: $7,825-9,325
- **Per package**: $15.65-18.65

**Bulk Mail Method**:
- Boxes (Eisenbach): $250-500 (estimated $0.50-1.00 per box)
- Bulk mail permit: $240 (annual)
- Postage: $250-500 (500 × $0.50-1.00 non-profit rate)
- Matzah: $1,000-2,500
- **Total**: $1,740-3,740 (first year including permit), $1,500-3,500 (subsequent years)
- **Per package**: $3.00-7.00

**Savings Analysis**:
- Bulk Mail vs. First Class: Save $1,430-$1,930 (42-52% savings)
- Bulk Mail vs. Priority: Save $6,085-$5,585 (78% savings)
- **But**: Bulk requires advance planning (3+ weeks before Pesach for delivery)

### Implementation Recommendations

**For First-Time Matzah Mailing**:
1. Start with First Class (Sanoff method)
2. Learn logistics, breakage rates, recipient feedback
3. Evaluate whether worth bulk mail investment next year

**For Established Annual Program (500+)**:
1. Invest in bulk mail permit
2. Use Eisenbach hard boxes
3. Mail 4 weeks before Pesach
4. Save $1,500-2,000 annually to reinvest in more matzah or recipients

**For Premium Presentation**:
1. Use Priority Flat Rate
2. Include substantial printed materials (Haggadah, Pesach guide, etc.)
3. Position as high-value gift justifying higher cost
4. Target major donors or VIPs

**For Maximum Reach with Limited Budget**:
1. Bulk mail to maximize number of recipients
2. Simple packaging
3. Focus resources on matzah quality rather than shipping speed

### Practical Tips from the Discussion

**Packaging Materials**:
- **Bubble mailers**: Uline S-7550 proven effective
- **Hard boxes**: Eisenbach/MatzahBox.org for better protection
- **Labels**: Invest in Avery brand - time savings worth premium when doing 500 packages

**Weight Management**:
- Test package weight before bulk ordering materials
- Account for variations in matzah weight
- Round up to next postage tier to avoid individual weighing (Sanoff's strategy)
- Remember printed materials add significant weight

**Workflow Efficiency**:
- Production line approach: One station stuffing, one labeling, one sealing
- Recruit volunteers - make it a community event ("Mivtza Matza" party)
- Allow 2-3 days for 500 packages with volunteer team
- Schedule 3-4 weeks before Pesach to allow shipping time

**Tracking Considerations**:
- First Class and Priority include tracking
- Bulk mail typically does not
- For major donors, consider upgrading to tracked service even if using bulk for others
- Tracking allows proactive follow-up if packages delayed

### Mailing List Considerations

**Quality Over Quantity**:
- 500 households is significant expense ($1,500-9,000 depending on method)
- Ensure addresses are current
- Consider segmentation: Core community vs. peripheral contacts
- May be more impactful to send fewer packages with personal notes than mass mailing

**Compliance**:
- Non-profit bulk mail requires statement of ownership
- Can't use bulk mail for commercial purposes
- Ensure Chabad house has current 501(c)(3) status
- Keep records for audit purposes

**Follow-Up Strategy**:
- Include note with matzah explaining how to use for Seder
- Add contact information for Pesach questions or Seder invitation
- Consider including response card or QR code to confirm receipt and gather feedback
- Use mailing as touchpoint for Pesach invitation or other engagement

### Timing Considerations

**First Class**: Mail 1-2 weeks before Pesach
- Arrives in 2-5 business days
- Buffer for delays
- Fresh arrival creates excitement

**Priority**: Mail 10 days-2 weeks before
- Faster delivery (1-3 days)
- Can mail closer to holiday

**Bulk**: Mail 4 weeks minimum before Pesach
- Slow processing (2-3 weeks)
- Must factor in postal service delays
- Risk of late arrival if mailed too close
