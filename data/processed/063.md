# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Jewish Women's Circle Chanukah Speech

## Complete Event Speech for Women's Program

**Occasion:** First Lyndhurst Chabad Jewish Women's Circle
**Date:** Yud Tes Kislev (19th of Kislev)
**Format:** Welcome speech + Chanukah teaching + Craft activity

---

## Opening: Welcome

> "B"H Good evening!"
>
> "It is my pleasure to welcome you to the Jewish Women's Circle. This event is being held in honor of the upcoming holiday of Chanukah."

---

## Special Significance of the Date

### Yud Tes Kislev - The Alter Rebbe's Liberation

**Historical Context:**
> "Today actually happens to be an important day in the Chabad movement and interestingly is the first of our Lyndhurst Chabad Jewish women's circle."

**What Happened:**
> "Why is today a monumental day in Chabad? It is Yud tes kislev, the 19th of kislev, about 200 years ago on this day, the first Chabad Rebbe, the Alter Rebbe was redeemed from being imprisoned for teaching chassidus, a deeper explanation to the torah."

---

### The Story: Where Are You?

**The Setting:**
The Alter Rebbe imprisoned for teaching Chassidus

**The Question:**
> "One of the ministers of the govt posed the Alter Rebbe with a question: 'The verse in the beginning of the book genesis after Adam and Eve sinned, states "And G-d called to Adam, where are you" the minister asked "didn't G-d know where adam was?"'"

**The Alter Rebbe's Answer:**
> "When a person is for ex so & so many years old, G-d asks him 'Where are you'? Are you aware of why you were created on this earth? Are you aware of what you are expected to do and how much you have actually done?"

---

### Application to Us

**The Personal Question:**
> "This applies very much to us as Jewish Women."

**Implied Questions:**
- Where are you in your Jewish journey?
- Why were you created?
- What are you expected to do?
- How much have you accomplished?
- Are you fulfilling your purpose?

---

## The Special Relationship: Women and Chanukah

### Introduction

> "Having said that now I would like to focus on the special relationship of women to the story of Chanukah. There are two great heroines that we remember on Chanukah. Each connected to a different aspect of the Chanukah tale."

---

## Heroine #1: Chana

### The Mother of Seven Sons

**Who She Was:**
> "First we remember Chana. Chana was the mother of seven righteous sons."

---

### The King's Sport

**Antiochus's Cruelty:**
> "The wicked King Antioches would make a sport of forcing Jews to bow down to an idol."

---

### The Arrest and Martyrdom

**The Day:**
> "One day Chana and her seven sons were arrested and Antioches commanded each son to prostrate himself to the idol."

**Each Son's Response:**
> "Each son respectively defied the king and refused to compromise their devotion to G-d. One by one each son was killed in front of Chana's eyes."

---

### Chana's Final Act

**Her Proclamation:**
> "Immediately after that, our sages tell us, Chana climbed to the roof of the nearby building and proclaimed 'G-d A-lmighty, I have fulfilled my mission in this world – I have educated all of my sons to be loyal to you and your Torah. Please accept their deaths as a sacrifice on behalf of your nation Israel!' and she plunged to her death."

---

## Heroine #2: Yehudit

### Who She Was

**Her Background:**
> "Then there is the heroine Yehudit. This courageous woman came from the priestly family and was related to the Maccabees."

---

### The Oppressive Law

**Jus Primae Noctis:**
> "At the time, the law of the land was that any maiden that wished to marry must first present herself to the local Greek general."

---

### Her Plan

**The Setup:**
> "On the eve of her wedding she showed up to the army camp with a basket of cheese and wine."

**The Execution:**
> "When she was alone with the general she fed him cheese. As a result he became very thirsty. She served him very strong wine and in short order the man was asleep in a drunken stupor."

**The Victory:**
> "Wasting no time Yehudit drew his sword and severed his head and fled from the scene. When the soldiers saw their leader dead they were thrown in confusion and a great victory for the Maccabees (the jewish army) occurred."

---

## What These Heroines Teach Us

### Introduction

> "Both of these heroines are eternal examples for the Jewish woman in all generations."

---

### Lesson from Chana: Education

**The Imperative:**
> "We must be like Chana – devoted to the education of our children. It is in our hands to ensure they grow to be devoted and proud Jews. Willing to withstand the pressures of society and proudly carry the banner of Judaism into the future."

**Our Responsibility:**
- Educate children in Judaism
- Raise devoted Jews
- Build pride in identity
- Teach resistance to pressure
- Ensure continuity

---

### Lesson from Yehudit: Stepping Up

**The Challenge:**
> "And we are inspired by Yehudit to step up to the plate under all circumstances –now days luckily we don't have to endanger our very lives for the survival and integrity of our nation, however we all have sacrifices that we can make to ensure the stability of our people."

**Modern Examples:**
- "Whether it's being different by decorating your home for Chanukah as opposed to the way your neighbors are decorating their home"
- "Or by giving up something in order to celebrate Shabbat with your family etc."

**The Point:**
Small sacrifices today ensure Jewish survival tomorrow

---

### The Conclusion

**Our Identity:**
> "This year as we light the Menorah and retell the stories of old to our families – we realize that we are the Chana's and Yehudit's of today. We as Jewish Women have the power to ensure the eternal continuity of our people."

---

## The Miracle of Chanukah

### Transition

> "Now I would like to talk about the Miracle of Chanukah, what happened so many years ago, why do we celebrate Chanukah today?"

---

### The Historical Overview

**The Evil King:**
> "In the times of the 2nd holy temple there was a Greek king Antiochus who believed he ruled over Israel."

**His Decrees:**
> "He decreed that everyone must serve his idols, Jews may not keep Shabbat, have a brit, keep kosher food or learn from the Torah."

---

### Jewish Resistance

**Hiding and Learning:**
> "When the Jews heard this many of them ran away and hid in caves in order to teach their children Torah. If any Greeks Soldiers came they would hide their torah scrolls and take out dreidels, and pretend they were just playing driedel (this is the reason we play driedel on Chanukah)."

**The Origin of Dreidel:**
Hidden Torah study - when soldiers came, Jews pretended to be gambling

---

### The Maccabees

**The Threat:**
> "Then the Jews heard news that thousands of greek soldiers were coming to fight them. They were scared, they were only a few compared to the Greeks."

**Judah's Courage:**
> "One man Judah was brave and said whoever believes in G-d come with me and Judah the Macabee formed a jewish army called the macabees to fight the greeks."

**The Victory:**
> "Having G-d on their side they won, which was clearly miraculous."

---

### Finding the Oil

**The Destroyed Temple:**
> "When the Jews came back to the holy temple it was a wreck, idols everywhere. They cleaned up and when they wanted to light the Menorah in the holy temple, they found all the jugs of oil were broken, none had the seal which was needed in order for the oil to be used for the menorah."

**The Search:**
> "They searched and searched until they finally found one jug that had a seal."

**The Miracle:**
> "It was a little bit that really should have only lasted one day however a great miracle happened and the oil kept the Menorah lit for 8 days until they were able to get more oil."

---

### Why We Celebrate

**The Commemoration:**
> "That is why we celebrate chanuka today, we remember the miracle that g-d performed by lighting our own menorahs on each of the 8 nights of Chanukah."

**The Purpose:**
> "And through this may we merit to bring more light into the world."

## Activity: Decorative Oil Jars

### Transition

### The Options

**Two Formats:**
1. **Decoration only** - using vinegar
2. **Herb oils** - using olive oil

**Recommendation:**
> "Even though the main point is the oil, vinegar is a better preservative and the olive oil bottles would need to be kept in fridge and used within a week to prevent risk of bacteria etc. you can choose which option you would like I do highly recommend using vinegar and just making it as a decoration"
