## Key Planning Highlights

### Location Selection

**Critical Considerations:**
- Choose a location other than your Chabad House when possible
- May attract those wary of Chabad House and Orthodox religion
- Appeals to those looking to celebrate the season

**Layout Questions:**
- Does the room provide easy way to cordon areas for specific stations?
- Will you need room dividers?
- Sufficient electric outlets in reasonable locations? (You will be using lots of electricity)
- How many stations/activities based on space and anticipated traffic?

---

### Insurance Requirements

**VITAL:** Notify your insurance company that you are planning to hold an event of such nature, whether in your Chabad House or at any other location.

**Why Important:**
- In case of any mishaps
- Must be apprised prior to the event
- Applies to any venue location

---

### Timing Strategy

**Planning Questions:**
- How many days of Chanukah will you run the event?
- What else is scheduled in your community during these days?
- Any special shows or events to highlight on particular days?

**Special Features:**
- Chanukah Storytime
- Concert
- Puppet show

---

### Activity Selection Guidelines

**Choose Activities That:**
- Your crowd is least likely to have opportunity to do elsewhere
- Are appropriate for children up to age 12-13
- Include toddler section options

**Variety Is Key:**
- Mix of active and passive activities
- Educational and entertainment balance
- Craft take-homes and interactive experiences

---

### Marketing & Promotion

**Press Release Strategy:**
- Write up advance press release
- Describe sampling of activities, shows and events
- Include contact information for reservations
- Offer group reservation options

**Key Message:**
> "It's not a religious holiday, but a seasonal holiday"

**Use This To Your Advantage!**

**Distribution Channels:**
- Community calendars
- Youth magazines
- Local newspapers
- Social media
- Email lists
- School newsletters

**Pro Tip:** You can post "Location to Be Announced" if you don't have one yet!

## Success Factors

### What Makes It Work

1. **Accessibility** - Easy location to find and access
2. **Variety** - Multiple stations and activities
3. **Quality** - Well-planned and executed stations
4. **Flow** - Good traffic management
5. **Staff** - Enough volunteers at each station
6. **Atmosphere** - Festive Chanukah environment
7. **Value** - Free or low-cost admission
8. **Inclusivity** - Welcoming to all community members

### Avoid Common Pitfalls

- ❌ Insufficient electrical outlets
- ❌ Crowded spaces without clear flow
- ❌ Activities that are too advanced or too simple
- ❌ Inadequate supervision at stations
- ❌ Running out of craft supplies
- ❌ Long wait times
- ❌ Poor signage and wayfinding

## Resource Planning

### Supply Management

**Craft Supplies:**
- Order in bulk
- Have 20% extra for each craft
- Pre-sort materials by station
- Replenishment system during event

**Equipment:**
- Test all electrical equipment beforehand
- Have backup supplies
- Extension cords and power strips
- Tables and chairs
- Display materials

### Volunteer Coordination

**Staffing Needs:**
- Station supervisors (1-2 per station)
- Greeters and guides
- Setup and cleanup crew
- Photography team
- Troubleshooters

**Training:**
- Pre-event briefing
- Station instructions
- Safety protocols
- Schedule and breaks
