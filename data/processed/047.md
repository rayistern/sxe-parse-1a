## Template

**Dear Mr. & Mrs. [Last Name],**

This Chanukah, for the **fifth time**, we will be having a Public Menorah lighting ceremony in Liverpool City Centre. The **20-foot menorah** will be erected outside St. George's Hall and lighting will take place every evening at 6:00.

As a **friend and long-time supporter** of Lubavitch Foundation Liverpool, please accept this **personal invitation** to attend the special communal lighting ceremony, which will take place at **6:00 on Tuesday evening, December 3, the 5th candle of Chanukah**, in the presence of the **Lord Mayor of Liverpool**.

This will be an event for the entire community, young and old, **not to be missed**.

Wishing you a very joyous Chanukah,

Rabbi [REDACTED-NAME]
Lubavitch Liverpool

## Key Elements of Effective Invitation

### 1. Establish Tradition

> "for the fifth time"

**Why This Works:**
- Shows this is established event
- Not first-time experiment
- Proven track record
- Annual tradition they shouldn't miss

---

### 2. Impressive Details

> "20-foot menorah"

**Specificity Matters:**
- Exact height creates visual
- Impressive scale
- Professional execution
- Worth attending

> "erected outside St. George's Hall"

**Prestigious Location:**
- Well-known landmark
- Central location
- Public prominence
- Easy to find

---

### 3. Personal Touch

> "As a friend and long-time supporter"

**Recognition:**
- Acknowledges relationship
- Appreciates their support
- Makes them feel valued
- Not mass mailing (even if it is)

> "please accept this personal invitation"

**Exclusivity:**
- Special invitation
- VIP treatment
- Personally selected
- Privileged access

---

### 4. Specific Time and Date

> "6:00 on Tuesday evening, December 3, the 5th candle of Chanukah"

**Complete Information:**
- Exact time
- Day of week
- Secular date
- Which night of Chanukah
- No confusion

---

### 5. Highlight Special Guest

> "in the presence of the Lord Mayor of Liverpool"

**VIP Element:**
- Important dignitary
- Official recognition
- Photo opportunity
- Civic importance
- Social status

---

### 6. Broad Appeal

> "This will be an event for the entire community, young and old"

**Inclusive:**
- Bring whole family
- All ages welcome
- Community gathering
- Not exclusive or limited

---

### 7. Urgency/FOMO

> "not to be missed"

**Creates:**
- Sense of importance
- Fear of missing out
- Compelling attendance
- Social expectation

## Final Thoughts

**This Invitation Works Because:**

1. **Personal** - Acknowledges recipient specifically
2. **Detailed** - All necessary information included
3. **Impressive** - Highlights scale and importance
4. **Prestigious** - VIP guest mentioned
5. **Inclusive** - Everyone welcome
6. **Urgent** - "Not to be missed"
7. **Professional** - Well-written and clear

**Result:**
Higher attendance from key community members and supporters

**Use this template** as basis for your own personalized invitations to ensure strong turnout from your balebatim and community leaders.
