**Organization Tips:**

**Sample Playlist Structures:**

## Volume Management

### Best Practices

**Background Music:**
- Quiet enough to talk over
- Creates atmosphere
- Not distracting
- Adjust by area

**Active Music:**
- Loud enough to energize
- Clear and clean
- Not painfully loud
- Consider different ages

**Age Considerations:**
- Elderly: Lower volume
- Young children: Moderate
- Teens: Can handle louder
- Mixed group: Conservative
