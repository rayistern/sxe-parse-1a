## Maintenance & Setup Tips

### 1. WD40 for Menorah Sockets

**Prevents Rust and Stuck Bulbs**

**After Chanukah:**
- Before putting away your menorah (both car and large)
- Spray the light sockets with WD40
- Prevents rust during storage
- Next year's bulbs will install easily

**Before Chanukah:**
- Spray sockets BEFORE inserting bulbs
- Makes bulb removal much easier later
- "You would be having a much easier time getting the bulbs out :-)"

**Application:**
- Light spray in each socket
- Let dry before installing bulbs
- Works for both indoor and outdoor menorahs

---

### 2. Paraffin Candle Preparation

**For Souvenir Shop Candles**

**Pre-Event Wick Pulling:**
- Take tweezers (or do by hand)
- Pull wicks out a drop - not too much
- Creates easier lighting
- Produces larger flame

**Pre-Light New Candles:**
> "Pre light them - especially new ones, will save you alot of tzoros..."

**Why This Works:**
- New candles often have stiff wicks
- Pre-lighting creates carbon tip
- Much easier to relight at event
- First flame "primes" the wick

**Fuel Squirt Technique:**
> "Right before you light each candle... turn the candle upside-down and squeeze. It will squirt out some fuel. It should make it easier to light."

**Application:**
- Just before lighting (or during setup)
- Turn candle upside down
- Gentle squeeze
- Fuel spreads on top
- Dramatically easier ignition

## Fresh Latke Frying at Events

### Equipment Setup

**The Game-Changer:**
> "We bought a camping grill (double flame with a wind guard). They also sold a griddle in the same outdoor items area. We brought it to the menorah lighting and fried fresh latkes."

**Why Camping Grill vs Electric:**
> "The electric ones are not really hot enough."

**Equipment List:**
1. **Camping grill** - double flame with wind guard
2. **Griddle** - sold in outdoor items area (same section)
3. **Extra fuel** - one bottle sufficient for one lighting, but bring backup
4. **Different fuel types** available:
   - Home Depot: Multiple fuel types (different color bottles)
   - Target: Regular camping type only

**For Cold Climates:**
> "If you are in a very cold area, consider using a hotter burning fuel."

### DIY Lighting Torch

**Problem:** Need to reach tall menorah flames safely

**Solution:** Homemade torch

**Construction:**

**Materials:**
- Wire hanger
- Paint stick (or broomstick)
- Cotton cloth (or cotton rope, strip of old undershirt/towel)
- Lighter fluid

**Assembly:**
1. Straighten out hanger
2. Fold it a few times (for strength)
3. Attach to end of paint stick/broomstick
4. Tie cotton cloth to the end

**Critical Safety Note:**
> "Make sure that it's cotton. Burn some dry and see if it burns or melts. Don't use it if it melts."

**Usage:**
- Dip in lighter fluid
- Light the torch
- Use to reach menorah lights
- Replace cotton almost every night

**Benefits:**
- Long reach for tall menorahs
- Safe distance
- Reusable
- Inexpensive
- Professional appearance

## Safety Considerations

### Fire Safety

**DIY Torch:**
- Test cotton material first
- Keep lighter fluid away from flames until ready
- Replace worn cotton immediately
- Have fire extinguisher nearby
- Don't over-soak torch

**Latke Frying:**
- Stable surface for camping grill
- Wind guard properly positioned
- Keep children at safe distance
- Have burn kit available
- Extra fuel stored safely away from heat

**Electrical:**
- Weather-rated bulbs for outdoor use
- Proper outdoor-rated extension cords
- GFCI protection if available
- Keep connections dry
- Regular inspection of wiring
