## Purchasing a Car Menorah

### Important Considerations

Bear in mind that your unique situation may mean that one advantage is more important than another.

**Consider:**
- **Highway Speed:** Need sturdy menorah rated for 70+ MPH
- **Bulk Installation:** Need affordable, easy-to-install menorahs for car menorah parade
- **Budget:** Initial investment
- **Installation:** Quick setup vs. maximum durability

---

## Car Menorah Options

### Option 1: Klar Car Menorah

**Link:** https://www.menorah.net/product/2-ft-led-car-display-menorah/

**Price:** $300

**Features:**
- Sturdy construction
- Both suction cups AND straps
- More likely to last through Chanukah on car roof
- Durable design

---

### Option 2: Shaya Smetana Car Menorah

**Contact:** gr8nes.com, +1 (718) 210-3771

**Price:** $225

**Features:**
- Cheaper and flashier
- Entire menorah lights up
- "Happy Chanukah" sign built in
- Made of plastic
- Lightweight
- Two suction cups hold it

**Tradeoffs:**
- Easy installation
- Less durability

---

### Option 3: Magnet Menorah

**Link:** http://www.magnetmenorah.com/

**Price:** $300

**Features:**
- Ideal for quick and easy installation
- Included magnets - simply slap onto roof
- Can withstand high speeds (with extra straps and magnets)
- Lights up

**Considerations:**
- Looks less like traditional menorah
- Often out of stock
