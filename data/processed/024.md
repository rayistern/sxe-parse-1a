## Police Escort for Menorah Parade

### Why It's Difficult Without One

It is difficult to pull off a menorah parade without a police escort, as **traffic lights and other cars will often break up your formation**, leading to confusion and lost cars.

---

## How to Obtain a Police Escort

### Step 1: Reach Out to Local Police Department

Request a police escort.

### Typical Escort Setup

**Lead Car:**
- One police car in front

**Chase Car:**
- One police car bringing up the rear

**Additional Vehicles:**
- Occasionally, additional vehicles deployed to block intersections as the parade passes by

---

## If Police Department is Unwilling or Unable

### Some Potential Solutions:

---

### Solution 1: Reframe the Request

**If Their Concern:**
"They don't want to appear to be joining/supporting a religious event"

**Your Response:**
"You don't need them to lead, you need them to secure the parade. They will be protecting your free speech, not engaging in their own."

---

### Solution 2: Hire Off-Duty Officers

You can often hire police officers off duty.

**Cost:** Anywhere from $35-$100 per officer per hour

---

### Solution 3: Apply for Parade Permit

You can also apply and pay the fee for a parade permit.

**See:** License for Parade section
