## Opening: Personal Baseball Anecdote

### The Curveball Challenge

**Personal Admission:**
> "Can you believe its almost April? With the baseball season only days away, my mind turns to my non existent curveball. You see, the curveball is one of the many reasons I will never be a pitcher. My curveball just doesn't curve. I can throw it straight, fast or slow, but I can't make it curve."

**The Mystery:**
- How do pitchers even do that?
- Can understand straight throw
- While ball in hand, control direction
- But control trajectory after it leaves hand?
- Never gets it right

**Humorous Image:**
> "I am reminded of the children who release their bowling ball and then twist their bodies to the right, hoping the ball will follow suit. Yes, that's me and the curveball."

---

## Purim as Curveball

### Life's Unexpected Challenges

**The Expression:**
- "Life threw you a curveball"
- No reason to expect challenge
- Arrives when off guard
- Caught completely by surprise

**Purim Fit:**
> "Well Purim was certainly one mean curveball."

---

### Jews' Comfortable Position

**Before Haman's Decree:**
- Not expecting annihilation threat
- Comfortable in Persia
- Wealthy
- Socially accepted
- Friends in high places
- Mordechai: respected minister in palace
- Esther: the queen

**Then:**
> "One day, out of the blue, G-d threw them a curveball by the name of Haman and the Jews were caught completely by surprise."

---

### Second Curveball: G-d's Silence

**Historical Pattern:**
- In every Jewish crisis until Purim
- Jews turned to G-d
- He intervened with direct miracle
- Rarely if ever failed
- Pattern: Sin → Punishment threat → Repentance → G-d's response

**G-d's Responses:**
- Either miracle saving them
- Or inform sins beyond salvation
- One thing G-d would definitely NOT do: sit idly by

**Purim Was Different:**
> "Purim was the first occasion, when G-d met the Jewish entreaty with silence."

**The Experience:**
> "Their heart rending pleas for help ascended to the heavens, but returned empty; their cadence bouncing back to earth, their echoes fading off distant hills with nary a response from above."

---

### Human Initiative

**Out of Desperation:**
- Jews decided to act on their own
- Mordechai appointed Esther to appeal
- Esther consented
- Appeared before king with impeccable timing

**Everything Fell Into Place:**
- With perfection
- Scene for dramatic salvation set
- Esther revealed identity
- Precision of maestro
- Haman fell off chair and out of favor

**Result:**
> "A looming crisis became a joyful holiday in the span of a moment."

---

### The Unexpected Nature

**How Unexpected:**
- No miraculous arm from above
- No wall of water descending
- No drowning Persians
- Heavens silent that night
- But grounds thundered with salvation

**Assessment:**
> "A most unusual spectacle. Another true curveball."

---

## The Greatest Curveball Pitcher

### G-d's Mastery

**Baseball Debate:**
> "The book is out on who is the greatest curveball pitcher of all time. Sandy Koufax or Dwight Gooden, your guess is as good as mine. But I can tell you one thing. Nobody's curveball is quite as good as G-d's."

---

### How a Curveball Works

**Mechanics:**
- Thrown high
- Somewhere between mound and plate
- Bottom drops out of ball
- Comes in low

**G-d's Execution:**
> "G-d is really good at it, and Purim was His shining moment."

**Purim Application:**
- Jews prayed
- Expected Him to come in high
- With heavenly miracle
- But He surprised them
- With curveball
- Let bottom drop out
- Came in low
- With earthly, non-miraculous solution

---

## The Fastball vs The Curveball

### The Fastball Pitcher

**Characteristics:**
- Comes in throwing heat
- No matter how fast batter swings
- Ball blows right by
- Open challenge
- Pitcher stares batter down
- Dares him to swing
- Everyone knows what's coming
- Blows it by anyway

**G-d's Fastball:**
> "When G-d comes in throwing heat, no one can stand up to Him."

**Examples:**
- Think you can hurt His children?
- Think you can annihilate His people?
- Think you have it figured out?
- G-d comes along and stares you down

**Historical Examples:**
- Walls of Jericho tumbling down
- Cruise of oil burns eight days
- First born of Egypt die
- Who can stop Him?
- Nature is putty in His hands

---

### When G-d Throws the Curveball

**The Scenario:**
- Jews in crisis
- Call to the bullpen
- Pray and fast
- Call on G-d for relief pitching
- G-d comes trotting in
- But refuses to throw fastball
- Refuses to bail us out

**Appearance:**
- Sluggers line up opposite side
- Swing away at will
- Appear to be in control
- Hitting long hard drives
- Flying over wall
- Just another inning or two
- Game will be lost
- G-d's children going down

**Then:**
> "Then in the bottom of the ninth, when you least expect it, G-d starts throwing curve balls and strikes out the side."

---

### What It Looks Like

**When G-d Overrules Nature:**
- Bread falls from sky
- You know He's in charge
- Clear divine intervention
- No question who's controlling

**When G-d Lets Nature Run:**
- Allows world's bullies upper-hand
- Seems nature slipped from control
- Enemies appear winning
- We appear losing
- To all intents and purposes
- As if He let go

---

## The Mystery of the Curveball

### Author's Fascination

**Recall Opening:**
> "Remember what I wrote at the beginning of this essay? I am fascinated by the curveball because the pitcher controls the ball though it is no longer in his hands?"

---

### G-d Does the Same

**With Nature:**
- Rather than taking control of patterns
- Lets it run loose
- But astoundingly
- Remains in control
- Even after He lets go

**The Mystery:**
> "In some inexplicable way, the randomness of nature ends up delivering the same result that G-d would have delivered had He intervened directly."

---

### Enemies Scratching Heads

**Their Confusion:**
- How did He do that?
- We had this one
- He wasn't interfering
- He had let go
- And He still won out
- How did He control what He already released?

---

### Historical Examples

**Nazi Germany:**
- Record incredible victories
- But allies discover bomb first

**Stalin:**
- Plan of annihilation against Soviet Jews
- Days before carrying out
- Stalin up and dies

**1948 Israel:**
- Five Arab armies converge
- Yet manage to lose the war

**The Question:**
> "How does G-d pull of these seemingly random conclusions?"

---

## Two Types of Miracles

### The Fastball Miracle

**Characteristics:**
- Bends nature to His will
- Doesn't surprise us
- Open intervention
- Direct control
- Hard hitting
- Everyone expects it
- No one can stop it

**Example:**
- Splitting of Red Sea
- Manna from heaven
- Clear supernatural events

---

### The Curveball Miracle

**Characteristics:**
- Hidden in nature
- Takes you by surprise
- Fast dropping
- Comes in through natural means
- Strikes out anyway
- Takes breath away
- By far more impressive

**Why More Impressive:**
> "When random patterns of nature save us from annihilation each time we are threatened, we are definitely surprised."

---

### The Greater Recognition

**When Nature Expresses G-d's Will:**
- We recognize no daylight
- Between G-d and His nature
- Unity revealed
- Hidden becomes manifest

**Comparison:**
> "The miracle that overrules nature is the hard hitting fastball – every one expects it and no one can stop it. The miracle hidden in nature is the fast dropping curveball. It takes you by surprise. It drops down to earth and strikes you out anyway. It takes your breath away and is by far the more impressive miracle."

---

### The Winning Pitch

**Final Assessment:**
> "The pitch that wins the game."

**Implication:**
- Curveball more effective
- More complete victory
- Demonstrates greater control
- Reveals deeper truth
- Nature itself expressing divine will
