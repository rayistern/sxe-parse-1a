## Music Coordination Options for Car Menorah Parade

### Option 1: FM Radio Transmitter

**How It Works:**
1. Buy FM radio transmitter (eBay has them)
2. Everybody can tune in to the same station in their cars
3. If you want loudspeakers: Get MP3 player with radio and play it from there

**Benefits:**
- Synchronized music
- All cars on same frequency
- Professional sound

---

### Option 2: Internet Streaming

**Requirements:**
Good cell service throughout the route

**How It Works:**
1. Create internet streaming feed (QuickTime live streaming is one way)
2. Give people the link
3. They listen on their smartphones
4. For loudspeakers: Connect smartphone audio to loudspeaker input jack

**Benefits:**
- Works with smartphones
- Can add loudspeakers
- Modern solution

**Considerations:**
- Requires reliable cell service
- Data usage
- Potential connectivity issues

---

### Option 3: Burn CDs and Distribute

**How It Works:**
1. Burn CDs
2. Hand them out
3. They won't be in sync
4. But everybody in their car will hear the music loud and clear

**Benefits:**
- Simple
- Reliable
- No technology issues
- Everyone hears music

**Tradeoff:**
- Not synchronized
- Less professional
