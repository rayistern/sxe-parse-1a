## Template 1: Passover Seder Project Thank You

### Structure and Components

---

#### Salutation

> "Dear Mr. & Mrs. [REDACTED]:"

**Note:** Personalized with formal titles

---

#### Acknowledgment with Specific Amount

> "Thank you for your recent contribution to Chabad of NW Metro Denver of **four hundred dollars**."

**Key Element:** State exact amount (spelled out, not just $400)

**Why:**
- Shows attention to detail
- Confirms correct recording
- More personal than number

---

#### General Mission Statement

> "Your contribution will continue to help further a vibrant Jewish identity and awareness throughout our community."

**Purpose:**
- Broad impact statement
- Ongoing nature emphasized
- Community-wide benefit

---

#### Specific Holiday Impact

> "This past Passover we reached hundreds of community members, with special hand-baked matzah and complete Seder Packages of matzah, wine/grape juice and a delicious holiday meal that we delivered to home-bound seniors and nursing home residents as part of the Seder Project."

**Details Included:**
- Specific holiday
- Quantified reach ("hundreds")
- Exact items provided
- Target population (homebound, seniors, nursing homes)
- Program name ("Seder Project")
- Reference to enclosure

---

#### Additional Program Details

> "In addition, we hosted two community seders with limited participants to comply with Covid guidelines."

**Shows:**
- Adaptation to circumstances
- Safety consciousness
- Multiple program components
- Beyond distribution

---

#### Impact Statement with Religious Context

> "Please know that as a result of your partnership with Chabad of NW Metro Denver, the words of the Hagadah 'whoever is hungry let them come and eat' were fulfilled, with many community members enjoying Passover food of both physical and spiritual nourishment."

**Elements:**
- "Partnership" language (not just donor)
- Quote from Hagadah
- "Fulfilled" - completed religious obligation
- Physical and spiritual dimensions

---

#### Blessing for Donor

> "In merit of your generosity may you and yours be granted the blessings of good health, happiness and prosperity."

**Traditional Jewish format:**
- Merit-based
- Comprehensive blessings
- Includes family ("you and yours")

---

#### Closing

> "With warm regards,
> [SIGNATURE]"

---

### Template Analysis

**Tone:** Professional yet warm

**Length:** Medium (single page)

**Focus:** Specific impact with religious framing

**Donor Stewardship Elements:**
1. Personal salutation
2. Specific amount acknowledgment
3. Program description
4. Impact quantification
5. Religious context
6. Blessing
7. Enclosure reference

## Template 2: Purim/Passover Combined Thank You

### Structure and Components

---

#### Opening Acknowledgment

> "On behalf of the Chabad Center of Sudbury I would like to express my thanks and appreciation for your generous contribution/s towards the work of the center."

**Note:**
- "Contribution/s" (plural) suggests multiple gifts
- Organization named
- Formal appreciation

---

#### Context Setting

> "During these challenging times when so much has changed and many are dealing with personal hardship, your kind support has helped make a real difference to our efforts at providing support, meaning and inspiration to so many."

**Elements:**
- Acknowledges difficult period (COVID)
- Donor support as especially meaningful
- Broad impact statement
- "So many" quantification

---

#### Purim Program Details

> "In recent months with the support of people like yourself we were able to hand deliver joy to many hundreds of homes in the greater Sudbury area. This included **over three hundred Purim packages** that were hand delivered before the holiday and helped bring an added dose of joy to the holiday."

**Specifics:**
- Timeline ("recent months")
- Geographic scope ("greater Sudbury area")
- Quantified: 300+ Purim packages
- "Hand delivered" emphasized
- Emotional impact ("joy")

---

#### Passover Program Details

> "Likewise for Passover we delivered **almost two hundred and fifty Passover care packages** that were hand delivered with some extra care and a personal touch and **sixty Passover Catered Dinners** that were brought to the homes of homebound seniors or others who it made a difference to."

**Specifics:**
- 250 Passover care packages
- 60 catered dinners
- Target: homebound seniors
- "Personal touch" emphasized
- Impact language

---

#### Testimonial

> "In the words of one recipient, 'as a senior living alone, I had no way to have a Seder this year, until your thoughtful and beautiful Passover Seder Dinner was brought to my door, and enabled me to celebrate the joy of this holiday'."

**Power of testimonial:**
- First-person voice
- Specific need addressed
- Emotional impact
- Direct connection to donor

---

#### Additional Programming

> "In addition, in recent months we have continued to hold many socially distant programs and services for children, adults and families, as well as a wide range of Adult Ed activities via Zoom."

**Shows:**
- Beyond holiday programs
- Adaptation to COVID
- Multi-generational
- Educational programming
- Technology adoption

**Bottom Line:**

Two professional thank you letter templates for holiday program donors demonstrating effective donor stewardship. Template 1 (Passover-focused) is concise single-holiday acknowledgment stating specific amount ("four hundred dollars" spelled out), describing Seder Project impact ("hundreds of community members" received "hand-baked matzah and complete Seder Packages" delivered to "homebound seniors and nursing home residents"), plus two community seders with COVID safety, connecting to Hagadah quote "whoever is hungry let them come and eat" fulfilled through "physical and spiritual nourishment," closing with traditional Jewish blessing "in merit of your generosity may you and yours be granted...good health, happiness and prosperity." Template 2 (multi-program) covers Purim and Passover comprehensively: "over three hundred Purim packages" plus "almost two hundred and fifty Passover care packages" and "sixty Passover Catered Dinners" for homebound, includes powerful recipient testimonial ("as a senior living alone, I had no way to have a Seder this year until your...Passover Seder Dinner was brought to my door"), mentions additional socially-distant programming and Zoom education during COVID. Key elements both demonstrate: specific quantification, "hand delivered" emphasis, religious/mission framing, "partnership" language (not just donor), impact stories, warm yet professional tone. Best practices: spell out dollar amounts, include testimonials, quantify reach, acknowledge context, express genuine gratitude, connect to mission, reference enclosures. Template 1 better for single holiday, higher-dollar donors, religious audience, formal relationship; Template 2 better for multiple programs, general donor base, impact emphasis, community-building.
