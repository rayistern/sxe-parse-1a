---

## How It Works

### The Concept

**Process:**
1. Organization sends member list to every member
2. Each member selects recipients for Purim wishes
3. Organization receives donation for each selection
4. Software auto-compiles unique letters to each family
5. Letters indicate which families sent blessings
6. Organization prepares mishloach manot baskets (one per family)
7. Baskets delivered with personalized letters

---

**Why It Works:**

### Reciprocity Feature

**Smart Automation:**
- Tracks who sent to whom
- Auto-adds reciprocal sends
- Prevents awkward situations
- "Forgot someone" safety net

**Example:**
- You select 20 people
- 5 additional people select you
- System auto-adds those 5 to your list (at $8 each)
- Everyone who sent to you receives from you

**Bottom Line:**

Purim Project (www.purimproject.com) is web-based fundraising software enabling Chabad centers to run automated mishloach manot campaigns with significant revenue potential while fulfilling mitzvah and building community. System works by giving each member unique login to select recipients from community list, organization receives donation per selection ($7 basic, $8 with reciprocity auto-add, $20 for non-list members, $250 package deal for unlimited selections of people already receiving), software auto-compiles personalized letters showing which families sent blessings, organization prepares one basket per family delivered with letter. New Orleans example demonstrates professional implementation with clear email template explaining concept, login instructions, pricing tiers, payment methods, Feb 27 deadline, volunteer recruitment call. Revenue potential substantial: 200 families × 15 average selections × $7 = $21,000 gross, minus ~$2,000 basket costs = $19,000 net, plus upsells. Key features include reciprocity safety net (auto-adds anyone who selected you so "even if you have forgotten to include someone, we will do it for you"), out-of-town inclusion (receive card instead of basket), customizable greetings, secure payment processing. Free for first-time users, integrated with ChabadOne sites. Benefits beyond fundraising: makes mitzvah easy, removes logistics burden, connects community members, shows appreciation, inclusive approach. Operational needs: volunteer packing event and delivery drivers.
