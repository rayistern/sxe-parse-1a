## Whole Foods Latke Taste Testing

### Event Overview

A national Whole Foods initiative that provides opportunities for kosher Chanukah food tastings at their stores across the US.

**Event Timing:**
- Saturdays and Sundays throughout November and December
- Nationally advertised by Whole Foods
- Same day and time at every Whole Foods Market store in the US

**Partnership Benefits:**
- New customer traffic due to Whole Foods/Amazon partnership
- National advertising support
- Store traffic and visibility

---

### How It Works

**Whole Foods Partnership:**

From Whole Foods contact:
> "We are hosting a holiday tasting on the same day and time at every Whole Foods Market store in the US, so these dates will be nationally advertised. We expect to see a lot of new customers due to advertising and our new partnership with Amazon."

---

### Logistics

**Food Preparation:**

**Option 1: Home Preparation (Current Model)**
- Prepare all foods at home
- Bring finished product to store
- Keep latkes hot in sternos
- Professional presentation

**Option 2: Store Preparation**
- Check with your local store manager
- May vary by location

**Cost Coverage:**
- Whole Foods pays for all ingredients
- Ingredients must be bought in their store
- Usually $100 or so worth of goods
- Bring receipt to customer service desk
- Store rings up on Whole Foods card

---

### Setup Process

**1. Initial Contact:**
- Reach out to local Whole Foods store manager
- Explain the national holiday tasting program
- Mention kosher foods for Chanukah
- Request partnership

**2. Shopping Coordination:**
- Let store know when you plan to shop
- Purchase all ingredients at Whole Foods
- Bring items to customer service desk
- Store charges to Whole Foods account

**3. Event Day:**
- Arrive with prepared latkes
- Set up tasting station
- Use sternos to keep latkes hot
- Engage shoppers
- Share Chanukah message

---

### Key Questions & Answers

**Q: They allow you to bring in food cooked outside?**
A: Yes, check with your local store manager to confirm their policy.

**Q: They pay for said food?**
A: Yes, Whole Foods pays for ingredients purchased in their store (typically ~$100 budget).

**Q: This is organized with your local store manager?**
A: Yes, coordinate directly with your local Whole Foods store manager.

## Latke Preparation Tips

### Baking Frozen Latkes (Large Quantities)

**For Trader Joe's Frozen Latkes:**

**Setup:**
- Stack latkes (while still frozen) side by side
- Use double-sized pan
- Fits around 110 latkes in each pan

**Baking Instructions:**
1. Cover and bake at 375°F for about 2 hours
2. Uncover and bake for about 1 hour more
3. Latkes should be crispy and golden

**Advantages:**
- Can prepare large quantities
- No active frying required
- Easier cleanup
- Consistent results

---

### Fresh Frying at Events

**Outdoor/Onsite Frying:**

**Equipment:**
- Camping grill (double flame with wind guard)
- Griddle (sold in outdoor items area)
- Propane or fuel source

**Why This Works:**
> "We bought a camping grill (double flame with a wind guard) - they also sold a griddle in the same outdoor items area. We brought it to the menorah lighting and fried fresh latkes. The electric ones are not really hot enough."

**Benefits:**
- Fresh, hot latkes
- Theatrical element (people watch frying)
- Delicious aroma attracts crowd
- Authentic experience

**Safety Considerations:**
- Adequate ventilation
- Safe distance from crowds
- Fire extinguisher on hand
- Responsible supervision
- Weather considerations

## Troubleshooting

### Common Questions

**Q: What if the store says no to home-cooked food?**
A: Ask if you can use their kitchen facilities or prepare on-site with camping equipment outside.

**Q: What if they won't cover costs?**
A: Seek sponsorship from community members or use your Chabad House budget - the outreach value is worth it.

**Q: How many latkes should I prepare?**
A: For a 3-4 hour event, plan 200-300 latkes (1-2 per taster).

**Q: What if I don't have a 501(c)(3)?**
A: Work under your parent Chabad organization's 501(c)(3) if available.
