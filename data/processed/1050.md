## Complete Poem

```
B"H

                                          Purim and Havdalah

At the conclusion of Shabbat in the Havdalah,
We say a verse from Queen Esther's Megillah:
"To the Jews there was light, joy, gladness and glory",
What does Havdalah have to do with the Purim story?

Light's Torah, joy's festivals, gladness-bris, and glory's tefillin.
Why these specific mitzvot? - the blanks I'll try to fill in.

Haman had no issue with our spirituality,
What bothered him was our materiality.
What makes you Jews different from us? his claim,
Our bodies and activities as yours are the same!

We both value intellect, celebrate holidays fine,
Do circumcisions and recognize a sacred sign.
But he banned these mitzvot knowing they embody -
The intrinsic specialness of a Jew's body.

They display our G-dly bond, that transcends rationality,
By connecting through faith, we elevate even physicality.

So, as we enter a new week at each Shabbat's end,
We recall our uniqueness once again and again.
Our bodies are holy, just as our souls,
We must uphold this always, set lofty goals.

When imbuing G-dliness into ALL that we do,
We'll experience true joy - Happy Purim to you!

 [REDACTED-NAME] and [REDACTED-NAME]
```

## Content Analysis

### Opening Connection

**The Question:**
> "At the conclusion of Shabbat in the Havdalah,
> We say a verse from Queen Esther's Megillah"

**Verse Referenced:**
"LaYehudim Haysa Ora V'Simcha V'Sasson Vikar"
(To the Jews there was light, joy, gladness and glory)

**Initial Puzzle:**
What connects Havdalah ceremony to Purim story?

---

### The Answer - Four Mitzvot

**Rabbinic Interpretation:**

1. **Light** = Torah study
2. **Joy** = Festivals
3. **Gladness** = Bris (circumcision)
4. **Glory** = Tefillin

**Why These Four?**
The poem explains their special significance

---

### Deep Explanation

#### Haman's Real Issue

**Not Spirituality:**
> "Haman had no issue with our spirituality,
> What bothered him was our materiality."

**His Claim:**
> "What makes you Jews different from us? his claim,
> Our bodies and activities as yours are the same!"

**Universal Elements:**
- Intellect (Torah study)
- Holidays (festivals)
- Circumcision (sign in flesh)
- Sacred signs (tefillin)

**Everyone does these** - so what's special?

---

#### The Jewish Difference

**Physical Mitzvot:**
> "But he banned these mitzvot knowing they embody -
> The intrinsic specialness of a Jew's body."

**Key Concept:**
These four mitzvot specifically involve the **physical body**:

1. **Torah** - studying with physical faculties
2. **Festivals** - physical celebration
3. **Bris** - permanent mark on body
4. **Tefillin** - worn on body (head and arm)

---

### The Deeper Message

**Transcendent Connection:**
> "They display our G-dly bond, that transcends rationality,
> By connecting through faith, we elevate even physicality."

**Not Just Spiritual:**
- Jewish souls are holy
- Jewish **bodies** are holy too
- Physical acts have spiritual meaning
- Material elevated to Divine

---

### Application to Weekly Life

**Havdalah Reminder:**
> "So, as we enter a new week at each Shabbat's end,
> We recall our uniqueness once again and again."

**Weekly Reset:**
- Shabbat concludes
- New week begins
- Remember who we are
- Carry holiness forward

**Personal Application:**
> "Our bodies are holy, just as our souls,
> We must uphold this always, set lofty goals."

---

### Conclusion

**Practical Living:**
> "When imbuing G-dliness into ALL that we do,
> We'll experience true joy - Happy Purim to you!"

**Key Teaching:**
- Not just spiritual activities
- **ALL** actions can be holy
- Physical + spiritual = complete
- This brings true joy
