## Elegant Fundraising Through Candle Sponsorship

**Concept:** Honor 8 individuals/couples with lighting candles, $1,000 sponsorship each

**Experience:** "Remarkably successful" - could be used by Shluchim in many ways

```
December 5, 2006
14 Kislev, 5767

Dr. Ross & Dani Clevens

Dear Dr. Ross & Dani,

As you have probably already heard, we are planning a sensational program that will put Chanukah in Brevard this year on a different plateau. This event, sponsored by Chabad of the Space & Treasure Coasts is open to the entire community and promises to be a dynamic evening of first class entertainment as well as a great way to celebrate the fourth night of Chanukah for the whole family.

World renowned Latino-Chassidic-Rock Sensation Yehudah Glantz from Israel will perform Live in Concert at the King Center for The Performing Arts Monday, December, 18 2006. I will be calling you shortly about this. We hope you will accept this honor and assist in bringing more light into our community.

Your sponsorship will make this Chanukah so special and wonderful; for so many. Your contribution will enable us to bring joy, and spiritual warmth to Jews throughout Brevard and Indian River Counties.

[Signature]
```

## Implementation Guide

### Step 1: Select Prospects (Week 1)

**Identify 15-20 prospects for 8 spots:**
- Major donors
- Community leaders
- Successful professionals
- Those who value recognition
- Can afford $1,000
- Will attend event

**Why 15-20 for 8:**
- Not everyone will say yes
- Some not available that night
- Over-subscription is fine (add candles!)
- Competition for spots

---

### Step 2: Send Letters (Week 2)

**Personalize Each:**
- Hand-address envelope
- Real signature
- Personal salutation
- Date it

**Include:**
- Event details
- The honor
- The ask
- Response method
- Follow-up notice

---

### Step 3: Follow-Up Calls (Week 3)

**As Promised:**
> "I will be calling you shortly about this"

**Script:**
"Hi [Name], Rabbi [X] calling. I wanted to follow up on the letter I sent about our Chanukah event. Did you have a chance to review it? We'd be honored to have you and [spouse] light one of the candles..."

**Be Ready For:**
- "I need to think about it" → "I understand. Can I answer any questions?"
- "That's a lot" → "It includes 10 tickets, and you'll be honored on stage"
- "Who else is doing this?" → Name 2-3 who committed
- "I'll get back to you" → "Great! I'll check in [specific day]"

---

### Step 4: Write Grammen (Week 4-5)

**For Each Sponsor:**
- Research them
- Find unique angle
- Personal compliment
- Connection to Chanukah theme
- Rhyme with their name (challenging!)

**Tips:**
- Start early (takes time)
- Keep meter consistent
- Test reading aloud
- Make sure names clear
- Practice pronunciation

---

### Step 5: Prepare Ceremony (Week 6)

**Logistics:**
- Choose girl to read grammen
- Practice reading (emotion, timing)
- Choose girl for candle table
- Set up table with white cloth
- Have candles ready
- Test sound system
- Cue music tracks
- Practice lighting timing
- Have printed grammen as backup

---

### Step 6: Execute (Event Night)

**Before Ceremony:**
- Greet all sponsors
- Confirm they're present
- Brief them on process
- "When your name called, come to stage"
- Make sure they feel special

**During Ceremony:**
- Clear audio
- Good lighting
- Spotlight on stage
- Music cued and ready
- Smooth transitions

**After:**
- Thank them personally
- Photo opportunities
- Newspaper/photographer get shots
- Recognition in program

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue

**8 Sponsors at $1,000:** $8,000

**If Oversubscribed (13 sponsors as in example):** $13,000

---

### Costs Against This

**10 Tickets per Sponsor:**
- 8 sponsors × 10 tickets = 80 tickets
- If tickets are $20: $1,600 in lost revenue
- But tickets to your own event = minimal real cost

**Candles/Setup:**
- Minimal
- Candles: $50
- Table setup: nominal
- Printing grammen: $10

**Staff Time:**
- Writing grammen: significant but one-time
- Recruiting sponsors: would do anyway
- Setup: minimal incremental

---

### Net Impact

**Financial:**
- $8,000-13,000 revenue
- Minus ~$2,000 in ticket value
- Minus ~$100 in direct costs
- Net: $6,000-11,000

**Plus Intangibles:**
- Donor cultivation
- Prestige of event
- Community buzz
- Future major gifts
- Those who wished they sponsored

**Bottom Line:**
This elegant 8-candle sponsorship program raised $13,000+ for Chanukah concert by honoring donors with stage candle-lighting ceremony featuring personalized rhyming grammen, spotlight, music ("There's a Light" and "Candles"), and refined presentation with young girl elegantly handing candles from white-tableclothed table. The result: "couples felt like a million bucks...walked like going down aisle to their wedding" and crucially "first time balebatim who didn't respond really felt left out"—creating powerful FOMO for future years. The winning invitation letter frames $1,000 as "sponsorship" (not donation) honoring them as one of "8 friends representing Chanukah candles" plus 10 complimentary concert tickets and "merit of great mitzvah." Grammen structure: 4-6 rhyming lines connecting Chanukah themes (Maccabees, light from darkness, miracles) to personal compliments, ending with couple's names and call to stage. Success psychology: scarcity (only 8 spots), public recognition, fair value exchange ($1,000 = honor + tickets + recognition), social proof (prominent community members), and professional execution (music, spotlight, elegant touches). Implementation: identify 15-20 prospects for 8 spots, send personalized letters, follow-up calls as promised, write tailored grammen (most time-consuming), practice ceremony, execute flawlessly. Adaptable: 4 candles at $500 for smaller events, 8 at $1,800 (chai) for established communities, or multiple nights with 8 sponsors each. Net revenue $6-11K after ticket value, plus intangible donor cultivation. Complete materials package with sample letters, full grammen, audio tracks available on Shluchim Exchange.
