## Assembly Strategy

### Efficient Production

**Assembly Line:**
- Station 1: Lay out packaging
- Station 2: Add tissue/filler
- Station 3: Place main items
- Station 4: Add treats
- Station 5: Close/seal
- Station 6: Add card/ribbon
- Station 7: Quality check

**Volunteers:**
- Recruit helpers
- Pre-sort all items
- Clear instructions
- Music/refreshments
- Make it social event

**Timeline:**
- Start 2-3 weeks before Purim
- Order supplies 4 weeks out
- Assembly party 1 week before
- Delivery day of/day before
