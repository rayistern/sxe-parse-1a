## Menorah Parade Planning and Ideas

### The Challenge

It's important that it be clear to anyone watching the parade what this is. You may have far more cars than you have menorahs, and while it's wonderful to involve as many people as possible, if you're not careful it can **look like a funeral procession rather than a parade**.

### The Solution

To avoid this—and to ensure the maximum pirsumei nissa—you can decorate every car in the parade, even if they don't have a menorah.

---

## Budget-Friendly Enhancement Options

**Cheaper than the $225-$400 that a car-top menorah typically costs:**

### 1. Magnets

See the Car Menorah page for options. You can print dozens of magnets inexpensively or buy them from Shluchim Services and they can be easily attached and detached from cars.

---

### 2. Balloons

Chanukah-themed balloons—or any balloons—can be inflated with helium and left to trail out of windows.

---

### 3. Flags

Chanukah flags are available for $5 each and install easily on windows.

---

### 4. Music

**CRITICAL: This is a wonderful addition, but MUST BE DONE CORRECTLY!**

**The Problem:**
If every car is blasting its own Chanukah tunes, the result is a cacophony. And if left to their own devices, many participants may play music unsuitable for a Chabad menorah parade.

**The Solution:**
Get an FM transmitter (option on Amazon with 1000-foot range) and share the channel frequency with attendees. This allows everyone to play the same music from their cars—the music you choose.

---

### 5. Police Escort

This is important for:
- Security
- Cohesiveness
- Adds a look of "officialness" to the parade

---

### 6. Fire Truck

Ask your local fire department if they can join the parade.

**Benefits:**
- Fire truck leading brings visibility
- Kid favorite!

---

### 7. Inflatable Dreidel

This can be added to the parade if cars will be consistently going slowly.

---

### 8. Giant Menorahs on Pickups

Giant 6-foot menorahs can be strapped to the flatbed of pickup trucks for extra visibility.

**Safety:** Add sandbags to the base for extra stability.
