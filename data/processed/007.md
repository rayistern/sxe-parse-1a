### Press Release Content

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

The Chabad Jewish Center welcomes the entire community to celebrate the Festival of Lights at the annual public Menorah lighting ceremony. The Chanukah celebration will take place on Tuesday, December 15th at 5:30 PM (the fifth night of Chanukah) at the Needham Town Common.

Beginning at 5:30 PM, attendees will be able to watch the Menorah being carved out of hundreds of pounds of ice. Following the kindling, the program will feature live music, dancing, donuts, hot chocolate and more.

#### The Universal Message

Chanukah propagates the universal message that ultimately:
- Good will prevail over evil
- Freedom over oppression
- Light over darkness

For many, Chanukah brings back fond memories of childhood years and serves to renew a sense of Jewish identity. The Chanukah lights provide us with warmth, joy, strength and inspiration. Such is the purpose of a community-wide celebration to be held at the Needham Town Common.

#### Global Outreach

In its Chanukah outreach campaign, the Chabad Jewish Center joins thousands of Chabad Lubavitch centers across the globe staging similar public displays of the Menorah and its symbolic lights.

**Locations Include:**
- Australia to Africa
- Colombia to Hong Kong
- New York City to the White House lawn
- Hundreds of thousands will experience the joy of Chanukah with Chabad Lubavitch

## Implementation Tips

### Menorah Types by Event Size

**Small Events (50-100 people):**
- Candy/Jelly Bean Menorah
- LEGO Menorah
- Clics Menorah

**Medium Events (100-300 people):**
- Balloon Menorah
- Can Menorah
- Penny/Coin Menorah

**Large Events (300+ people):**
- Ice Menorah (dramatic visual impact)
- Giant LEGO Menorah

### Planning Considerations

**Ice Menorah:**
- Requires professional ice sculptor or DIY carving skills
- Need ice blocks (hundreds of pounds)
- Weather considerations (temperature must be below freezing)
- Lighting logistics (LED candles work best)
- Timing: carve on-site for dramatic effect

**LEGO Menorah:**
- Partner with local children's museum for LEGO loan
- Can be pre-built or built on-site as activity
- Durable and reusable
- Great for photo opportunities
- Kid-friendly

**Can Menorah:**
- Collect food cans in advance
- Can donate cans to food bank after event
- Community service angle
- Cost-effective
- Easy to assemble

**Candy Menorah:**
- Purchase bulk candy/gumballs
- Consider allergen-free options
- Visual appeal for kids
- Can give away candy after lighting
- Sweet theme tie-in

### Safety Notes

- All alternative menorahs should use **LED candles** or **electric lights**
- Do not use real flames with flammable materials
- Ensure stability of structure
- Consider wind/weather factors for outdoor displays
- Have backup lighting plan
