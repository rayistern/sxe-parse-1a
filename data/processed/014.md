## Before You Start

### Choosing the Right Location

**Location is paramount to the success of your Chanukah Wonderland.**

**Off-Site Benefits:**
If you can conduct your Chanukah Wonderland from a location other than your Chabad House, you may very well attract those who are:
- Wary of a Chabad House
- Concerned about orthodox religion
- Looking to do something to celebrate the season
- More comfortable in a neutral venue

**Layout Considerations:**

When evaluating a location, assess:
- Does the room provide easy ways to cordon areas for specific stations?
- Will you need room dividers?
- Are there sufficient electric outlets in reasonable locations?
- How many stations/activities can fit based on space?
- What's the anticipated traffic/attendance?

---

### Insurance Requirements

**CRITICAL: Notify Your Insurance Company**

It is vital that you notify your insurance company that you are planning to hold an event of such a nature, whether in your Chabad House or at any other location.

**Why This Matters:**
- In case of any mishaps chas v'shalom
- They must be apprised prior to the event
- Covers liability issues
- Protects your organization

## Preparing for Chanukah Wonderland

### Timing Your Event

**Key Questions:**

**Duration:**
- How many days of Chanukah will you run the Wonderland?
- Full day or specific hours?
- Multiple shifts or continuous?

**Scheduling:**
- What else is scheduled in your community during these days?
- Are there conflicts to avoid?
- Best days for maximum attendance?

**Special Events:**
- Schedule special shows on particular days
- Chanukah Storytime
- Concerts
- Puppet shows
- Guest performers

**Advantages:**
- Highlights draw repeat visitors
- Can advertise specific attractions
- Builds buzz and anticipation

### Choosing Your Activities

**See detailed activity and craft lists below with instructions and shopping lists.**

**Selection Criteria:**

**1. Uniqueness:**
Choose activities and crafts that your crowd is least likely to have an opportunity to do elsewhere.

**Examples:**
- Olive press demonstration
- Candle making
- Glow-in-the-dark quest
- Life-size games
- Latke frying station

**2. Age Appropriateness:**
Choose activities and crafts appropriate for children up to age 12-13.

**Include:**
- Toddler section (see ideas below)

- Pre-teen challenges

### Safety Rules and Regulations

**CRITICAL SAFETY MEASURES:**

**Capacity:**
- Limit your crowd to maximum allowed capacity
- Monitor entrances
- Have plan for overflow

**First Aid:**
- Prepare complete First Aid Kit
- Know location of nearest hospital
- Have emergency contacts posted
- Designate first aid coordinator

**Electrical Safety:**
- Secure all electric wires to floor or walls
- Use cable covers
- Test all electrical equipment in advance
- Avoid overloading circuits

**Heat Activities:**
- Latke frying
- Candle making
- Any activity involving heat

**Requirements:**
- Properly set up
- Manned by competent, responsible adults
- Staff trained in emergency procedures
- Fire extinguisher accessible
- Clear safety zone

### 1. Latke Making

**You Will Need:**

**Equipment:**
- Sturdy table in safe area
- Large fryer
- Spatula
- Serving plate
- Paper towels
- Electric burner and outlet

**Food Preparation:**
- Food processor and electric outlet OR
- Plastic hand-held graters ($5.95 each at http://www.kitchenworksinc.com/PLASTIC-PYRAMID-GRATER__546721.aspx)
- Disposable food preparation gloves (Box of 500 for $4.47)
- Plates for volunteers to peel and grate
- 5 peelers
- Bowls/glasses to crack eggs
- Mixing bowl and spoon (if not using food processor)

**Ingredients:**
- Latke ingredients and recipe
- Potatoes
- Eggs
- Matzo meal
- Salt, pepper

**Safety:**
- Heavy duty plastic tablecloth
- Drop cloth or old carpet for floor protection
- Heavy duty plastic to protect wall near fryer from oil stains

---

**Setup Notes:**

IMPORTANT:
- Set up in safe area
- Far away from bowling balls, bean bags, running children
- Manned by very responsible volunteers
- Protect wall and floor around and under frying area from oil stains

---

**Activity Instructions:**

1. Choose 5 children to each peel and grate one potato (or one peels, another grates)
2. Choose one child to crack and check eggs
3. Have other volunteers add salt, pepper, matzo meal, and mix
4. Responsible adult fries the latkes

**Additional Notes:**
Great opportunity to discuss:
- Significance of eating latkes
- Oil miracle
- Other short Chanukah lessons

### 7. Glow in the Dark Quest for the Oil

**You Will Need:**

**Room Setup:**
- Small room that can be very well darkened
- Enough black fabric to cover all windows and walls
- White oaktag
- White construction paper or cardstock

**Lighting:**
- Black light that makes white and light-colored objects glow

**Optional Enhancement:**
600 8" Glow Light Stick Bracelets Wholesale Pack, 500 Connectors Included
$39.99, free shipping
http://www.amazon.com/Light-Bracelets-Wholesale-Connectors-Included/dp/B003U39PWI/ref=sr_1_18?s=toys-and-games&ie=UTF8&qid=1320787188&sr=1-18
(Smaller amounts also available)

**Audio:**
Sound track of Chanukah Story in Time Machine Format for Dreidel House
By Rabbi [REDACTED-NAME], Copenhagen, Denmark
http://www.shluchimexchange.com/en/article.html?h=1810&o=2092139

---

**Setup Notes:**

1. Prepare cutouts of variety of Chanukah and miscellaneous shapes from white oaktag
2. Include ONE flask of sealed olive oil
3. Hang up each white cutout throughout room
4. Cover each with white cardstock paper that can be lifted "flap-style"
5. Ensure olive oil cutout is in hard-to-find location
6. Darken room very well by covering all windows and walls with black fabric
7. Set up black light so room is completely dark and only white cardstock flaps can be seen

---

**Activity Instructions:**

1. Invite a number of children as a group to search for hidden flask of oil
2. Children can be provided with "Glow Light" necklaces, bracelets or other glow items for added effect
3. Winner receives a prize

### 12. "Feed the Latke" Race

**Contributed by:** Mrs. [REDACTED-NAME], Surrey, British Columbia, Canada

**You Will Need:**
- Two 8-10 foot tables set up to form a square
- 8 chairs (4 lined up along each of two opposing sides, facing each other)
- 4 long, thin wooden rods (long enough to reach across width of table, plus 1-2 spares)
- Masking tape
- Lots of plastic forks
- Lots of disposable plates
- Napkins
- Lots of latkes

---

**Setup Instructions:**

1. Set up 4 chairs along two opposing sides of table, facing each other
2. Set up plate with latke in front of each of 4 chairs along one side of table
   - Cut up for easier version
   - Whole for more challenging version
3. Tape plastic fork to one end of each wooden rod, creating 4-5 foot long fork

---

**Activity Instructions:**

1. Four sets of partners compete against each other
2. Goal: First team to successfully have one partner feed the other a latke across table
3. Each pair sits opposite each other on either side of wide table
4. Team Member #1 equipped with 4-5 foot long fork
5. Team Member #1 charged with (cutting and) feeding latke sitting in front of Team Member #2
6. Team Member #2 holds hands behind back
7. Booth manager announces start, cheers contestants, declares winner
8. Winning pair receives prize
9. All contestants receive chocolate Chanukah gelt

### 1. Chanukah Candle Making

**You Will Need:**

**Materials:**
- Wax pellets (order more than you think - gets used quickly, stores for next year)
- Wax dye
- Wax crock pots (1-5 depending on crowd size, allows variety of colors)
- Wicks

**Equipment:**
- Sturdy table near electric outlet
- Extra durable thick plastic tablecloth
- Drop cloths or floor protection, secured to floor
- Wire hangers (like from dry cleaners) - enough for each person plus extra
- Scissors to cut wicks
- Buckets of cold water

**Take-Home:**
- Chanukah Souvenir Bags or other bag to hold candles

---

**Craft Instructions:**

1. Tie one wick to bottom wire of clothes hanger
2. Dip wick into wax for 3 seconds
3. Dip into cold water for 2 seconds
4. Alternate in this manner until candle meets desired thickness
5. Pull bottom of candle to straighten as necessary

---

**Additional Notes:**

**Engraving:**
Provide skewers to engrave Chanukah images or words onto completed candle immediately after final dip

**Glitter Options:**
- Add fine glitter to the wax, OR
- Roll candles in glitter immediately following final dip

**Age Restriction:**
You may want to limit this activity to children above age 7 unless accompanied by an adult.
