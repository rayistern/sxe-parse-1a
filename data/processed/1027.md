# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Purim Story "Right/Left" Pass-the-Gift Game

## Note: Purim Content (Not Chanukah)

**Game Type:** Interactive storytelling with physical passing

**Activity:** Pass gifts right/left based on story cues

**Duration:** 5-7 minutes

**Participants:** All ages, seated in circle or at tables

**Materials Needed:** Small wrapped gifts (mishloach manot packages), charity boxes, coins

---

## Complete Script

### Opening

> "In the story of Purim, King Achashveros felt it **RIGHT**, to appoint Haman as his Prime Minister."

**Action:** Pass gift to person on your RIGHT

---

> "Now, Haman felt he had the **RIGHT** to demand, that all people, must bow down to him. So, as he walked the streets all the people on the **RIGHT** and **LEFT** bowed down, All, except for one Jew! There was one who was **LEFT** out from the Crowd. His name was....... Mordeichai"

**Actions:**
- RIGHT → pass right
- LEFT → pass left
- LEFT → pass left again
- LEFT → pass left again

---

### Haman's Anger

> "When Haman saw this he would get MAD, **RIGHT** away!"

**Action:** Pass RIGHT

---

> "Haman went **RIGHT** to King Achavshverosh and he said 'You should **RIGHT** a decree that the Jews had no **RIGHT** to exist at all!'"

**Actions:**
- RIGHT → pass right
- RIGHT (as in "write") → pass right
- RIGHT → pass right

---

### The Decree

> "Every Jew, **RIGHT** the way through the kingdom, was to be wiped out on the 13th of Adar. Men Women and children. Not a single Jew would be **LEFT**."

**Actions:**
- RIGHT → pass right
- LEFT → pass left

---

### The Messengers

> "Haman sent messengers, marching to deliver the decree.
> **(SLOW) RIGHT, LEFT,**
> **(SLOW) RIGHT, LEFT,**
> **(SLOW) RIGHT, LEFT, RIGHT!**"

**Actions:**
- Slow, deliberate passing
- RIGHT, LEFT alternating like marching
- Final RIGHT

**Performance Note:** Say this slowly with marching rhythm, allowing time to pass

---

### Mordechai's Response

> "This **LEFT** us Jews in a very difficult situation! Rabbi Mordeichai **LEFT** his home and went **RIGHT** to work."

**Actions:**
- LEFT → pass left
- LEFT → pass left
- RIGHT → pass right

---

> "He gathered all the Jews to pray and fast **RIGHT** away."

**Action:** Pass RIGHT

---

### Unity and Action

> "Once all the Jews, from the far **RIGHT**, to the far **LEFT** were united as one, Queen Esther went to speak to the King, and beg him to change his mind **RIGHT** away."

**Actions:**
- RIGHT → pass right
- LEFT → pass left
- RIGHT → pass right

---

### The Revelation

> "King Achesverosh was **RIGHT**fully surprised, when he heard that Queen Esther was a Jew. He didn't want to be **LEFT** without a queen. He hung Haman and his ten sons on the gallows **RIGHT** away."

**Actions:**
- RIGHT (rightfully) → pass right
- LEFT → pass left
- RIGHT → pass right

---

### The Victory

> "Excitement spread throughout the city. The Jews celebrated that they were **LEFT** in peace, with a new Holiday and customs to add to their calendar."

**Action:** Pass LEFT

---

### The Mitzvot

> "In order to increase in friendship and unity, today we will do those mitzvot **RIGHT** away."

**Action:** Pass RIGHT

---

## The Culmination - Mishloach Manot

### The Gift Exchange

> "Soooooooo take your **RIGHT** hand and give this gift of food to your person on your **LEFT**."

**Actions:**
1. Everyone stops passing
2. Use RIGHT hand
3. Give package to person on LEFT
4. Keep what you received!

---

### WAIT! The Twist

> "**WAIT!!**"

**Dramatic pause - everyone freezes**

---

## Matanot La'evyonim (Charity)

### The Charity Mitzvah

> "Now, on your table, there is a silver bag of coins. Please take two coins and put one in each charity box on your table. The content will go to people in need."

**Action:**
- Find charity boxes on table
- Take two coins from provided bag
- Place one coin in each box
- Fulfills mitzvah of matanot la'evyonim

---

### Closing

> "Thank you and have a happy PURIM"

---

## Setup Instructions

### Before the Game

**Seating:**
- Participants in circle
- Or at round tables
- Everyone needs a seat
- Clear left/right orientation

**Materials Preparation:**
- One small wrapped gift per person
- Gift could be:
  - Mishloach manot package
  - Small bag of treats
  - Hamantaschen
  - Small toy
  - Purim item

**Charity Setup:**
- Charity boxes (tzedakah boxes) on each table
- One silver bag per table
- Two coins per person in bag
- Clearly visible and accessible

---

## Performance Tips

### For the Leader

**Pacing:**
- Read clearly and slowly
- Emphasize RIGHT and LEFT
- Pause after each direction
- Watch that everyone passes
- Don't rush - allow time

**Energy:**
- Start enthusiastic
- Build excitement
- "WAIT!!" should be dramatic
- Keep momentum going

**Clarity:**
- Project your voice
- Enunciate RIGHT and LEFT
- Make eye contact
- Ensure comprehension

---

### Managing the Group

**Before Starting:**
> "Everyone will receive a gift. When I say RIGHT, pass to your right. When I say LEFT, pass to your left. Keep passing until I say STOP. Ready?"

**During:**
- Watch for confusion
- Help if someone stuck
- Keep energy high
- Encourage participation

**The Messengers Section:**
- Slow down significantly
- "SLOW" is a cue
- Marching rhythm
- Give time to pass

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"I'm confused about left and right!"**
- Pre-teach before starting
- Have everyone practice
- Point as you say it
- Assistants help

**"I didn't get what I started with!"**
- That's the point!
- Everyone ends with different gift
- Part of the fun
- Surprise element

**"Someone's hoarding gifts!"**
- Remind: one gift per person
- Keep passing when told
- Trust the process
- Address directly if needed

**"We don't have enough coins!"**
- Symbolic OK
- Actual donations can be made
- Provide more coins
- Or adjust activity
