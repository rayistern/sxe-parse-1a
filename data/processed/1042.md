## Shtetl Theme Complete

### Historical Context

**What Is a Shtetl:**
- Small Eastern European Jewish town/village
- Predominantly Jewish population
- Yiddish-speaking
- Pre-Holocaust communities
- Simple, traditional lifestyle
- Close-knit community
- Religious observance center

**Famous Shtetls:**
- Anatevka ("Fiddler on the Roof")
- Chelm (folklore tales)
- Various Polish, Ukrainian, Russian villages

---
