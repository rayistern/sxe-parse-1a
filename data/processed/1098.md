## Opening Examples

### Levi Yitzchok's Teaching

**The Comparison:**
> "Won't do Aveira Beshogeg if careful about chamets"

> "Levi yitzchok said after if the angle from shofer not good enough to bring brachas, then the cleaning and scrubbing for pesach should"

**Point:**
- If angel from shofar not perfect enough
- Then Pesach cleaning should certainly count
- Effort and kavanah matter
- Scrubbing has spiritual value

## The Haggadah Question

### What We Read

> "We read at Seder 'this matzah we eat cuz r father dough didn't rise, before hashem revealed himself and redeamed.'"

---

### Questions to Ask

**Question 1:**
> "We eat matza cuz dough didn't rise. So if dough would've risen we wouldn't eat matzah on pesach???"

**Seems incidental:**
- Because they rushing we eat matzah?
- Doesn't seem right
- Everything in Yiddishkeit has spiritual reason
- Why is Matzah so significant?

**Compare to Other Holidays:**
> "We don't say sukkos cuz ..."
- Other holidays have clear spiritual reasons
- Not presented as accidental
- Matzah seems different - why?

---

**Note:**
> "Matzah mitzvah is only the first night (first two in chutz laoretz) rest of pesach no obligation"

**Implication:**
- First night critical
- Rest of Pesach different status
- Timing significant

---

**Question 2:**
> "Reason eat it cuz didn't have time to rise, till hashem came to redeem them."

**Sequence Problem:**
> "We usually think it is hashem revealed then they packed and went not first they get ready then hashem reveal ??"

**Timeline Confusion:**
- Which came first?
- Did they pack, then Hashem revealed?
- Or Hashem revealed, then they packed?
- Affects understanding of matzah

---

**Question 3:**
> "What spiritual significant of chamatz and matzah. How it related back then and how it relates today"

**Seeking Connection:**
- Past and present
- Physical and spiritual
- Personal application

---

## Bread of Affliction Question

> "What we mean by saying 'this is the bread of affliction', this not that bread - might taste like it - but it's not it. So must b smtn the same about it"

**Analysis:**
- Not literally same bread
- But we say "this is"
- Something connects them
- What is it?

## Core Definitions

### Chometz = Ego

**Simple Equation:**
- Chometz represents ego
- Puffed up
- Inflated
- Self-importance

---

### Matzah = Submission

**Characteristics:**
- Letting go
- Being humble
- Submission
- Humility

---

## Pesach Main Idea

**What Pesach About:**
- Letting go
- Freedom
- Getting out of own restrictions
- Own perception
- Limitations put on self
- Something you can't do
- Can't reach
- Need to do
- Need to keep

---

### Defining Freedom

**Negative Definition:**
> "What is freedom? usually defined as negative, not slaved, not restricted, free."

**Positive Definition:**
> "Freedom is the act of reaching within ourselves, realizing who we r and not limiting ourselves to our outer part."

**Expanded:**
- Nothing wrong [with outer part]
- But we can do more

**Shabbos Parallel:**
> "Just like shabbos, the rest was an act of the 7th day, an act of itself, so too freedom is the act of looking within."

**Application:**
- Shabbos rest is active, not passive
- Freedom is active, not just absence of slavery
- Looking within is the act

---

## Going Out of Mitzrayim

> "Going out of mitzrayim, is setting free of limitations. We all prisoners but sit on key."

**Personal Application:**
> "Whatever ambission you have in yiddishkeit, spiritual growth, should b and can b conquered on pesach."

---

### True Celebration

**What Makes It Real:**
> "If food was hot, had matzah, not too much left over... Not celebrated properly, if you haven't left your mitzrayim, broken free from limitations."

**Not Just Physical:**
- Good food important
- But not the essence
- Must have spiritual liberation
- Must break your limitations

**Parenting Parallel:**
> "Just as in parenting not catigorize kids, the can b everything."

**Application:**
- Don't limit children
- They can be everything
- Same with ourselves
- Don't self-limit

---

## Step vs. Leap

**Step:**
- Still connected to previous space
- One foot on ground
- Gradual progression

**Leap:**
> "Leap both feet off the ground, into totally new place."

**Pesach Means:**
> "Pesach means to leap."

**Implication:**
- Complete transition
- Both feet off ground
- Totally new reality
- Not gradual growth

---

## Why Dangerous Place

### Danger #1: Amalek

> "Dangerous place to b, cuz what comes right after amalek"

**What is Amalek:**
> "Source of all negative traits? Ego."

**The Problem:**
> "When you make a real meaningful move, u feel very proud of yourself, without realizing that hashem helped u do it."

**Therefore:**
> "That y here at this time when moving forward in yiddishkeit, he goto have no chametz in mix"

**Application:**
- Making spiritual progress
- Natural to feel proud
- But that's ego (Amalek)
- Must avoid chometz (ego) at this time
- Chometz undermines the leap

---

### Danger #2: Foundation Stage

> "#2 when make a new platform of yiddishkeit, making new leap, new sense of spiritual self, need to have no ego in it, cuz it the foundation, you don't want chometz in that foundation."

**Why Critical:**
- Foundation determines everything
- New platform being built
- New sense of spiritual self
- Ego corrupts foundation
- Must be ego-free

---

## How Get Rid of Chometz?

### Two Ways

**Way #1: Our Effort**
> "Clean, search... Put in lots of your own effort"

**Physical Work:**
- Cleaning
- Searching
- Active labor
- Our responsibility

---

**Way #2: Revelation from Above**
> "Other way to get rid of chomotz, such a great revelation from above that you naturally humbled."

**What Happens:**
> "What happens pesach, OORigibaly pesach hashem was reveled in such a great way they were naturaly humbled."

**The Mechanism:**
> "The dough was unable to become chometz - not cuz they worked fast, it was unable to rise - no space for chometz, cuz hahsme was revelead so no scaope for ego"

**Why We Have Ego:**
> "We think we ego cuz hahsme hidden. When hashem revealed, so naturally inability to b chometz, no place for who."

**Original Pesach:**
- Hashem revealed so openly
- Naturally humbled
- Dough **unable** to rise
- Not because they hurried
- Because no space for ego
- Revelation left no room for ego

### What's "Ma"?

**The Word:**
> "What is ma - ability to loose self, to b totally submitted to hashem, just like chochma, is undefined, just a spark of idea"

**Purpose:**
> "Why we eat matzah on pesach, in order for us to achieve level ma, to loose sence of ego in front of hashem."

---

### Four Sons Connection

**Chacham's Question:**
> "When we have four sons, chochom ma hu omer.. They all have the ma, whee is the submission"

**Analysis:**
- All sons have "ma" in their question
- Where is the submission?
- Wise son: "ma" (what)
- Question as submission

---

### Ma Nishtana

> "Also by ma nishtana, shoel - bowwor. Here the son bowwor ability to bitul."

**The Youngest:**
- Asks Ma Nishtana
- Shoel (asks)
- Shows he's a bor (simple)
- But bor shows ability to bitul (nullification)
- Asking = admitting you don't know
- Not knowing = humility

---

### Matzah = Ma

> "MMAtzah. Ability to submit to hashem"

**Connection:**
- Matzah contains "Ma"
- Ma = submission
- Eating matzah = achieving ma
- Humility embodied

---

## First Night vs. Rest of Pesach

**First Night:**
> "Just like back then incredible g-dly revelation no ego, so too today first night goto have matzah, let go of ego, rest of pesach is own work."

**Why Difference:**
> "Only when building smtn new needs get rid of ego, but last day of pesach can and should eat gebrochtz."

**Explanation:**
> "Cuz hahsme blow mind, all overwhlend to hashem, but now u goto work on yourself and build yourself up piece by piece to b that level"

---

### First Week of Omer

> "First week of the omer, is foundational, chesed work through yourself to build foundation and now u can let bit of chometz - goto b healthy build can't b total nobody, need Jewish pride, work with your self."

**Progression:**
- First night: Divine revelation, no ego
- Foundation week: Building on that revelation
- After foundation: Need healthy self
- Can't be "total nobody"
- Need Jewish pride
- Chometz (in moderation) now okay

---

### Chometz Timing

> "CCHometz only threat at foundation. Pesach time is the leap, when making smtn totally new."

**Other Times:**
> "Rosh hashana, is more smtn when make leap."

**Application:**
- Foundation moments need no chometz
- Pesach = foundation of year (freedom)
- Rosh Hashana = foundation of year (judgment)
- Both are "leap" moments
- Chometz threatens these foundations
