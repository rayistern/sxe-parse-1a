### Storage Considerations

**How Much Refrigerator Space:**

**High Volume Items:**
- 30 eggs (2.5 cartons)
- 4 heads lettuce
- 45 zucchini and squash combined
- 30+ potatoes
- Multiple other vegetables

**Recommendation:**
- Start shopping 1 week before
- Items that keep: potatoes, onions, beetroots, squash, oranges, apples
- Buy 2-3 days before: lettuce, avocados, jicama
- Buy day before: herbs, delicate items

**Storage Tips:**
- Potatoes: Cool, dark place (not fridge)
- Onions: Cool, dry place (not with potatoes)
- Lettuce: Wash, dry, refrigerate in container
- Avocados: Buy at varying ripeness stages
- Eggs: Refrigerate immediately

### Prep Timeline

**1 Week Before:**
- Shop for non-perishables and sturdy produce
- Potatoes, onions, beetroots, squash, oranges, apples

**3 Days Before:**
- Shop for remaining produce
- Lettuce, avocados, jicama, peppers
- Eggs, canned goods

**2 Days Before:**
- Wash and prep vegetables that can be prepped
- Cook beetroots (3 hours!)
- Make any kugels that freeze well

**1 Day Before:**
- Finish vegetable prep
- Cook any dishes that improve overnight
- Roast beef, some kugels

**Day Of:**
- Final cooking
- Assembly
- Plating

---

### Quality Control

**Selecting Produce:**

**Beetroots:**
- Firm, smooth skin
- No soft spots
- Smaller ones cook faster

**Potatoes:**
- Firm, no green tinge
- No eyes or sprouts
- Even sizes for uniform cooking

**Lettuce:**
- Crisp, no wilting
- No brown edges
- Heavy for size (more moisture)

**Peppers:**
- Firm, glossy
- No wrinkles
- Heavy for size

**Avocados:**
- Buy at varying stages
- Some hard (for later)
- Some yielding (for soon)
- No dark soft spots

---

### Waste Reduction

**Using All Parts:**

**Lettuce:**
- Outer leaves: Soup or smoothies
- Cores: Compost or discard

**Carrots:**
- Tops: Make vegetable stock
- Peels: Stock or compost

**Onions:**
- Skins: Save for stock
- Scraps: Stock

**Celery:**
- Leaves: Add to soup/salad
- Ends: Stock

**Make Stock:**
- Save all vegetable scraps
- Boil with water, strain
- Freeze for later use

---

### Substitutions

**If Can't Find Certain Items:**

**Jicama:**
- Substitute: Water chestnuts, daikon radish, or crisp apple
- Or: Omit and increase other salad vegetables

**KLP Raisins:**
- Check if really need KLP (depends on your custom)
- Substitute: Other dried fruit if allowed

**Butternut Squash:**
- Substitute: Other winter squash (acorn, kabocha)
- Or: Sweet potatoes

**Red/Yellow Peppers:**
- Can use all one color
- Or mix with green peppers (less expensive)
