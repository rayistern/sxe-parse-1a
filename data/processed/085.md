**Bottom Line:**
This Glendale Galleria press release exemplifies successful mall partnership with powerful strategic elements: the branded "Giant 'Menorah of Freedom'" phrase (patriotic, universal), prominent mall partnership ("Glendale Galleria AND Chabad join"), mall-appropriate entertainment (clowns, balloons, live music), and crucial global context placing local event within worldwide campaign. The geographic sweep is strategic gold—"from Australia to Africa, Columbia to Hong Kong, New York City's ground zero to the White House lawn"—establishing legitimacy and mainstream acceptance. Ground Zero reference (2002, one year post-9/11) is emotionally powerful for freedom-over-oppression theme. White House lawn mention implies presidential blessing, making local officials comfortable participating. Three-part universal message (good over evil, freedom over oppression, light over darkness) transcends religion while rabbi's quote bridges ancient Temple rededication to modern "making this world better and brighter." For mall approach: emphasize foot traffic benefits, provide turnkey professional event, carry own insurance, time for maximum shoppers (4:30 PM Sunday), specify exact location using store landmarks ("Banana Court, front of Banana Republic"), and keep tone festive not overly religious. The global campaign framing—"thousands of Chabad centers," "hundreds of thousands participating"—transforms local menorah into participation in international movement, providing legitimacy that makes approvals easier and attendance higher.
