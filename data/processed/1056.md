**Description:** Tikkun format Megillah (practice text)

**What is a Tikkun?**
- Side-by-side format
- One side: text with vowels and cantillation marks
- Other side: text without vowels/marks (like actual scroll)
- Practice tool for ba'alei kriah
- Helps identify and correct reading errors
- Standard preparation method

**Use Case:**
- Ba'al koreh preparation
- Training new Megillah readers
- Self-practice and review
- Identify difficult words
- Master proper pronunciation
- Learn cantillation patterns

## For Ba'alei Kriah (Megillah Readers)

### Preparation Timeline

**30 Days Before:**
- Begin daily practice
- Use Tikkun for practice
- Focus on difficult sections
- Record yourself reading
- Listen back for errors

**2 Weeks Before:**
- Increase practice frequency
- Practice entire Megillah without stopping
- Time yourself
- Work on speed vs clarity balance
- Review all challenging words

**Week Before:**
- Daily full read-through
- Practice with actual kosher Megillah if possible
- Minimize looking at Tikkun
- Focus on stamina
- Vocal rest when not practicing

**Days Before:**
- Light practice only
- Vocal care (warm liquids, rest)
- Mental preparation
- Review plan for graggers/noise
- Confirm scroll is kosher

---

### Practice Tips

**Using the Tikkun:**
1. Read from side with marks first
2. Cover marked side
3. Read from plain side
4. Check against marked side
5. Repeat problem areas
6. Practice transitioning

**Common Challenges:**
- Similar names (confusing characters)
- Long sentences
- Repeated phrases
- Uncommon words
- Physical stamina (standing, reading aloud)
- Audience noise/graggers

**Solutions:**
- Slow down deliberately
- Mark scroll mentally
- Take brief pauses
- Stay hydrated
- Practice standing up
- Simulate noisy environment
