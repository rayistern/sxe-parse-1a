## Salad Dressings

### 1. Papaya Dressing

#### Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon papaya seeds
- 1/2 small red onion
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup oil

#### Instructions
Blend really well in food processor.

**Note:** Same as File 1132

---

### 2. Mango Dressing

#### Ingredients
- 1 mango
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- Salt

#### Instructions
Blend till smooth

---

### 3. Avocado Dressing

#### Ingredients
- 1 avocado
- 1/2 small red onion
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt

#### Instructions
Blend all till smooth

## Desserts

### Ices - Three Layer or One Layer

**Note:** Same recipe as File 1131

#### Base Ingredients
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 ¾ cups orange juice
- ½ cup lemon juice

#### Add Either:
- 4 mango
- 1 pineapple
- 1 papaya
- 8 kiwi

#### Instructions
1. Blend
2. Freeze
3. Blend again and it gets creamy
4. If you want to do layers, freeze again, then layer

## For Shluchim: Dressing and Salad Guide

### Three Dressings Compared

**Papaya Dressing:**
- Unique: Papaya seeds
- Flavor: Peppery, tangy
- Color: Light tan
- Best on: Green salads, mixed vegetables

**Mango Dressing:**
- Unique: Mango fruit
- Flavor: Sweet, fruity, tangy
- Color: Orange/yellow
- Best on: Fruit salads, tropical salads, lettuce with fruit

**Avocado Dressing:**
- Unique: Avocado base
- Flavor: Creamy, mild, tangy
- Color: Light green
- Best on: Hearty salads, vegetables, eggs

---

### Making All Three for Variety

**Why Offer Multiple Dressings:**
- Different taste preferences
- Color variety on buffet
- Pair with different salad types
- Some guests prefer creamy, some prefer light
- Creates abundant feeling

**Quantities for 50 People:**
- Make 3-4 batches of each dressing
- Total: 9-12 cups dressing
- Label clearly
- Serve in squeeze bottles or small pitchers
- Keep refrigerated until service

### Storage and Shelf Life

**All Three Dressings:**
- Store: Refrigerate in sealed containers
- Shelf life: 5-7 days
- Best: Make 1-2 days before event
- Shake/stir before serving (may separate)

**Specific Notes:**

**Avocado Dressing:**
- May brown slightly (lemon helps prevent)
- Stir well before serving
- Shortest shelf life (3-5 days max)

**Mango Dressing:**
- Most stable
- Longest shelf life (up to 1 week)
- Color may darken slightly

**Papaya Dressing:**
- Very stable
- Seeds may settle (shake well)
- Up to 1 week
