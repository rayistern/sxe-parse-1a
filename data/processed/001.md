## Chanukah Sheet Music

Dear Shluchim,

As Chanukah is approaching, we are pleased to provide you with a collection of sheet music that you can give to your local band to enhance your Chanukah event. It includes:

- **12 Chanukah songs**
- **Several lebedike nigunim** to get your guests dancing

This music was compiled and transcribed by **Avremi G of 'A Notable Idea'**.

### About the Composer

Avremi has been the musical director of the Kinus for the past 6 years and is the largest supplier of Jewish Sheet music worldwide, having transcribed well over 5,000 Jewish tunes. He is also known as the arranger of Avraham Fried's Chabad CDs:
- 'Avinu Malkeinu'
- 'Yankel Yankel'

**Contact:** avremig@gmail.com

### Download

[Chanukah Sheet Music PDF](http://www.shluchim.org/Shluchimnet/SheetMusicForChanukah.pdf)

Wishing you a happy Chanukah.
