**Bottom Line:**
Four essential Hilchos Chanukah video resources on Merkos 302 platform provide comprehensive halachic training for Shluchim: (1) Rabbi Mordechai Farkash's Q&A format addressing practical questions, (2) Rabbi Zushe Winner's focused treatment of menorah-specific halachos, (3) Rabbi Yosef Ginzburg's in-depth Hebrew shiur for advanced study, and (4) Rabbi Yosef Shusterman's comprehensive refresher perfect for annual pre-Chanukah review. Recommended viewing schedule: 6 weeks before watch refresher for overview, 4 weeks before focus on menorah halachos for public lighting preparation, 2 weeks before review Q&A to prep for community questions, and reference Hebrew shiur as needed for deeper understanding. Critical topics for public menorah: height limits (20 cubits max), fuel types (kerosene yes with bracha, gas/butane no bracha, electric no bracha), timing (plag hamincha earliest, 30 minutes fuel past nightfall), non-Jewish participation (shamash yes, candles no), women's role (tznius - no bracha), mandatory announcement that public lighting doesn't fulfill personal obligation, blessing protocol with multiple lighters, and minyan preferred but not required. Use videos for personal study and staff training, but extract and simplify for community education rather than screening directly—create handouts, FAQ sheets, and visual guides based on content. Platform offers streaming access, searchable library, organized by topic, professional quality lectures. These videos provide halachic foundation that complements this document collection's practical operational guidance—together they form complete resource covering both why (halacha) and how (implementation). Keep bookmarked with timestamp notes for quick reference when questions arise during Chanukah.
