## Supply Planning

### Ingredients (100 Hamantaschen Example)

**Dough:**
- Flour: 10-15 cups
- Eggs: 10-15
- Sugar: 3-5 cups
- Oil/butter: 2-3 cups
- Baking powder: 2 Tbsp
- Vanilla: 2 Tbsp

**Fillings:**
- Poppy seed: 2-3 cans
- Prune butter: 2-3 jars
- Jams: 3-4 jars
- Chocolate chips: 2 bags

**Other:**
- Flour for dusting
- Parchment paper
- Cooling racks
- Packaging materials

---

### Equipment Needed

**Baking:**
- Multiple ovens
- Baking sheets (many)
- Mixing bowls
- Rolling pins
- Circle cutters (3-4 inch)
- Measuring cups/spoons
- Spatulas
- Cooling racks

**Setup:**
- Tables for stations
- Tablecloths
- Aprons
- Hand washing station
- Cleaning supplies
- Trash/recycling

## Timing and Logistics

### Schedule

**2-3 Hour Event:**
- Welcome/instructions: 15 min
- Dough preparation: 30 min
- Assembly stations: 60-90 min
- Baking: continuous
- Cleanup: 20 min

**Staff Arrival:**
- 1-2 hours before
- Prep ingredients
- Set up stations
- Test equipment
