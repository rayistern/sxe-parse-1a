# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Purim Play Script - Complete Megillah Story

## Note: Purim Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Performance script for Purim spiel

**Setting:** Ancient Persia, 4th century BCE

**Cast:** Narrator, King Achashverosh, Guard, Vashti, Bigsan, Seresh, Mordechai, Haman, Esther

**Duration:** Approximately 15-20 minutes

**Target Audience:** All ages, family-friendly

---

## Complete Script

### Opening - Narrator

> "It all began in Ancient Persia in the 4th century BCE. The Jewish people were subjects of the mighty Persian Empire which extended over 127 lands."

**Stage Direction:** Set the scene, perhaps with backdrop of palace

---

## Scene 1: The Party and Vashti's Refusal

### Narrator Introduction

> "3 years after King Achashverosh ascended the Persian throne, when he felt secure in his new position, he celebrated by throwing a grand 180-day-long party for all of his subjects. Following this extravagant gala, Achashverosh hosted a smaller week-long party for the residents of the capital city of Shushan. In the palace's woman's quarters, Achashverosh's wife, Queen Vashti, hosted her own party for the Shushanite womenfolk."

---

### The King's Plot

**King Achashverosh:**
> "Ahh good wine we have here! *hiccup*"

**Guard:**
> "Yes yes, very good your majesty. I see everyone here is enjoying themselves…especially those Jews!! Hehehehe"

**King Achashverosh:**
> "Ahh yes! *whispering* That is all part of the plan! They will enjoy themselves here with us, turn away from their G-d, and then He will abandon them! Then they are completely in our hands!! Hahahaha!"
>
> "Anyway, I hope all of you are enjoying yourselves as well!"

**Guard:**
> "Yes your majesty, we most certainly are!"

**Key Theme:** King's sinister plot against Jews revealed

---

### Summoning Vashti

**King Achashverosh:**
> "Excellent! Now…I would like to present to you my most beautiful possession, more beautiful than anything you see here, or anything in the world for that matter! I would like to present to you my queen….Vashti!! Guard, go fetch my queen!"

**Guard:**
> "Yes your majesty"

**Guard:**
> "Vashiiiiii, ohh Vashtiiii!"

**Vashti:**
> "What do you want!?"

**Guard:**
> "I have a command from the king for you to appear before him in your royal crown."

---

### Vashti's Miraculous Affliction

**Vashti:**
> "Ohhhh, is that so?? Hehe I wouldn't mind a chance to show off my beauty! Let me just have one last look in the mirror….OH MY GOSH! What on earth is this!!??? Pimples all over my face???? A tail???? I-I-I…can't believe it! Guard, tell the king I WILL NOT COME!!!"

**Stage Direction:** Vashti looks in mirror, dramatic shock, comedy opportunity

**Theme:** Divine intervention - Vashti suddenly afflicted, refuses to appear

---

### Transition - Narrator

> "The King, horrified at the Queen's refusal to appear before him, turned to his advisors to see what to do next. Haman, one of the king's close advisors advised that Vashti be executed. The king followed his advice but soon missed his queen and decided to look for another one. A beauty pageant was orchestrated across all the lands of Achashverosh to find the most beautiful woman for the king. Esther was a beautiful Jewish girl, the niece of the Jewish leader Mordechai. She did not want to have to leave her Jewish home to live with the king. Nevertheless, she was taken to the palace where she refused to put on any lotions or perfumes that the other women were using. Still, she was chosen to become the new queen!"

**Key Points:**
- Vashti executed
- Beauty pageant
- Esther chosen despite using no cosmetics
- Divine will evident

---

## Scene 2: The Assassination Plot

### Bigsan and Seresh's Plan

**Bigsan:**
> "Hey Seresh!"

**Seresh:**
> "ya bigsan…"

**Bigsan:**
> "I have this really good idea…it'll take care of all our problems and get rid of that nasty king of ours."

**Seresh:**
> "really? Let me hear…"

**Bigsan:**
> "but I don't want anyone to find out what our plan is so I'm going to speak to you in a different language."

**Bigsan:**
> "*speaks in mushakabable*"

**Seresh:**
> "*answers in mushkabable very excitedly*"

**Stage Direction:** Actors can use gibberish language, comedic effect

---

### Mordechai Overhears

**Narrator:**
> "Meanwhile Mordechai was standing by. Bigsan and Seresh didn't realize that he knew all languages. Mordechai heard their plan to poison the king and sent a message to Esther who passed it on to the king in Mordechai's name. The king was saved and Bigsan and Seresh were put to death."

**Key Theme:** Mordechai's wisdom saves the king - will be important later

---

### Haman's Decree

**Narrator:**
> "The King issued a decree that everyone must bow to Haman…As Haman walked through the streets enjoying all the honor he was receiving, he noticed one man…Mordechai…who would not bow. Haman was infuriated and decided to take revenge against all the Jews. He threw lots to determine the 'lucky' day when he would implement his plan. The lot fell on the 13th day of the Hebrew month of Adar. With permission from the king, Haman sent out a decree ordering the people of the king's land to rise up against the Jews and kill them all –men, women and children!"

**Key Elements:**
- Mordechai refuses to bow
- Haman's rage
- Purim - meaning "lots"
- Genocidal decree

---

### Mordechai's Request to Esther

**Narrator:**
> "After hearing the horrible news, Mordechai sat in sack cloth and ashes in front of the king's palace. He sent a message to Esther asking her to approach the king and beg him to spare her people. Esther knew that if she went to the king without being summoned she may be killed. Mordechai told her that the Jews are in terrible danger! Perhaps she was put into the position of queen for this reason! To save all the Jews! Esther agreed to go but asked that all Jews fast for 3 days before. All the Jews fasted, repented and davened to Hashem."

**Key Themes:**
- "Perhaps you were put in this position for this reason"
- Three days of fasting
- Unity of Jewish people
- Prayer and repentance

---

## Scene 3: Esther's First Party

### Esther Approaches Unsummoned

**King:**
> "Who dares enter without having been summoned!?"

**Esther:**
> "Your majesty, it is I, Queen Esther."

**King:**
> "Oohhh Queen Esther! Please enter, what can I do for you?"

**Theme:** King accepts her - miraculous

---

### The Invitation

**Esther:**
> "I would like to invite the king and Haman to a small feast I have prepared."

**King:**
> "Oh how nice, I would be honored to attend. I will send a message to Haman as well."

**Esther:**
> "Thank you your majesty!"

**Stage Direction:** *The next day*

---

### First Party Scene

**King:**
> "wow what a magnificent feast Esther!"

**Haman:**
> "yes it is truly spectacular!"

**Esther:**
> "Thank you."

**King:**
> "Esther, what is your request? I will give you up to half of the kingdom?"

**Esther:**
> "Would the king and Haman please come to another party tomorrow?"

**Haman:**
> "oh ya!!!"

**King:**
> "yes we will attend."

**Theme:** Esther's strategic delay - builds suspense

---

## Scene 4: The Sleepless Night

### Haman's Gallows Plan

**Haman:**
> "Mordechai still won't bow down to me! I am soo mad! What can I do???? Hmmm… I know! I will build a gallows and eventually hang him on it! Hahahahhaha! I better ask the king permission…."

**Stage Direction:** Haman's evil plan, comedy in his villainy

---

### The King Can't Sleep

**Meanwhile…**

**King:**
> "I can not sleep! Gosh this is getting ridiculous. I think I'll read from my book of chronicles..Oh I forgot about that! Mordechai the Jew saved my life! Was he ever repaid??"

**Theme:** Divine intervention - king remembers Mordechai at perfect moment

---

### The Reversal

**Haman:**
> "knock knock…"

**King:**
> "It's the middle of the night! What do you want?"

**Haman:**
> "I had a request for the king"

**King:**
> "Well I have a question for Haman…What should be done to a man whom the king wants to honor?"

**Haman:**
> "*thinks…he must be talking about me!* Well, I think he should be rode around on the king's horse and in the king's royal garments. Someone should lead him around saying 'so shall be done to the man whom the king wishes to honor!!!' How's that??"

**King:**
> "Peerrrffect! Do that to Mordechai the Jew."

**Haman:**
> "WHO?????"

**King:**
> "I said Mordechai the Jew."

**Haman:**
> "Mordechai the WHO????"

**King:**
> "THE JEW!!! Now GO!!!"

**Comedy Element:** Haman's repeated disbelief, audience loves this

---

### Transition

**Narrator:**
> "So Haman led Mordechai around Shushan the next day in the royal clothes on the royal horse and attended the queen's party, not in such a good mood."

**Stage Direction:** Show Haman's humiliation

---

## Scene 5: The Revelation and Victory

### Esther's Request

**King:**
> "Esther what is your request?"

**Esther:**
> "If it pleases the king, let my life be granted me by my plea, and the life of my people by my request. For my people and I have been sold to be annihilated, killed and destroyed!"

**King:**
> "Ridiculous! Who would do such a thing?"

**Esther:**
> "Him! (she points at Haman) It is Haman!"

**Haman:**
> "(chokes on his food) Excuse me??"

**King:**
> "no excuses that is absolutely outrageous! Guards! Kill him!"

**Stage Direction:** Dramatic pointing, Haman's shock, king's rage

---

### Final Resolution - Narrator

> "Haman was taken to the big gallows he had built for Mordechai and hanged there. The king then made a new decree that the Jews could defend themselves. On the 13th of Adar, the Jews killed many of their enemies including Haman's ten sons. They were given another day to destroy their enemies. The 14th of Adar and on that day they celebrated. They made that day into a special holiday to commemorate these amazing events. This is why we have the holiday called 'Purim!'"

**Themes:**
- Measure for measure (Haman hanged on own gallows)
- Jews defend themselves
- Victory and celebration
- Origin of Purim holiday

**Bottom Line:**

Complete Purim spiel script dramatizing full Megillah story in five scenes covering approximately 17 minutes with 9 cast members (Narrator, King Achashverosh, Guard, Vashti, Bigsan, Seresh, Mordechai, Haman, Esther). **Scene 1**: King's 180-day party reveals sinister plot ("They will enjoy themselves with us, turn away from their G-d, and then He will abandon them!"), Vashti refuses to appear after miraculously developing pimples and tail, beauty pageant results in Esther becoming queen despite using no cosmetics. **Scene 2**: Bigsan and Seresh plot assassination speaking "mushkabable" language, Mordechai (who "knew all languages") overhears and saves king, Haman's decree against all Jews after Mordechai refuses to bow, Esther agrees to approach king after three-day fast ("Perhaps you were put in this position for this reason!"). **Scene 3**: Esther enters unsummoned ("Who dares enter!"), invites king and Haman to feast, defers request until second party tomorrow building suspense. **Scene 4**: Haman plans gallows for Mordechai, king can't sleep and reads Chronicles remembering Mordechai saved his life, Haman arrives to request permission, king asks what to do for "man whom the king wants to honor," Haman (thinking it's himself) suggests royal horse and garments, king orders "Do that to Mordechai the Jew"—Haman's repeated "WHO????" creates comedy climax. **Scene 5**: At second party Esther reveals "my people and I have been sold to be annihilated," points to Haman, Haman chokes on food, king orders execution on gallows Haman built, Jews defend themselves on 13th Adar, celebrate on 14th creating Purim holiday. Built-in comedy: king's hiccup, Vashti's mirror scene, "mushkabable" gibberish, Haman's disbelief repetition. Props needed: crowns, wine cup, mirror, book, royal garments, prop horse. Audience participation opportunities: boo Haman, cheer heroes, use groggers. Educational themes: divine providence, Jewish unity, courage, reversal of fortune, evil plans backfire.
