**Resource Type:** Spiritual/philosophical teaching on matzah

**Purpose:** Explain deeper meaning of matzah and Pesach

**Format:** Questions, concepts, included essay

**Audience:** Classes, divrei Torah, educational programming

---

## Opening Question from Haggadah

### The Text

> "This Matzah that we eat for what reason? Because the dough of our fathers did not have time to become leavened before the King of the kings of kings, the Holy One, blessed be He, revealed Himself to them and redeemed them."

---

### The Problem

**It Seems So Insignificant:**
- Almost an accident?
- Just because they were rushing?
- We have Pesach because of this?

**The Question:**
> "There must be a spiritual idea behind the Matzah, something significant about it."

---

## Second Question: Bread of Affliction

### The Text

> "This is the bread of affliction that our fathers ate in the land of Egypt. Whoever is hungry, let him come and eat; whoever is in need, let him come and conduct the Seder of Passover. This year [we are] here; next year in the land of Israel. This year [we are] slaves; next year [we will be] free people."

---

### The Problem

**"This is the bread..."**
- This is not literally that bread
- Might taste like it
- But it's not physically the same

**The Question:**
> "Why are we saying it is? There must be something in common between our bread now, and theirs back then."

---

## Circular Nature of Time

**Principle:**
> "Each year is a circle, the event that happened back then, reoccurs on some level each year."

**Implication:**
- Not just remembering
- Spiritual energy returns
- Original event's power available
- Annual renewal of experience

---

## Core Concepts

### The Contrast

**Chometz = Ego**
- Puffed up
- Inflated
- Self-important
- Arrogance

**Matzah = Humility**
- Flat
- Simple
- Submission
- Letting go

---

### Pesach - The Concept

**Word Meaning:** "To leap"

**Not a Step:**
- Step: Still connected to previous space
- Leap: Both feet off the ground
- Totally new place
- Complete transition

---

### Egypt - The Limitation

**Mitzrayim = Limiting**
- Narrow straits (meitzarim)
- Constrictions
- Boundaries
- Restrictions

**Going Out of Egypt:**
- Breaking free of limitations
- Overcoming restrictions
- Transcending boundaries

---

## What Is Freedom?

**Not Negative Definition:**
- Not just "not enslaved"
- Not just "not restricted"
- Freedom is positive act

**True Freedom:**
> "Reaching within and realizing our true potential, that we are truly a piece of G-d, we can accomplish much more then we think"

**The Act:**
- Looking within
- Realizing who we are
- Not limiting ourselves to outer part
- Connecting to essence

---

## Being in the Place of Change

**Why Risky?**

### Risk 1: Amalek - Ego Can Set In

**The Danger:**
- Make real meaningful move
- Feel very proud of yourself
- Don't realize Hashem helped
- Conceit and arrogance
- Amalek comes right after Exodus

**What is Amalek?**
- Source of all negative traits
- Especially ego
- When making spiritual progress
- Threatens to make you conceited

---

### Risk 2: Foundational Stage Is Fragile

**The Problem:**
- Making new platform in Yiddishkeit
- New leap in spiritual self
- This is the foundation
- Don't want negativity mixed in
- Don't want chometz in foundation

**Therefore:**
> "Pesach we need to avoid chometz at all cost."

---

## How Do We Get Rid of All the Chometz?

### Method 1: Work Hard

**Our Effort:**
- Scrubbing
- Toiling
- Physical labor
- Removing chametz actively

---

### Method 2: Divine Revelation

**From Above:**
> "Hashem reveals himself in such an open way that we are naturally humbled."

**What Happened at Original Exodus:**
> "At the time of Mitzrayim that is what happened, Hashem was reveled in such an open way, that they were naturally humbled."

---

## Understanding the Haggadah Text

### The Question Repeated

> "This Matzah that we eat for what reason? Because the dough of our fathers did not have time to become leavened before the King of the kings of kings, the Holy One, blessed be He, revealed Himself to them and redeemed them."

---

### The Deep Read

**Why do we eat the Matzah?**
- Because of "Ma"
- What is "Ma"?

**The Word "Ma":**

**In Hebrew:**
חכמה (Chochma - Wisdom)

**Broken Down:**
כח מה (Koach Ma)

**Meaning:**
- The power of "what"
- The power of the unknown
- Humility
- Subjection
- Lose self
- Nothingness

---

### Why We Eat Matzah

**Purpose:**
> "In order to achieve Ma - ability to lose self, to be totally submitted to Hashem, just like chochma, is undefined, just a spark of idea."

**The Mechanism:**
- Chochma is undefined
- Just a spark
- Not fully formed
- State of nullification before ego

---

## The Original Pesach Energy

**What Happened:**
> "During the original Pesach the dough was unable to rise, because Hashem was revealed, there was no space for ego."

**Not Because They Rushed:**
- Dough was **unable** to rise
- Not just didn't have time
- Couldn't rise
- No space for chometz
- Hashem revealed, so no space for ego

**Why We Think We Have Ego:**
- Because Hashem is hidden
- When Hashem revealed
- Naturally no ego
- No place for "who am I"

---

## Annual Energy

**Each Year:**
> "That energy is with us each year on the first night of Pesach. The energy to let go of ourselves and our ego."

**First Night:**
- Divine revelation energy
- Natural humility
- Let go of ego
- Foundation moment

---

## Last Night Different

**By Last Night:**
> "We lean a bit more toward chometz, we are not at foundational stage anymore, therefore we need the sense of self to come in, to build it up, we can't sit back and rely on Hashem to continue inspiring us."

**Why Chometz Allowed Later:**
- Not at foundation anymore
- Need to build yourself up
- Can't be total nobody
- Need Jewish pride
- Work with yourself piece by piece

**Gebrokts on Last Day:**
- First week of Omer is foundational (chesed)
- After foundation built, can let bit of chometz
- Need healthy build
- Chometz only threat at foundation

---

## Soul Scrubbing: A Passover Lesson

**Author:** Mimi Hecht (Notik)

**Context:** Student at Machon Alte in Tzfat, Israel

---

### Opening - The Physical Work

**The Experience:**
> "My right arm is officially a hundred times stronger than my left, still burning and smelling of bleach."

**The Job:**
- Day of cleaning before Passover
- Scrub the floor
- Scrub 30 refrigerator racks
- Scrub counters
- Scrub dishes

**Initial Reaction:**
- Removing layers of grime
- Mold and rotting food
- Overwhelmed by work

---

### Seeking Meaning

**The Question:**
> "As I was removing layers of grime, mold and rotting food from the surfaces of all the items I encountered, I struggled to use my overly-exposed-to-fumes brain to tap into the meaning and purpose of all the cleaning."

**Tzfat Lesson:**
> "Learning in Tzfat, the city where the study of Kabbalah originated in northern Israel, has taught me enough to expect more from the seemingly mundane in this world."

**Principle:**
- Nothing merely physical
- Everything has deeper meaning
- Mundane contains profound
- Tzfat teaches to look deeper

---

### The Alter Rebbe's Example

**The Story:**
> "The Alter Rebbe is known to have spent an enormous amount of time intensely cleaning his one-room house."

**The Standard:**
- Bedikat chametz takes ~20 minutes
- Alter Rebbe went all through the night
- Carefully guiding candle along floor
- Searching for crumbs

**The Question:**
> "Clearly, there is something here that goes beyond spring cleaning."

---

### The Insight

**Not Just House:**
> "The Alter Rebbe wasn't only searching his house. He was searching his soul."

**His Purpose:**
- Identifying proper motives for cleaning
- Checking for crumbs covering our soul
- Removing layers of dirt
- Stopping soul from shining full light

**Many Explanations Exist:**
> "There are many explanations for the much dreaded Passover cleaning. But for me, the Alter Rebbe's approach stands out."

---

### The Inner Dialogue

**While Scrubbing:**
> "So here I am scrubbing and scrubbing. I've got my soul on my mind. And I'm talking to myself. I'm saying, 'Mimi, scrub it away.'"

**The Mantra:**

> "Scrub away negativity. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away the suggestive powers of society. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away the barriers between your brothers and sisters. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away your silly insecurities. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away your ego. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away laziness. Let your soul shine."

> "Scrub away meaningless distractions. Let your soul shine."

---

### The Revelation

**Purpose:**
> "Scrub away all the layers. Reveal the light, the shine."

**What to Reveal:**
- Your mission
- Your powers
- The meaning
- The light inherent in the dark
- The G-dliness
- Redemption

---

### Universal Participation

**Personal and Cosmic:**
> "This little light of mine, I'm gonna let it shine. And the whole world, too, is with me."

**The Month:**
> "It is Nissan, the month of redemption, and Jews of every type and affiliation are cleaning away."

**The Activity:**
- Soul scrubbing
- World scrubbing
- Personal and universal
- Individual and collective

---

### The Result

**The Transformation:**
> "The world is starting to freshen and sparkle."

**The Anticipation:**
> "Passover is on its way."

**The Hope:**
> "I'll see you in Jerusalem."
