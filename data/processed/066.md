## Lead Paragraph: Then vs. Now

### The Hook

> "Times Change. Not so long ago, celebrating Chanukah meant some latkes, a few spins of the dreidel, some songs and of course, the menorah. A jelly doughnut or two and some gelt if lucky. Now, the lucky children of the Five Towns get to visit the annual Chanukah Wonderland and the family can experience the beautiful lighting of the Ice Menorah and Menorah of Freedom."

**Why This Works:**
- Nostalgic opening
- Contrast: simple past vs. elaborate present
- Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Positions as upgrade from ordinary celebration

## Why This Press Release Works

### Structure

**1. Catchy Headline**
- Command/challenge format
- Active language
- "Experience" vs. just "celebrate"

**2. Nostalgic Opening**
- Relatable beginning
- Then-and-now contrast
- Creates interest

**3. Multiple Events**
- Something for everyone
- Throughout holiday
- Different times/locations

**4. Superlatives**
- "Largest Ice Menorah in New York"
- "Largest Menorah in Five Towns"
- "Awesome spectacle"

**5. Concrete Details**
- Exact addresses
- Specific times
- Named participants
- Phone/website

**6. Universal Message**
- Not just for Jews
- Themes anyone can relate to
- Freedom, good vs. evil

**7. Global Connection**
- Part of worldwide movement
- From White House to Ground Zero
- Legitimacy and scale

**8. Media Invitation**
- Explicitly welcome reporters
- Promise access to spokesperson
