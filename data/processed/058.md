## Night #1: The Right to Believe in G-d

### The Core Question

**What We're Fighting For:**
> "Fight for G-dliness, the right to believe in things that can be felt even if they can't be seen, touched or heard."

---

### The Modern Challenge

**The Question Everyone Asks:**
> "So what would you answer when asked- Why G-d? Why Judaism? Why can't I just be a good person?"

**Why This is Valid:**
> "Now more than ever, it's such a valid question to ask. Our kids can volunteer in so many organizations, we can marry good respectful people, be a fair boss, align ourselves politically with people who fight for the rights of others- so who needs G-d in all of that?"

### Historical Context: The Greek Challenge

**Before the Greeks:**
> "For many years Jews more enlightened than society around them, suddenly pops up this cultured learned, body caring for/ worshiping, clean people known as the Greeks and Hellenistic culture and the confusion and temptation sets in. Until now there really wasn't much of an appealing choice."

**The New Temptation:**
For many years, Hellenism offered:
- The Olympics
- Greek Gods
- Worship of intellect
- Worship of the body
- Culture and sophistication

**Initially Free Choice:**
> "For many years Hellenism, the Olympics, Greek Gods, the intellect, the worship of the body etc was simply laid out in front of the Jews in a tempting way but you had free choice to do what you want. And many Jews did assimilate."

---

### The Turning Point: Forced Assimilation

**After Years of Temptation:**
> "After quite some years of the Greek culture laid out temptingly before the Jews but not forced upon them finally king Antiochus put his foot down and forced obedience."

**The Decrees:**
- No more kosher
- No more brit milah
- No Shabbos
- Otherwise you would die

**The Pattern:**
> "Specifically the laws that we do because Hashem said so, not because they made sense to Greek culture."

---

### The Stories of Resistance

**Mentioned:**
1. **Chana and her seven sons** - Ultimate martyrdom
2. **Yehudis** - Strategic victory
3. **Maccabees formed** - Military resistance

**The Victory:**
Jews won, came to rededicate the temple.

## The Oil: Why That's the Focus

### The Discovery

**The Situation:**
> "While there was LOTS of oil to be found, But it was the pure oil with the seal of the high priest that was not, Until G-d miraculously had them find it and then had it burn for 8 days when there was enough for 1."

---

### How the Talmud Summarizes Chanukah

**Talmud, Shabbat 21b:**
> "What is Chanukah? ... When the Greeks entered the Sanctuary, they contaminated all its oil. Then, when the royal Hashmonean family overpowered and was victorious over [the Greeks], they searched and found only a single cruse of pure oil that was sealed with the seal of the kohen gadol (high priest)-enough to light the menorah for a single day. A miracle occurred, and they lit the menorah with this oil for eight days. The following year, they established these [eight days] as days of festivity and praise and thanksgiving for G-d."

---

### The Puzzling Focus

**Wait, What?**
> "Wait, that's chanukah?? Not the fact this group of kohanim, Maccabees won against the most sophisticated army in the world?! Not that Yehudis, a women single handedly shut down one of the fiercest generals and the war took a tremendous positive turn?"

**The Question:**
> "Chanukah is about the oil??? Note that the focus of our celebration is all about oil- menorah, latkes. True we mention the military victory in Chanukah prayers, but still. It totally takes a back seat. Why? Why Was the fight and the victory mainly about oil?"

---

## What the Greeks Had Against Us

### Not Physical Destruction

**The Greek Approach:**
> "The Greeks did not endeavor to physically destroy the Jewish people, or even to deprive them of their religion and way of life; they merely wished to Hellenize them -- to enlighten their lives with the culture and philosophy of Greece."

**Their Message:**
> "Keep your books of wisdom, they said to the Jew, keep your laws and customs, but enrich them with our wisdom, adorn them with our art, blend them into our lifestyle."

**The Offer:**
- Worship your G-d in your temple
- But also worship the human body in the sports stadium we'll build
- Study your Torah
- But integrate it with principles of our philosophy and aesthetics of our literature

**The Evolution:**
> "Eventually, It led to our lives being in danger so we had to fight back but for many years they only had issue with what oil represents- the little cruise of G-dliness within each of us – the spiritual flame that flickers, the G-d given aspect of Torah."

### The Real Battle: The Spark of G-dliness

**What Oil Represents:**
> "The little cruise of G-dliness within each of us – the spiritual flame that flickers, the G-d given aspect of Torah."

**Why This Matters:**
The Greeks wanted to extinguish:
- The inexplicable
- The beyond-reason
- The purely G-dly
- The choiceless connection
- The soul's essence

**This is Why Oil is the Focus:**
- Oil = pure G-dliness
- Sealed oil = untouched by contamination
- One cruse = enough for Jewish identity to survive
- Eight days = beyond natural (seven is natural cycle)
- The miracle wasn't just military - it was spiritual preservation
