## Enhancement Ideas

### Suggestions from Yonasan Abrams (Temecula, CA)
*Posted: Wed Oct 28, 2015 13:53 PM ET*

1. **"Chanukah Foods"** - Serve traditional Chanukah treats

2. **Raffle** - *Always do a raffle. It's the best way to get new names!*

3. **Photo Opportunity** - Photo with Judah Maccabee character

4. **Music and Singing** - Before and after the program

5. **Contest with Judges**
   - Preferably staff of Home Depot as judges
   - Award for the best, most creative menorah

## Best Practices

### Program Flow

1. **Arrival & Registration**
   - Check-in and distribution of free worker aprons
   - Raffle ticket distribution

2. **Welcome & Music**
   - Opening music and singing
   - Introduction of the program

3. **Menorah Building Activity**
   - Hands-on menorah construction
   - Home Depot staff assistance
   - Supervised use of tools

4. **Food & Socializing**
   - Donut wall
   - Chanukah refreshments
   - Photo opportunities

5. **Contest & Awards**
   - Judging of menorahs
   - Award ceremony
   - Raffle drawing

6. **Closing**
   - Final music and singing
   - Distribution of take-home materials
