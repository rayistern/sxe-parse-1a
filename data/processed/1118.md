## Opening Note

**Instruction and Support:**

> "Someone will be there to instruct and show where things are."

**Important Context:**

> "Please note: there are some very unique and interesting traditions which we observe during the Passover dinner, all will be clearly instructed on this outline. Sometimes these random traditions are served to all guests (ex: matza cracker, lettuce, hard boiled egg)."

---

## 6:15 PM - Pre-Event Job

### Room Set-Up

**Tables:**
1. Table cloths
2. Chairs
3. Plates
4. Silverware
5. Napkins
6. Cups
7. Wine cups
8. 1 small plate under wine cup
9. A Passover book on each chair

**Center of Tables:**
1. 1 bottle of water
2. One of each salad

---

### Prepare Seder Plate (Per Table)

**Traditional Passover Plate at Center of Each Table**

**Components:**
1. A hard boiled egg
2. Piece Romaine lettuce
3. Grated white horseradish
4. Roasted chicken bone
5. Slice of potato/onion
6. Apple/wine/nut dip

---

### Extra Tables - Washing Stations

**Purpose:**

> "During the evening we have the tradition for every attendee to wash their hands with a pouring cup, twice."

**Setup:**
- Table set up with 2 basins
- Washing cups
- Paper towel
- Garbages
- Water should be filled up from the bathroom

---

## 6:45 PM - Guests Begin to Arrive

**Task:** First course is served on tables

---

## 7:35 PM - Pre-Ceremony Preparation

**Clear:**
- First course plates
- Salads

**Bring Out:**
- 1 dry wine (per table)
- Grape juice (per table)
- Matza
- Seder plates
- Bowls of saltwater (one per table)
- Bowls of onion/potato (one per table)

---

## 7:55 PM - Formal Program/Ceremony Begins

### First Tradition

**Everyone blesses on cup of wine**

**All guests will be washing their hands**

---

### Hors D'oeuvres/Tradition #1: Potato & Saltwater

**Serve Per Table:**
- 1 small bowl or platter of 1" cube size cooked potato squares
- A bowl of very salty water per table

**After Service:**
- Clear potatoes after guests have taken 1, dipped and eaten
- Refill the washing stations with fresh water

---

### Stories

**Stories from Passover book will be read**

**Duration:** Approx. 20 minutes later...

---

### Second Hand Washing

**All guests will be washing hands again**

**While Washing:**
- Numbers 7-8 should be brought out (referring to next traditions)

---

### Hors D'oeuvres/Tradition #2: Lettuce, Horseradish, Apple

**Serve Each Table:**
- Large bowls filled with pre-washed and dried whole leaves of romaine lettuce
- Grated horseradish
- Apple [dip]

---

### Hors D'oeuvres/Tradition #3: Hard Boiled Egg

**Serve:**
- Every person a hard boiled egg in a small bowl

**Clear:**
- Clear away all the lettuce, horseradish, dip, egg, saltwater at this point (after egg is eaten)

---

## 8:40 PM (Approx.) - Main Course

**MAIN COURSE should be ready to be brought out**

---

## During Main Course - Dessert Preparation

**As guests are enjoying meal:**

**Prepare dessert ready to serve:**
- Cake with a swirl of chocolate sauce

---

## After Main Course - Final Traditions

**Before Clearing:**

Guests will:
1. Eat their last serving of matza cracker
2. Drink 2 more glasses of wine/grape juice

---

## Waiters Can Begin Cleanup

### Clearing Dinner

**Tasks:**
- Clearing the guests' dinner plates
- Consolidating main course back in prep room

**Important:**

> "Please do not discard any of the traditional items on the tables (ex: horseradish, apple dip, matza…) as they are used on 2nd night."

---

### Complete Room Cleaning

**Tasks:**
- Clear all platters/bowls/serving dishes/non-disposables
- Consolidate all drinks
- Place all remaining food back in Ziploc

---

## Hand Washing Blessing

**Blessing Posted for Reference:**

> "Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu, melech ha-olam asher kidishanu b'mitz'votav v'tzivanu al n'tilat yadayim."

**Translation:**
> "Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning the washing of hands."

## For Shluchim: Planning Community Seder Service

### Waiter Role Definition

**From This Document:**

**Pre-Event (6:15-6:45):**
- Complete room setup
- Table setting with specific placement
- Seder plate assembly (6 components per table)
- Washing station setup

**Service (7:35-8:40):**
- Clearing and bringing items
- Serving traditional Seder items
- Maintaining washing stations
- Coordinating with Seder progression

**Post-Event:**
- Complete cleanup
- Careful preservation of reusable items
- Food storage

**Compare to Waitress Role (File 1119):**
- Waitress: Food prep, plating, course sequencing
- Waiter: Room setup, traditional items, transitions

---

### Timeline Analysis

**Full Event Duration:**
- 6:15 PM: Setup begins
- 6:45 PM: Guests arrive
- 7:55 PM: Formal ceremony begins
- 8:40 PM: Main course
- Approximate end: 9:00-9:30 PM

**Total Duration:** Approximately 3-3.5 hours

**Staff Hours:**
- Setup: 30 minutes before guests
- Service: 2-2.5 hours
- Cleanup: 30-45 minutes
- Total per waiter: 3.5-4 hours

---

### Seder Traditions Served

**From Waiter Perspective:**

**1. Kadeish (Wine Blessing) - 7:55 PM:**
- Everyone blesses on cup of wine
- Dry wine and grape juice pre-placed

**2. Urchatz (First Washing) - After Kadeish:**
- All guests wash hands
- Washing stations used first time
- Refilled after this use

**3. Karpas (Vegetable Dipping) - Tradition #1:**
- Potato cubes in saltwater
- Cleared after one piece taken

**4. Stories/Maggid - 20 minutes:**
- Stories from Passover book read
- Gives waiters time to prepare next items

**5. Rochtzah (Second Washing) - After Maggid:**
- All guests wash hands again
- With blessing this time
- While washing, bring out lettuce/horseradish/apple

**6. Motzi Matzah/Maror/Korech - Tradition #2:**
- Whole lettuce leaves (for maror)
- Horseradish (bitter herbs)
- Apple dip (charoses)

**7. Hard Boiled Egg - Tradition #3:**
- Not technically part of Seder order
- Traditional addition
- Served individually

**8. Shulchan Orech (Meal) - 8:40 PM:**
- Main course served
- Guests enjoy meal

**9. Tzafun (Afikoman) - After Meal:**
- Last serving of matzah
- Two more glasses of wine

### Washing Station Critical Importance

**Why Two Washings:**

**First Washing (Urchatz):**
- Before karpas (vegetable)
- Without blessing
- All guests participate

**Second Washing (Rochtzah):**
- Before matzah
- With blessing (provided in document)
- All guests participate

**Logistics:**
- Each attendee washes with pouring cup
- Twice during event
- Requires basin setup
- Water from bathroom
- Must refill after first washing
- Paper towels and garbage needed

**Planning:**
- For 64-96 guests (estimated from other documents)
- Need multiple stations
- Document says "2 basins" minimum
- More efficient with 4-6 stations
- Dedicated "washer" per station helpful (from File 1116)

---

### Critical Preservation Note

**Important Instruction:**

> "Please do not discard any of the traditional items on the tables (ex: horseradish, apple dip, matza…) as they are used on 2nd night."

**Implications:**

**This Reveals:**
- Two-night Seder operation
- Cost savings through reuse
- Careful item tracking needed
- Ziploc storage system

**Must Preserve:**
- Horseradish (unused portions)
- Apple dip (charoses)
- Matzah (unopened)
- Seder plate items
- Wine/grape juice (unopened)

**Can Discard:**
- Used plates/napkins/cups
- Eaten food
- Saltwater (make fresh)
- Wilted lettuce (use fresh)

### Risk Management

**Critical Points from Document:**

**Food Safety:**
- Hard boiled eggs (refrigeration until service)
- Horseradish (keep cold)
- Apple dip (oxidation prevention)
- Main course (hot holding at 8:40 PM)

**Ritual Accuracy:**
- Correct blessing available
- Seder plate components exact
- Washing station functionality
- Timing coordination with Seder leader

**Operational:**
- Water supply for washing stations (from bathroom)
- Refilling stations between washings
- Garbage capacity (multiple uses)
- Staff knowing "where things are"

**Preservation:**
- Clear labeling for second-night items
- Ziploc bags ready
- Storage space at venue or transport
- Don't accidentally discard
