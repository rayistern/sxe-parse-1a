### Budget Considerations

**Low Budget:**
- Hay bales (often free from farms)
- DIY wanted posters
- Bandanas (cheap in bulk)
- Simple BBQ
- Downloaded country music
- Volunteer entertainment

**Medium Budget:**
- Rented mechanical bull ($500-1000)

- Photo booth rental

**High Budget:**

- Extensive authentic decor
