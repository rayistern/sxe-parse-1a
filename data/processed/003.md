## Build-A-Menorah Competition

**Concept:** Getting all sorts of materials for people to assemble Menorahs using their creativity

**Materials Needed:**
- Glue guns
- Metal bonding material
- Scrap metal
- Various craft supplies

**Additional Activities:**
- Olive press demonstration (haven't done it for several years for the general community)
- Local musician volunteer
- **Cost:** A few hundred dollars total
- **Typical Attendance:** 150+ attendees

## Chanukah Menorah Contest

**Source:** Got this idea from another Shliach - it's fantastic!

### Concept

Children build their own menorah and bring it to the party (we do ours in a big mall here).

**Judges:** The Mayor who helps light the Shamash will also judge the menorah contest

**Prizes:**
- Winner gets a big prize
- Every kid with a menorah gets a nice prize

### Expansion Ideas

**On-Site Crafting Station:**
- Collect a variety of items usable to craft a menorah
- Charge people per item
- Make stations with glue, hammers, etc.
- Kids and adults can build a menorah on-site
- End with a judged contest

## Downtown at the Gardens Chanukah Event

### Event Information

**Date:** Thursday, December 22nd, 2011
**Time:** 6:00 PM
**Location:** Downtown at the Gardens
**Admission:** FREE and open to the public

### Activities

- Celebrate under the open skies in the heart of our beautiful city
- **Giant 14-foot Menorah** to be lit by the City Council of Palm Beach Gardens
- Annual light show
- Illusion magic show that will take your breath away
- Free sufganiot (traditional Chanukah doughnuts)
- Face painting
- Glow-in-the-dark Menorah necklaces
- Music
- Chanukah gelt for every child in attendance

### History & Tradition

**Fourth Annual Festival** - The Chanukah event at Downtown has come to be somewhat of a tradition as Chabad of Palm Beach Gardens kicks off its fourth annual festival.

**Official City Event:** With City officials joining in the festivities, it has become the official city Chanukah event.

#### Councilman Eric Jablin's Statement:

> "I used to arrange the city's annual Chanukah Celebration at City Hall for over ten years running. Many people have asked me why I don't do it anymore. The reason is simply because the event was never paid for by public money, just private donors. With the tough economy, that money has become difficult to raise. It's nice to know that Chabad of Palm Beach Gardens has taken up that tradition, continuing this worthy endeavor, every year at Downtown at the Gardens. The Chabad of Palm Beach Gardens Chanukah Celebration has become the official City of Palm Beach Gardens Chanukah Celebration. My goal was always to open the event to the public and that is exactly what Chabad is doing. Even if you're not a member of any Synagogue, I urge you to participate in this beautiful event simply because the city of Palm Beach Gardens is celebrating Chanukah!"
