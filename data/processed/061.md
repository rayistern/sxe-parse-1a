## The Name "Chanukah"

### Jewish Holiday Names

**Every Holiday Has Meaning in Its Name:**
- **Pesach** = Pass over
- **Shavuot** = Oath (time we took oath to obey Torah)
- **Sukkot** = Booth (holiday when we sit in booths)

---

### Chanukah's Name

**The Literal Meaning:**
> "What does Chanukah mean? It means 'Chanu Chaf Hey' - they rested on the 25th day of the month of Kislev, because they had just won a big war against the Syrian Greek army, and therefore they rested."

**Breakdown:**
- **Chanu** = they rested
- **Chaf Hey** = 25 (numerical value of Hebrew letters כה)
- **25th of Kislev** = date of rededication

---

## The Power of the First Letter

### The Alter Rebbe's Teaching

**The Principle:**
> "The first Lubavitcher Rebbe explains that every word is made up of letters, and those letters are the DNA of the word. However the first letter of the word, is the most powerful letter of the word. In the first letter of the word is the entire theme of the word."

**Application to Chanukah:**
> "Therefore we are saying that the first letter of the word Chanukah, contains within it the entire theme of Chanuka."

---

### The First Letter: Chet (ח)

**The Question:**
> "So what's the first letter of the word Chanukah? The letter 'Chet'. Let's look into the letter Chet and how it shows us the whole theme of Chanukah."

---

## Three Dimensions of the Letter Chet

### Dimension #1: Numerical Value

**Chet = 8:**
- On Chanukah we light **8 candles**
- We celebrate for **8 days**
- The number 8 represents transcendence (beyond the natural 7)

---

### Dimension #2: The Word Cheyrut

**Chet Means Freedom:**
> "Ches means: Cheyrut = Freedom. On this holiday we became free from our oppressors. We became free from the Syrian Greeks."

**The Liberation:**
- Physical freedom from Greek rule
- Spiritual freedom to practice Judaism
- Internal freedom to be ourselves

---

### Dimension #3: The Design of the Letter

**The Arizal's Teaching:**
> "Now let's look at the design of the letter Chet. (Show ladies a copy of the ches, as it is in the Torah). According to the Arizal the letter Chet in the torah, as you see here on this paper is made up of three different letters: * a Vav. * a Zayin, and a 'Camels Hump' which is the upside down V."

**Three Components:**
1. **Vav** (ו) - Right vertical line
2. **Zayin** (ז) - Left vertical line
3. **"Camel's Hump"** (∩) - Upside down V connecting them

---

## Three Miracles of Chanukah

### How the Design Connects

> "So how does the design of the letter ches connect to the holiday of Chanukah? Let's break down the three parts of the letter Chet and see its connection."

**The Holiday Had Three Separate Miracles:**

---

### Miracle #1: Victory in War (24th of Kislev)

**What Happened:**
- Jews defeated mighty Greek army
- Few overcame many
- Weak defeated strong

**Level of Miracle:**
> "This miracle of the victory, the Jews were involved with. It is called a salvation. Even though Hashem performed many miracles in this victory (eg: few Jews killing many Greeks etc...), but the Jews practically fought the war, nature didn't change in any way at all, and therefore this is the lowest kind of a miracle."

**Classification:**
> "This kind of miracle is called a Salvation. Where the miracle is hidden in nature. (same with the story of Purim, nature didn't change, the miracles were hidden in nature)"

**Date:** 24th of Kislev

---

### Miracle #2: Finding the Jug of Oil (25th of Kislev)

**What Happened:**
> "The Syrian/Greeks came into the Temple and destroyed all the jugs of oil, but left one untouched. The fact that they couldn't find one jug of oil but found all the others…, is that called nature or miraculous?"

**Level of Miracle:**
> "It is in between. You can't put your finger on it, but you can't call it nature either. This kind of miracle is called a 'wonder'."

**Classification:**
- Not clearly natural
- Not clearly supernatural
- Something "in between"
- Called a **"Wonder"**

**Date:** 25th of Kislev

---

### Miracle #3: Oil Lasting Eight Days (26th of Kislev onward)

**What Happened:**
Oil sufficient for one day lasted for eight days.

**Why It Started Day 2:**
> "...which really started on the 26th day of Kislev- because there was enough oil for one day, so the first day that the Menorah burned, was not a miracle."

**Level of Miracle:**
> "This is a miracle that was totally G-dly, way above nature. This is called a Nes."

**Classification:**
- Completely supernatural
- Beyond any natural explanation
- Pure G-dly miracle
- Called a **"Nes"** (miracle)

**Date:** Started 26th of Kislev, lasted through 2nd of Tevet

---

### Three Levels Summary

1. **Salvation** (24th) - Miracle hidden in nature
2. **Wonder** (25th) - Something in between
3. **Nes** (26th+) - Openly supernatural miracle

**Connection to Chet:**
The three components of the letter Chet correspond to these three distinct types of miracles.

## Two Ways of Serving G-d

### The Great Debate: Hillel vs. Shamai

**The Question:**
How to light the Chanukah menorah?

**Shamai's Method:**
- Night 1: Light **8 candles**
- Night 2: Light **7 candles**
- Continue **downward**
- Night 8: Light **1 candle**

**Hillel's Method:**
- Night 1: Light **1 candle**
- Night 2: Light **2 candles**
- Continue **upward**
- Night 8: Light **8 candles**

**The Ruling:**
> "Our rabbis say that now we will light like Hillel."

---

### The Philosophy Behind Each Approach

**What's the Difference?**
> "What is behind the logic of Shamai and Hillel? Shamai's focus is on the darkness, and Hillel focuses on the light."

---

### Shamai: Focus on Darkness

**His Logic:**
> "Shamai says that the first night there is a lot of darkness, so when you have a lot of darkness, you need a big light, to get rid of it and therefore you have to light all 8 candles, and as the darkness gets less, we light less candles."

**The Approach:**
- Identify the darkness/negativity
- Attack it with maximum force
- Eliminate evil directly
- Focus on what needs fixing

**School of Thought:**
Focus on the **negative** to eliminate it

---

### Hillel: Focus on Light

**His Logic:**
> "But Hillel says no, even though there is a lot of darkness lets focus on the light, and find the light. Each night lets looks for more light in the darkness, and therefore we increase in the lights each night."

**The Approach:**
- Identify the light/goodness
- Build on it gradually
- Add positivity incrementally
- Focus on what's already good

**School of Thought:**
Focus on the **positive** to increase it

---

### Same Goal, Different Methods

**The Unity:**
> "Their objective is the same: which means that both hillel and Shamai wants to get rid of the darkness, but their methods are different."

**Both Are Valid:**
- Same ultimate purpose
- Different paths to get there
- Both approaches have merit
- Each suited to different people/situations

---

## Connection to the Letter Chet

### The Two Lines

**The Vav and Zayin:**
> "These two school of thoughts represent the two lines of the letter ches; the vav and the zayin."

**The Breakdown:**
- **Vav** = Hillel = World of Kindness = Adding light
- **Zayin** = Shamai = World of Severity = Attacking darkness

**In Jewish Mysticism:**
- **Chesed** (Kindness) = Hillel's approach
- **Gevurah** (Severity) = Shamai's approach
- Both are divine attributes
- Both necessary for complete service

---

## Avraham: The Hillel Approach

### Pure Kindness and Love

**His Character:**
> "Avraham Avinu was like Hillel. Pure kindness and love. Always looking for the light in everyone and therefore touched so many people."

**His Approach:**
> "Accepting everyone for who they were, whether they were Jewish, Non Jewish, learned etc…. He only saw the light in each person."

**His Method:**
> "He didn't focus on taking all the bad (darkness) out of the person, but rather finding the Pintele yid of each Jew and bringing out the good in each person. He looked for the Good in everyone."

**The Result:**
> "He was like Hillel. Lighting one candle at a time. Lighting the life of one Jew at a time.."

**Impact:**
- Massive following
- Influenced thousands
- Built through positivity
- Universal reach

---

## Yitzchak: The Shamai Approach

### Severity and Perfection

**His Character:**
> "Yitzchak Avinu on the other hand was like Shamai. He was more severe than Avraham."

**His Focus:**
> "He focused on the Jews that was serious, the Jew who had the will power. He looked for the dedicated Jews. For the Jews who wanted perfection in their service of G-d as he did."

**His Method:**
> "He therefore didn't have nearly as big of a following as Avraham. He wanted to deal with Jews who wanted perfection in serving Hashem."

**His Philosophy:**
> "So too was Shamai, strict, demanding and a perfectionist. His philosophy was to get rid of negativity and darkness and so for that you need a lot of light. A lot of darkness needs a lot of light. Therefore Shamai, said we light 8 candles on the first night… we focus on all the darkness and get rid of it straight away…"

**Impact:**
- Smaller but more intense following
- Higher standards
- Deeper commitment
- More demanding

## Discussion: Chabad Philosophy

### Two Approaches in Torah

**Classic Debate:**
- **Sur Mera** = Turn away from evil (Shamai/Yitzchak approach)
- **Aseh Tov** = Do good (Hillel/Avraham approach)

---

**From Class Outline:**
> "Discuss the following with ladies: Talk about Chabad Philosophy as opposed to Non Chabad (concept of Sur Mera/ Asay Tov)….. How each Jew should treat each other..Focusing on positive and not on negative.. How we should treat ourselves, finding the good, etc….."

**Topics to Explore:**
1. **Chabad vs. Other Approaches:**
   - Chabad generally follows Hillel (focus on positive)
   - Other movements may emphasize fighting evil
   - Both have place in Jewish tradition

2. **How to Treat Others:**
   - Look for pintele yid (Jewish spark)
   - Build on strengths, not just fix weaknesses
   - Accept people where they are
   - Love brings people closer

3. **How to Treat Ourselves:**
   - Don't obsess over failures
   - Celebrate small victories
   - Build gradually
   - Add one more candle

4. **Practical Applications:**
   - Parenting
   - Marriage
   - Community leadership
   - Self-improvement

## The Future: Both Approaches United

### The Rebbe's Teaching

**Current Reality:**
> "The Rebbe says that now we follow the lighting of the candles according to Hillel, but when Moshiach come we will do both."

**The Question:**
> "How? It's not possible to light one candle and eight candles at the same time…."

---

### The Answer: Miracle of Moshiach

**The Resolution:**
> "Well that is the miracle of Moshiach. Even though in exile we can't grasp this concept of this. Somehow the Vav and the Zayin of the letter Ches will come together with the upside down 'V' uniting the two approaches, and it will be one.. Both Philosophies will come together…. In a perfect unity."

**The "Camel's Hump":**
- Third component of Chet
- Connects the two lines
- Unifies Hillel and Shamai
- Resolves the paradox
- Happens in Messianic era
