## Tips for Theme Selection

### Consider Your Audience

**Families:**
- Kid-friendly themes
- Interactive activities
- Accessible costumes
- Fun for all ages

**Adults:**
- Sophisticated themes
- Cultural exploration
- Gourmet food
- Elegant decor

**Teens:**
- Popular culture
- High energy
- Social media worthy
- Current trends

---

### Budget Considerations

**Review Before Committing:**
- Decor costs
- Food expenses
- Entertainment fees
- Activity materials
- Costume accessibility

**Mix and Match:**
- High-impact low-cost elements
- DIY where possible
- Borrow/rent big items
- Invest in reusables

---

## Implementation Strategy

### After Selecting Theme

**Early Planning (6-8 weeks before):**
- Download all materials
- Create detailed plan
- Book vendors
- Order supplies

**Mid Planning (3-4 weeks):**
- Confirm details
- Send invitations
- Recruit volunteers
- Finalize menu

**Final Prep (1 week):**
- Purchase perishables
- Set up early
- Brief volunteers
- Final walkthrough
