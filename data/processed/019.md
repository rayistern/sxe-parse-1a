## Event Overview

Chanukah - The Festival of Lights - for many of us brings back fond memories of childhood years and serves to renew our sense of identity. The Chanukah lights provide us with warmth, joy, strength and inspiration. Such is the purpose of a community-wide Car Menorah Parade and Grand Menorah Lighting celebration to be held at the Wesley Bolin Memorial Plaza this Chanukah.

Chabad of Arizona will celebrate the Festival of Chanukah Tuesday, December 3rd with a Grand Menorah Lighting across the State Capitol at Wesley Bolin Park followed by a Car Menorah Parade through Downtown Phoenix.

## Key Elements of an Effective Press Release

### Essential Components

**1. FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
- Always at top
- Tells media it's ready to publish

**2. Contact Information**
- Name
- Phone number
- Email
- Best times to reach

**3. Compelling Headline**
- Clear and descriptive
- Includes key details (what, where, when)
- Grabs attention

**4. Dateline**
- City and date
- Example: "PHOENIX - November 24, 2013"

**5. Opening Paragraph**
- Who, what, when, where, why
- Most important information first
- Grab reader's attention

**6. Supporting Details**
- Schedule of events
- Special guests
- Activities
- Community significance

**7. Quotes**
- From organizer
- From community leaders
- From special guests
- Humanizes the story

**8. Background Information**
- About Chanukah
- About your organization
- Global context

**9. Call to Action**
- Invite community
- Contact information
- Website/social media

**10. Boilerplate**
- Standard paragraph about your organization
- Contact details
- Always same at bottom of all releases

## Writing Tips

### Lead Paragraph Strategy

**Answer these questions in first 1-2 paragraphs:**
- **Who** is organizing? (Chabad of [City])
- **What** is happening? (Car Menorah Parade and Lighting)
- **When** is it? (Date, time, which night of Chanukah)
- **Where** is it? (Specific location)
- **Why** does it matter? (Community celebration, tradition, pirsumei nisa)

---

### Quote Strategy

**Include 2-3 Quotes:**

**Quote 1 - From You (The Organizer):**
- Explains significance of event
- Personal touch
- Why this matters to community

**Example:**
> "It is a holiday that enriches our lives with the light of tradition. In ancient times our ancestors rededicated the Temple in Jerusalem with the Menorah. Today, we rededicate ourselves to making this world a better and brighter place."

**Quote 2 - From Special Guest (If Available):**
- Mayor, city official
- Community leader
- Adds credibility and importance

**Quote 3 - From Participant or Community Member (Optional):**
- Testimonial from previous year
- Personal impact story
- Community voice

---

### Tone and Style

**Professional but Warm:**
- Factual information
- Enthusiastic about celebration
- Welcoming to all
- Family-friendly emphasis

**Inclusive Language:**
- "Open to the public"
- "Free of charge"
- "Bring the kids"
- "Join us"
- "Community-wide"

**Balance:**
- Educational (explain Chanukah)
- Inspirational (universal message)
- Practical (time, location, details)
- Invitational (call to action)

### When to Send

**Timeline:**

**3-4 Weeks Before Event:**
- Send first press release
- Announce event, basic details

**2 Weeks Before Event:**
- Follow-up release
- Highlight special guests or activities
- Build momentum

**1 Week Before Event:**
- Reminder release
- Media advisory format
- Photo/interview opportunities

**Day Before Event:**
- Final reminder
- Logistics for media coverage
- Contact information for day-of

**Day After Event:**
- Recap release
- Photos
- Attendance numbers
- Impact story
- Thank supporters

### Follow-Up

**After Sending:**
- Call media contacts 2-3 days later
- Offer additional information
- Suggest interview opportunities
- Provide photo opportunities
- Be available for questions

## Final Thoughts

An effective press release:
- **Informs** the media clearly
- **Invites** the community warmly
- **Inspires** with the Chanukah message
- **Includes** all practical details

Use this template as a starting point, then customize to:
- Your city and venue
- Your special guests
- Your unique activities
- Your organization's voice

Remember: The goal is pirsumei nisa - publicizing the miracle. Good media coverage helps achieve that goal!
