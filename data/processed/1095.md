## 1. Prohibition to Eat Chametz

### a. On the 7 Days of Pesach

**Source:** Exodus 13.3

**Duration:** All seven days of Passover

---

### b. After Noon of the Eve of Pesach

**Source:** Deuteronomy 16.3

**Biblical Prohibition:** From noon on erev Pesach

---

### c. Late Morning

**Status:** Rabbinical Prohibition

**Extends:** Beyond Biblical minimum

---

### d. After Pesach

**Status:** Rabbinical Prohibition

**Applies To:** Chametz that was in a Jew's possession over Pesach

**Reason:** Penalty for violation during Pesach

---

### e. As a Mixture with Other Foods

**Source:** Exodus 12.20

**Scope:** Even mixed with permitted foods

---

### f. Even a Minute Amount

**Standard:** No minimum quantity

**Strictness:** Any amount prohibited

---

## 2. Prohibition to Derive Benefit from Chametz

### a. Source

**Biblical Verse:** Exodus 13.3

**Derivation:** "It shall not be eaten"

**Expanded Meaning:** Not just eating, but all benefit

---

### b. On Pesach

**Applies:** Throughout Passover

---

### c. After Noon of the Eve of Pesach

**Biblical Level:** From noon onwards

---

### d. Late Morning

**Status:** Rabbinical Prohibition

**Earlier Restriction:** Before Biblical prohibition begins

---

## 3. Prohibition to Own or Possess Chametz During Passover

### a. First Source

**Verse:** Exodus 12.19

**Language:** "Shall not be found"

---

### b. Second Source

**Verse:** Exodus 13.7

**Language:** "Shall not be seen"

---

### c. Two Prohibitions

**Distinct Commandments:**
- Not to be found (bal yimatzei)
- Not to be seen (bal yeira'eh)

**Two separate negative commandments**

---

### d. Biblical Prohibition; Safeguard?

**Question:** Is this Biblical or Rabbinical safeguard?

**Status:** Biblical-level prohibition

---

### e. Yours, Not Others

**Key Distinction:** "Shall not be seen **of yours**"

**Implication:** Others' chametz permitted

**Application:** Non-Jew's chametz may be in your house

---

### f. Chametz, Not Non-Edible

**Scope:** Applies to edible chametz

**Non-edible:** Different status

---

## 4. Obligation to Dispose of Chametz Before Pesach

### a. Source

**Verse:** Exodus 12.15

**Command:** "Destroy fermented dough from your houses"

---

### b. When: When Prohibited or Before Prohibition?

**Question:** Timing of disposal obligation

**Options:**
- When chametz becomes prohibited
- Before prohibition begins

---

### c. How: Toss, Burn, etc.

**Methods:**
- Tossing
- Burning
- Other means of disposal

**Flexibility:** Various methods permitted

---

### d. Passive or Active

**Question:** Must one actively destroy?

**Or:** Can one passively abandon?

**Distinction:** Active disposal vs. passive removal

---

### e. Why Burn: Like Leftover Sacrifice

**Comparison:** Exodus 12.10

**Parallel:** Like leftover Pesach sacrifice

**Method:** Burning preferred like sacrifice remains

---

### f. What's Done Today

**Modern Practice:**
- Symbolic burning
- Sale to non-Jew
- Combination approach

---

## 5. Obligation to Check for Chametz - Bedikat Chametz

### a. Biblical Mitzvah as Part of Disposing

**Source:** Part of disposal obligation

**Level:** Biblical commandment

---

### b. Blessing on Checking: Disposing of Chametz

**Blessing Formula:** "...al biur chametz"

**Language:** On **disposing** of chametz (not on checking)

**Why:** Checking is means to disposal end

---

### c. When Only Rabbinically Required: Two Reasons

**Sometimes Reduced to Rabbinic Level**

**Two Possible Reasons:**
1. Already disposed of visible chametz
2. Sale makes possession Rabbinic issue

---

### d. When: Night Before Pesach

**Timing:** Night of 14th of Nissan

**Why Night:** Can search thoroughly with candle

---

### e. If One Leaves Their Home Before the Night Before Passover

**Early Departure:** Special procedures

**Timing Adjustment:** Search before leaving

**Considerations:** When to search if traveling

---

## 6. Obligation to Abandon Chametz - Bitul Chametz

### a. In Order Not to Possess

**Purpose:** Remove ownership status

**Effect:** "It's not mine" - mental abandonment

---

### b. In Case One Finds Good Chametz on Pesach

**Safeguard:** If valuable chametz discovered

**Human Nature:** Hard to destroy valuable food

**Solution:** Pre-nullified means no temptation

---

### c. As a Method of Disposing

**Alternative:** Instead of physical disposal

**Or Supplement:** In addition to disposal

---

### d. When: Night of Checking and When Burning

**Two Times:**
1. After bedikat chametz (night before)
2. After burning chametz (morning before)

**Two Declarations:** Different formulas

---

### e. How

**Method:** Verbal declaration

**Formula:** "All chametz in my possession..."

**Language:** Can be in any understood language

---

## 7. Selling Chametz to Non-Jew

### a. Talmud: Sell and Remove from Possession

**Source:** Talmudic permission

**Purpose:** Remove from possession

**Effect:** No longer "yours"

---

### b. Institutionalized to Sell: Approximately 600 Years Ago

**Historical Development:** Around 1400s

**Context:** European Jewish distilleries

**Economic Necessity:** Cannot destroy stock

**Timeline:** ~600 years of formal practice

---

### c. Communal Selling of Chametz via Rabbi: Last 250 Years

**Recent Development:** Last ~250 years

**Method:** Rabbi as community agent

**Terms:** Full price, debt structure

**Modern Form:** What we do today

---

## 8. Chametz After Passover

### a. Rabbinical Prohibition

**Status:** Chametz that was Jewish-owned over Pesach

**Level:** Rabbinic prohibition (not Biblical)

**Reason:** Penalty for violation

**Effect:** Permanently forbidden

---

### b. How It's Done Today

**Modern Practice:**
- Avoid purchasing from Jewish stores immediately after
- Check sources
- Rely on sale mechanism
- Community standards

## Torah Sources - Full Text

### Exodus 12:15

**Hebrew:**
> טו שִׁבְעַ֤ת יָמִים֙ מַצּ֣וֹת תֹּאכֵ֔לוּ אַ֚ךְ בַּיּ֣וֹם הָֽרִאשׁ֔וֹן תַּשְׁבִּ֥יתוּ שְּׂאֹ֖ר מִבָּֽתֵּיכֶ֑ם כִּ֣י | כָּל־אֹכֵ֣ל חָמֵ֗ץ וְנִכְרְתָ֞ה הַנֶּ֤פֶשׁ הַהִוא֙ מִיִּשְׂרָאֵ֔ל מִיּ֥וֹם הָֽרִאשֹׁ֖ן עַד־י֥וֹם הַשְּׁבִעִֽי:

**Translation:**
> "For seven days you shall eat unleavened cakes, but on the preceding day you shall clear away all leaven from your houses, for whoever eats leaven from the first day until the seventh day that soul shall be cut off from Israel"

**Key Points:**
- Seven days matzah
- Clear away leaven
- Punishment: Karet (spiritual excision)
- Timeframe: First through seventh day

---

### Exodus 12:17

**Hebrew:**
> יזוּשְׁמַרְתֶּם֘ אֶת־הַמַּצּוֹת֒ כִּ֗י בְּעֶ֨צֶם֙ הַיּ֣וֹם הַזֶּ֔ה הוֹצֵ֥אתִי אֶת־צִבְאֽוֹתֵיכֶ֖ם מֵאֶ֣רֶץ מִצְרָ֑יִם וּשְׁמַרְתֶּ֞ם אֶת־הַיּ֥וֹם הַזֶּ֛ה לְדֹרֹֽתֵיכֶ֖ם חֻקַּ֥ת עוֹלָֽם:

**Translation:**
> "And you shall watch over the unleavened cakes, for on this very day I have taken your legions out of the land of Egypt, and you shall observe this day throughout your generations, [as] an everlasting statute."

**Key Points:**
- Guard the matzot
- Commemorate the Exodus
- Eternal statute
- For all generations

---

### Exodus 12:19

**Hebrew:**
> יטשִׁבְעַ֣ת יָמִ֔ים שְׂאֹ֕ר לֹ֥א יִמָּצֵ֖א בְּבָֽתֵּיכֶ֑ם כִּ֣י | כָּל־אֹכֵ֣ל מַחְמֶ֗צֶת וְנִכְרְתָ֞ה הַנֶּ֤פֶשׁ הַהִוא֙ מֵֽעֲדַ֣ת יִשְׂרָאֵ֔ל בַּגֵּ֖ר וּבְאֶזְרַ֥ח הָאָֽרֶץ:

**Translation:**
> "For seven days, leavening shall not be found in your houses, for whoever eats leavening that soul shall be cut off from the community of Israel, both among the strangers and the native born of the land."

**Key Points:**
- Leaven not found (bal yimatzei)
- Seven days
- Punishment: Karet
- Applies to convert and native-born

---

### Exodus 12:20

**Hebrew:**
> ככָּל־מַחְמֶ֖צֶת לֹ֣א תֹאכֵ֑לוּ בְּכֹל֙ מֽוֹשְׁבֹ֣תֵיכֶ֔ם תֹּֽאכְל֖וּ מַצּֽוֹת:

**Translation:**
> "You shall not eat any leavening; throughout all your dwellings you shall eat unleavened cakes."

**Key Points:**
- No leavening at all
- All your dwellings
- Positive: Eat matzot

---

### Exodus 13:3

**Hebrew:**
> גוַיֹּ֨אמֶר משֶׁ֜ה אֶל־הָעָ֗ם זָכ֞וֹר אֶת־הַיּ֤וֹם הַזֶּה֙ אֲשֶׁ֨ר יְצָאתֶ֤ם מִמִּצְרַ֨יִם֙ מִבֵּ֣ית עֲבָדִ֔ים כִּ֚י בְּחֹ֣זֶק יָ֔ד הוֹצִ֧יא יְהֹוָ֛ה אֶתְכֶ֖ם מִזֶּ֑ה וְלֹ֥א יֵֽאָכֵ֖ל חָמֵֽץ:

**Translation:**
> "Moses said to the people, 'Remember this day, when you went out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, for with a mighty hand, the Lord took you out of here, and [therefore] no leaven shall be eaten.'"

**Key Points:**
- Remember the Exodus
- From house of bondage
- With mighty hand
- Therefore: No chametz eaten

---

### Exodus 13:7

**Hebrew:**
> זמַצּוֹת֙ יֵֽאָכֵ֔ל אֵ֖ת שִׁבְעַ֣ת הַיָּמִ֑ים וְלֹא־יֵֽרָאֶ֨ה לְךָ֜ חָמֵ֗ץ וְלֹא־יֵֽרָאֶ֥ה לְךָ֛ שְׂאֹ֖ר בְּכָל־גְּבֻלֶֽךָ:

**Translation:**
> "Unleavened cakes shall be eaten during the seven days, and no leaven shall be seen of yours [in your possession], and no leavening shall be seen of yours throughout all of your borders"

**Key Points:**
- Matzot for seven days
- No chametz seen "of yours" (bal yeira'eh)
- No leavening seen "of yours"
- Throughout borders

---

### Deuteronomy 16:2

**Hebrew:**
> בוְזָבַ֥חְתָּ פֶּ֛סַח לַֽיהֹוָ֥ה אֱלֹהֶ֖יךָ צֹ֣אן וּבָקָ֑ר בַּמָּקוֹם֙ אֲשֶׁ֣ר יִבְחַ֣ר יְהֹוָ֔ה לְשַׁכֵּ֥ן שְׁמ֖וֹ שָֽׁם:

**Translation:**
> "You shall slaughter the Passover sacrifice to the Lord, your God, [of the] flock, and [the Festival sacrifices of the] cattle, in the place which the Lord will choose to establish His Name therein"

**Key Points:**
- Pesach sacrifice
- Flock and cattle
- Designated place
- Where Hashem's Name dwells

---

### Deuteronomy 16:3

**Hebrew:**
> גלֹֽא־תֹאכַ֤ל עָלָיו֙ חָמֵ֔ץ שִׁבְעַ֥ת יָמִ֛ים תֹּֽאכַל־עָלָ֥יו מַצּ֖וֹת לֶ֣חֶם עֹ֑נִי כִּ֣י בְחִפָּז֗וֹן יָצָ֨אתָ֙ מֵאֶ֣רֶץ מִצְרַ֔יִם לְמַ֣עַן תִּזְכֹּ֗ר אֶת־י֤וֹם צֵֽאתְךָ֙ מֵאֶ֣רֶץ מִצְרַ֔יִם כֹּ֖ל יְמֵ֥י חַיֶּֽיךָ:

**Translation:**
> "You shall not eat leaven with it; for seven days you shall eat with it matzoth, the bread of affliction, for in haste you went out of the land of Egypt, so that you shall remember the day when you went out of the land of Egypt all the days of your life."

**Key Points:**
- Don't eat chametz with it
- Seven days of matzah
- "Bread of affliction"
- Reason: Left in haste
- Remember all your life

---

### Deuteronomy 16:4

**Hebrew:**
> דוְלֹא־יֵֽרָאֶ֨ה לְךָ֥ שְׂאֹ֛ר בְּכָל־גְּבֻֽלְךָ֖ שִׁבְעַ֣ת יָמִ֑ים וְלֹֽא־יָלִ֣ין מִן־הַבָּשָׂ֗ר אֲשֶׁ֨ר תִּזְבַּ֥ח בָּעֶ֛רֶב בַּיּ֥וֹם הָֽרִאשׁ֖וֹן לַבֹּֽקֶר:

**Translation:**
> "And no leaven shall be seen with you within all your border for seven days; neither shall any of the flesh you slaughter on the preceding day in the afternoon, remain all night until the morning."

**Key Points:**
- No leaven throughout borders
- Seven days
- Pesach sacrifice meat
- Not left overnight
