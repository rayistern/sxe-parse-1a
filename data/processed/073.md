# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Thank You Letter to Dignitary

## Sample Follow-Up Letter After Event

**Date:** December 26, 2001
**Organization:** Chabad of Glendale and the Foothill Communities
**Author:** Rabbi [REDACTED-NAME]
**Recipient:** Councilmember Frank Quintero

---

## The Letter

### Header

```
December 26, 2001

Councilmember Frank Quintero
613 East Broadway Suite 200
Glendale, CA 91206
```

---

### Opening: Immediate Thanks

> "Dear Councilmember Quintero,
>
> I would like to thank you for joining us at the Grand Menorah lighting at the Glendale Galleria. Your participation was very meaningful to the community and I was grateful for the opportunity to have met you in person."

**Elements:**
- Specific event mentioned
- "Very meaningful to the community" (impact statement)
- Personal touch: "met you in person"

---

### Character Appreciation

> "It is most gratifying and encouraging for us to know that there are people like you who are so willing to give of themselves to help others."

**Why This Works:**
- Compliments character, not just action
- "Give of themselves" (recognizes sacrifice)
- "Help others" (altruistic framing)
- Personal affirmation

---

### Broader Significance

> "Assuredly, it speaks gracious words of inspiration and encouragement for the future of the City of Glendale and our great country the United States of America."

**Strategic Elements:**
- Ties to city's future
- Patriotic reference
- Elevated language
- Inspiration and encouragement

---

### Future Relationship

> "Our mutual efforts for the City of Glendale complement each other and I look forward to working with you on future community projects."

**Key Points:**
- "Mutual efforts" (partnership)
- "Complement each other" (synergy)
- **Looking forward** (future-oriented)
- "Community projects" (broader than Chanukah)
- Door open for future collaboration

---

### Closing

> "With best wishes for your good health, happiness and prosperity.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Rabbi Simcha Backman
> Director, Chabad of Glendale and the Foothill Communities"

**Traditional Blessings:**
- Health, happiness, prosperity
- "Sincerely" (warm but professional)
- Full title included

---

## Why This Letter Works

### Structure

**1. Specific Thanks**
- Names event (Glendale Galleria)
- Acknowledges attendance
- Personal connection

**2. Character Praise**
- Goes beyond "thanks for coming"
- Recognizes personal sacrifice
- Affirms values

**3. Broader Impact**
- City benefit
- National significance
- Elevated language

**4. Future Focus**
- Ongoing relationship
- Partnership framing
- Open door

**5. Warm Wishes**
- Traditional blessings
- Genuine care
- Professional close

---

### Tone Analysis

**Formal But Warm:**
- "Councilmember" (formal title)
- "Your good health" (traditional)
- "Met you in person" (personal)

**Elevated Language:**
- "Most gratifying and encouraging"
- "Speaks gracious words"
- "Assuredly"

**Future-Oriented:**
- "Look forward to working"
- "Future community projects"
- "Mutual efforts"

## When to Send

### Timing Strategy

**Ideal:** 1-3 days after event
- Fresh in their mind
- Shows respect and efficiency
- While excitement is high

**Acceptable:** Within 1 week
- Still timely
- Professional
- Expected

**Late:** 2+ weeks
- Still send!
- Better late than never
- Acknowledge delay if very late

---

## Delivery Method

### Email vs. Mail

**Email (Fastest):**
- Send within 24 hours
- Attach photos
- Easy to forward
- Quick response

**Mail (More Formal):**
- Send within 3 days
- On letterhead
- Signed personally
- More traditional

**Both (Best):**
- Email immediately with photos
- Follow with mailed letter
- Shows extra effort
- Multiple touchpoints

**Medium-Term (3-6 months):**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Don't:**
- ✗ Wait too long (send within a week)
- ✗ Generic template without personalization
- ✗ Forget their name/title
- ✗ Overlook their specific contribution
- ✗ Miss opportunity to suggest future collaboration
- ✗ Send without proofreading
- ✗ Forget to include photos
- ✗ Make it all about you/your organization

**Do:**
- ✓ Send promptly
- ✓ Personalize with specific details
- ✓ Use correct title
- ✓ Mention their impact
- ✓ Open door for future
- ✓ Proofread carefully
- ✓ Include photos
- ✓ Focus on them and community

With best wishes,
Rabbi [Name]

**Bottom Line:**
This thank you letter succeeds by being prompt (within days), specific (mentions event and location), complimentary (affirms character, not just attendance), forward-looking (future collaboration), and warm (traditional blessings). It elevates the dignitary's participation from attendance to partnership, from one event to ongoing relationship. Send within 1-3 days, include photos, be specific about their contribution, and always open the door for future collaboration. This isn't just courtesy - it's relationship building that pays dividends for years to come.
