# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Waitress Instructions - Community Seder Service

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Operational instructions

**Purpose:** Service timeline and duties for waitress at community Passover Seder

**Format:** Checklist with timing and food preparation

**Related:** Files 1118 (waiter instructions), 1120 (service timeline)

---

## General Instructions

**Throughout the Meal:**
- Remove empty bottles
- Replenish with new bottles
- Applies to: water, wine, grape juice

---

## Hand Washing Stations

**Guests Wash Hands Twice During Meal**

**After First Washing:**
1. Replenish the water in dispensers
2. Empty tubs in the backyard
3. Clear used paper towels
4. Replace with new paper towels

---

## Schedule

### Upon Arrival

**Preparation Tasks:**
- Put horseradish in small bowls
- Put apple mixture in small bowls
- Prepare salt water (little bowls with water and salt)
- Put lettuce in long bowls

---

### Food Heating

**8:30 PM:**
- Put all food in the oven

**9:30 PM:**
- Light sternos
- Put food on sternos
- **Note:** If food is hot, only light one fire

---

### First Course Preparation

**Task: Plate First Course**

**Each Plate Should Have:**
1. Spring mix
2. 1 slice fish topped with sliced carrot
3. Drizzle basil dressing over salad
4. Half a boiled egg

**After Plating:**
- Cover
- Place in fridge

---

### Service Sequence

#### Lettuce, Horseradish, Apple Service

**When:** As soon as people sit down from washing their hands

**Bring Out:**
1. **LETTUCE** - Not the salad, only the whole lettuce leaves
2. **APPLE MIXTURE** - Add spoon before serving
3. **HORSERADISH** - Add spoon before serving

**Also During This Time:**
- Cut up pineapple

---

#### First Course Service

**When:** On cue

**Serve:** The first course (prepared earlier)

**Then:**
- Prepare soup bowls on the tray

---

#### Soup Service

**When:** Wait 10 minutes after first course

**First:** Clear first course, including all other small bowls on the table

**Then:** Immediately begin to serve soup

**Each Bowl Should Have:**
- A matzah ball
- A carrot

---

#### Buffet Service

**After Soup:**
- Clear soup bowls and spoons
- Open the buffet

**Buffet Items:**
1. Roast
2. Chicken
3. Kugel
4. Ratatouille
5. Sweet potato
6. 2 bowls of Caesar salad

**Ensure:** Everything has a serving spoon

---

#### Dessert Service

**Prepare Dessert:**

**Each Plate Should Have:**
- Fruit salad
- 2 ice cream cubes
- A teaspoon

**Then:**
- Serve dessert
- Clear dessert

---

### End of Service

**Final Tasks:**
1. Clear tables completely
2. Wash dishes if you have time

## For Shluchim: Planning Community Seder Service

### Staffing Considerations

**This Document Shows:**
- Need for dedicated waitress position
- Clear role differentiation from waiters
- Specific responsibilities for food preparation
- Timeline coordination essential

**Compare With:**
- File 1118: Waiter instructions (room setup, traditions, main timeline)
- File 1120: Overall service timeline (coordination between roles)

---

### Service Flow Design

**Key Elements:**

**1. Advance Preparation:**
- First course plated early
- Items prepared "upon arrival"
- Ready before guests enter

**2. Staggered Cooking:**
- 8:30 PM: Oven
- 9:30 PM: Sternos
- Allows for timing adjustments

**3. Sequential Service:**
- Lettuce/horseradish/apple first
- Wait for cue
- First course
- 10-minute wait
- Clear, then soup immediately
- Buffet opens after soup cleared
- Dessert after buffet

**4. Continuous Maintenance:**
- Bottles replenished throughout
- Washing stations reset after first washing
- Spoons added before serving

### Timing Analysis

**From Schedule:**
- 8:30 PM: Food to oven
- 9:30 PM: Light sternos
- First course: "On cue" (after lettuce service)
- Soup: 10 minutes after first course
- Buffet: Immediately after soup cleared
- Dessert: After buffet
- End: Tables cleared completely

**Estimated Duration:**
- Approximately 1.5-2 hours for full service
- Multiple courses with gaps for Seder rituals
- Coordination with Seder leader essential

---

### Staff Training Points

**What Waitress Needs to Know:**

**1. Seder Ritual Integration:**
- Understand hand washing tradition (twice)
- Know what "on cue" means
- Coordinate with Seder progression
- Don't rush ritual elements

**2. Food Safety:**
- Covering plated food
- Refrigeration timing
- Hot food temperature management
- Sterno safety ("only light one fire if food is hot")

**3. Quality Standards:**
- Each plate identical
- Basil dressing "drizzled"
- Spoons added before serving
- Everything has serving utensil

**4. Flexibility:**
- "On cue" service (not clock-based)
- "If you have time" dish washing
- Continuous bottle monitoring

### Scalability Considerations

**This Setup Suggests:**
- Multi-table event (mentions "2 bowls of Caesar salad")
- Buffet-style main course
- Plated appetizers and desserts
- Professional service standards

**For Smaller Events:**
- Reduce advance plating
- Family-style instead of buffet
- Simplified timing
- Fewer staff

**For Larger Events:**
- Multiple waitresses
- Assembly line plating
- Larger buffet
- More washing stations
- Additional coordination
