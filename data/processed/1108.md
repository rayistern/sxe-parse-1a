# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Miriam and the Tambourines - Women's Event Speech

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Speech/teaching for women's event

**Purpose:** Inspire Jewish women through Miriam's example

**Format:** Narrative speech with activity (tambourine decorating)

**Tone:** Personal, inspirational, empowering

**Audience:** Jewish women's gathering/event

---

## Opening: The Context

**She grew up surrounded:**
- By slavery
- By people in despair
- Everywhere: talk of grim future for her people

**But still:**
> "From a young age, she believed."

---

## Introducing Miriam

**Names/Titles:**
- Miriam
- Miriam the midwife
- Miriam, sister of Moses
- Miriam, the prophetess

---

## Miriam's Character from Youth

**Obvious Difference:**
> "From a young age, it was obvious that Miriam was different. She had a sharp tongue and was not afraid to use it."

### The Midwife Incident

**Pharaoh's Command:**
- Commanded Miriam and her mother
- Make sure all baby boys thrown into Nile

**Miriam's Response:**
> "It was Miriam who answered back saying he'd be punished for such an evil decree."

**Character Revealed:**
> "Miriam was not a victim of her circumstances - Miriam was in control."

---

## After the Ten Plagues

**The Scene:**
> "After the ten plagues, the Jews finally leave Egypt and witness an awesome miracle of the splitting of the sea."

---

## The Torah Verse

**Biblical Text:**
> "Now picture the scene; They are coming out of slavery. They had barely 12 hours to pack and leave Egypt. They traveled a week in the desert. They crossed the sea."

---

## The Question

**The Puzzle:**
> "Where did Miriam and the women have tambourines to dance with?"

**Think About It:**
> "Imagine packing in a rush - do you go searching for a tambourine?"

> "Imagine their life in slavery; were there such luxuries as a tambourine?"

---

## The Men's Response

**Look at the verse about the men:**
> "No mention of musical instruments."

---

## Where Did Women Get These?

**Modern Impossibility:**
> "Where did the women get these musical instruments? I doubt there was a Target in the desert that they could've stopped in to pick some up; I'm pretty certain that Amazon Prime and Google Express had not yet begun their operations."

**The Significance:**
> "This verse alone tells us everything we need to know about Miriam."

---

## Miriam's Greatness

**What We Know:**
- Miriam was great
- She was special
- Miraculous water supply in desert was in her merit
- When she was afflicted, entire community waited one whole week just for her

**Through This Verse:**
> "It is through this verse that we can grasp what was so great about Miriam."

---

## Miriam's Faith

**Despite Circumstances:**
> "While the women were embittered in slavery, seeing no future, Miriam's faith in G-d would not be diminished. She believed. She trusted. And she knew G-d had not abandoned them."

---

## What Miriam Did

**Boosting Morale:**
> "And so she would boost the morale of the women; reminding them of Hashem's promises. filling them with faith in Hashem."

**The Tambourines:**
> "And she encouraged them to make tambourines. Because after all, if they expect great miracles, they'd want to have instruments to accompany their singing of praise to Hashem"

---

## The Paradox

**Their Reality:**
> "And so while their conditions were terrible and the situation bleak, they made tambourines. And their faith in G-d was strengthened."

**When the Time Came:**
> "And when the time came to sing praise to Hashem, they were ready. They were prepared, thanks to Miriam."

---

## The Source of This Faith

**The Question:**
> "Where did this deep strength, a faith that defied logic, come from?"

**The Answer:**
> "It is the deep faith that is in me, in you; in every Jewish women. They all had it; only Miriam helped them tap into it."

---

## Miriam's Vision

**Ability to See:**
> "Miriam was able to see past the present difficulties; Miriam's faith helped her rise above."

---

## Why We Gather

**Purpose of This Event:**
> "And today, we are all gathered here, me, and each one of you, to celebrate that Faith. Because it's a strength that lies in each one of us, waiting to be tapped into."

---

## Three Mitzvot - Tapping Into Faith

### 1. Shabbat Candles

> "When a women lights shabbat candles, ushering in the holiness of Shabbat, she is reaching that deep place of unshakeable faith."

### 2. Mikvah

> "When a woman fulfills the mitzvah of mikvah, the guidelines of family purity, she is reaching that deep place of unshakeable faith."

### 3. Challah

> "When a woman bakes bread that is enough dough to recite a special blessing on the dough, she is reaching that deep place of unshakeable faith."

---

## When We're In Touch With Faith

**Then:**
> "And when we are in touch with that faith, miracles happen."

**During Miriam's Time:**
> "During Miriam's time; it was the exodus of Egypt. Their faith made them worthy of such miracles."

**In Our Times:**
> "In our times, it's our personal exoduses; our personal freedoms of overcoming challenges and staying positive."

---

## The Eternal Message of the Tambourine

**Torah's Emphasis:**
> "And it is the tambourine that gives us this message. The tambourine that the torah felt was so important that it needed to be included in the writing of the written Torah; the torah did not leave that detail for a commentary to add; no, G-d himself felt it important to include in the actual text."

**Why Important:**
> "Because the tambourines the women danced with hold an eternal message for every woman, of all generations."

---

## We Can All Be Miriam

**The Potential:**
> "We can all be Miriam. We can all rise above our challenges. We all have the potential; and it is up to us to use it."

---

## Celebration

**What We Celebrate:**
> "Today we are here to celebrate. To celebrate Miriam, to celebrate Jewish unity. To celebrate the Jewish woman; to celebrate me and to celebrate you."

---

## The Activity

**Tambourine Decorating:**
> "We will each decorate a tambourine today to take home - keep it as your reminder, your proof. You have the strength like Miriam did; keep the tambourine in sight, on display, as a reminder to never give up."

---

## Closing: The Final Redemption

**Looking Forward:**
> "And when the final redemption with the arrival of Moshiach occurs, may it be speedily, it will be me and each one of you, dancing with our tambourines, rejoicing in happiness, proclaiming to all; we believed. We did not give up. And we were ready."

---

## For Shluchim: Event Planning

### This Speech Works For

**Events:**
- Women's Passover event
- Pre-Passover gathering
- Tambourine decorating workshop
- Women's circle
- Mother-daughter event
- Community celebration

**Timing:**
- Weeks before Passover
- During Sefirat HaOmer
- Shavuot (women receiving Torah)
- Anytime celebrating Jewish women

---

### Structure Analysis

**Opening (1-5 minutes):**
- Context: slavery, despair
- Miriam's character
- Not a victim, in control

**Build-Up (5-10 minutes):**
- After ten plagues
- The verse
- The question: Where did tambourines come from?
- Modern impossibility (Target/Amazon)

**Climax (10-15 minutes):**
- Miriam's faith
- What she did for women
- Source of faith in all of us
- Three mitzvot connection

**Application (5-10 minutes):**
- Miracles then and now
- Torah's emphasis on tambourine
- We can all be Miriam
- Celebration

**Activity & Close (5-10 minutes):**
- Tambourine decorating
- Keep as reminder
- Final redemption vision

**Total:** 30-50 minutes depending on pace

---

### Preparation Needed

**Materials:**
- Plain tambourines (one per participant)
- Decorating supplies:
  - Paint, markers, stickers
  - Ribbons
  - Gems, sequins
  - Glue
  - Glitter (optional)

**Space:**
- Tables for decorating
- Covering for tables
- Good lighting
- Cleanup supplies

**Tech:**
- Microphone if large group
- Music (optional background during decorating)
- Perhaps video/images of tambourines

---

### Engagement Ideas

**Before Speech:**
- Display decorated tambourine samples
- Music as people arrive
- Name tags

**During Speech:**
- Call and response
- Ask rhetorical questions
- Pause for effect
- Eye contact

**During Activity:**
- Circulate among tables
- Share stories
- Encourage creativity
- No "right way" - personal expression
- Background music (uplifting)

**After Activity:**
- Photo opportunity with tambourines
- Circle dance with tambourines
- Sing together
- Share what tambourine means to each person

---

### Variations

**Shorter Version (20 minutes):**
- Focus on: Question, Answer, Application
- Quick activity or send home to decorate

**Longer Version (90 minutes):**
- Add: More Miriam stories
- Deeper dive into three mitzvot
- More elaborate decorating
- Dancing/singing session
- Food and socializing

**Family Version:**
- Include daughters
- Mother-daughter bonding
- Simplified concepts
- More hands-on, less lecture

---

### Marketing Angles

**Event Title Ideas:**
- "Tambourines and Tea"
- "Faith Like Miriam"
- "Never Give Up: A Women's Celebration"
- "Decorating Hope: Tambourine Workshop"
- "Miriam's Message"

**Promotion:**
- Emphasize: Inspiration, creativity, community
- Show: Sample decorated tambourines
- Promise: Take home meaningful reminder
- Highlight: Celebrate Jewish women

---

### Follow-Up

**After Event:**
- Photo sharing (with permission)
- Thank you email
- Remind them to display tambourine
- Before Passover: "Remember your tambourine!"
- Next year: "Bring your tambourine to Seder!"

**Long-Term:**
- Annual tradition
- Growing collection
- Community tambourine dance at event
- Alumni photos with tambourines

---

### Adaptations for Different Audiences

**Ba'alot Teshuvah:**
- Emphasize: You have this strength too
- Explain: Three mitzvot clearly
- Welcome: Questions
- Non-judgmental about practice

**Established Community:**
- Deeper: Chassidic concepts
- Reference: More sources
- Challenge: Specific applications
- Build: On existing knowledge

**Mixed Observance:**
- Universal: Faith and strength themes
- Accessible: Miriam's story
- Practical: Personal challenges
- Inclusive: All welcome

**Teens/Young Adults:**
- Modern: Amazon/Target references
- Relevant: Social media, peer pressure
- Interactive: More activity time
- Upbeat: Energy and music
