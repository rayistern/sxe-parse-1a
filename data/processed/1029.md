## Event Implementation

### Setting Up

**Display:**
- Project categories and points
- Or create physical board
- Visible to all participants
- Clear point values

**Teams:**
- Divide into teams
- Or individual play
- Assign team names
- Designate scorekeepers

---

### Game Flow

**Round Structure:**
1. Team selects category and point value
2. Host reads clue
3. Team/player responds in question form
4. Correct: Add points
5. Incorrect: Deduct points (or not)
6. Continue until board cleared

**Final Jeopardy:**
- One final question
- Teams wager points
- Written answers
- Reveal and score
- Determine winner

## Sample Question Structure

### Category: The Megillah Story

**$100:**
- Clue: This Queen refused to appear before the king
- Answer: Who is Vashti?

**$200:**
- Clue: Esther's uncle who raised her
- Answer: Who is Mordechai?

**$300:**
- Clue: The method Haman used to choose the date
- Answer: What is casting lots?

**$400:**
- Clue: The number of times the Megillah is read
- Answer: What is twice?

**$500:**
- Clue: The city where the Jews fasted for three days
- Answer: What is Shushan?

### Category: Purim Customs

**$100:**

- Answer: What is hamantaschen?

**$200:**

**$300:**
