## Implementation Tips

### For Events

**Games:**
- Select 3-5 games based on space and age range
- Print materials in advance
- Organize by difficulty level
- Have volunteers familiar with rules
- Keep score or offer participation prizes

**Crafts:**
- Calculate materials needed per child (+ 20% extra)
- Pre-organize supplies by station
- Have sample finished products on display
- Provide take-home bags
- Ensure adequate supervision

### For Programs

**Planning:**
- Review folder contents well in advance
- Test crafts before the event
- Ensure all materials are available
- Adapt projects to age group
- Consider time requirements

**Setup:**
- Print instructions for each station
- Organize supplies in bins/containers
- Label everything clearly
- Prepare cleanup materials
- Brief volunteers on each activity
