## For Shluchim: Choosing a Haggadah

### Considerations

**Audience:**
- **Beginners:** Tzivos Hashem (visual, balanced commentary)
- **Familiar Students:** Kehos (Rebbes' teachings)
- **Mixed Group:** "At our Rebbe's Seder Table" or Chaim Miller's
- **Budget Conscious:** Printable online version

**Visual Presentation:**
- Tzivos Hashem has pictures without being childish
- "Tzuras hadf" (visual form) helps orientation
- Clear print important for all ages

**Commentary Level:**
- Too much: People get distracted from Seder
- Too little: Miss teaching opportunities
- "Few little insights" = sweet spot

**Translation Style:**
- "Story telling style" makes it flow
- Easier to read aloud
- Feels natural, not stilted

---

### Combination Strategy

**From This Document:**
> "I also personally read beforehand tzvi friedmans haggadah and use some of his targum style translation to help the flow"

**Approach:**
- Use one Haggadah for participants
- Prepare from another for your teaching
- Combine best elements
- Smooth transitions between paragraphs

---

### Ordering Considerations

**Bulk Orders:**
- Contact publishers directly
- Chabad.org store for standard versions
- Check for Shluchim discounts
- Order early (well before Pesach)

**How Many:**
- Count expected guests
- Add 20% buffer
- Keep extras for next year
- Some guests take home (factor in)

**Mix and Match:**
- Can have different Haggadahs at different tables
- Leader uses one version
- Guests follow along
- Works if page numbers called out

---

### Printable Option Benefits

**When to Use:**
- Last minute guests
- Budget constraints
- Want to customize
- Test before bulk order
- One-time event

**How to Enhance:**
- Print on quality paper
- Bind or staple nicely
- Add cover page
