## Planning Tips

### Activities
- **Card Making Supplies**
  - Scrapbooking materials
  - Stamps and ink pads
  - Decorative papers
  - Embellishments
  - Card stock

### Food & Beverage
- **Wine Selection** - Choose quality kosher wines
- **Cheese Board** - Variety of fine cheeses
- **Accompaniments** - Crackers, fruit, nuts

### Program Flow
1. Welcome and mingling (7:00-7:15)
2. Introduction and ice breaker (7:15-7:30)
3. Card making workshop (7:30-8:30)
4. Discussion and D'var Torah (8:30-9:00)

### Marketing Points
- **Social** - Opportunity for women to connect
- **Creative** - Hands-on activity
- **Educational** - Meaningful Chanukah insights
- **Elegant** - Wine and cheese setting
- **Value** - Take home crafted items
- **Flexible** - Bring a friend pricing
