## The Concept

### How It Works

**Origins:**
> "Last year we started an idea at the Home Depot workshop and this year we extended it to every Chanukah event that we do..."

**Core Mechanism:**
Have people tag you or your wife or your Chabad with a picture of them lighting the menorah.

**Raffle System:**
- Every person who tags is entered into a raffle
- **8 nights, eight raffles**
- Different winner each night

**Daily Posts:**
Each morning post:
- A picture of a prize
- The name of the person who won

---

## Why This Works

### Engagement Benefits

**Direct Engagement:**
> "The people are loving it"

**Visual Proof:**
> "We get the Nachas (and the PR) of watching people actually lighting the menorahs"

**Bonus Nachas:**
> "The bonus is the extra nachas when members of other temples and their rabbi's are forced to like pictures (with you tagged in them) of them lighting."

---

### Viral Growth

**Organic Spread:**
> "Locally it has gone viral, people we don't even know are tagging us so they can be part of the contest"

**Why It Goes Viral:**
- Everyone wants to win
- Easy to participate
- Social proof (see others doing it)
- Fun and interactive
- Low barrier to entry
- Daily winners create urgency

---

## Implementation Guide

### Setup Requirements

**1. Decide on Prizes**
- 8 prizes (one per night)
- Varying value to keep interest
- Chanukah-themed or general prizes
- Post photo of each prize daily

**2. Create Contest Rules**
Post clear rules:
- Must tag [Your Chabad Name] in photo
- Photo must show menorah lighting
- One entry per night
- Winner announced each morning
- Must follow page to win

**3. Promote the Contest**
- Announce at every Chanukah event
- Post on Facebook before Chanukah
- Include in emails
- Mention at Home Depot workshop
- Tell everyone you give menorahs to

---

### Daily Process

**Night Of:**
- People light menorahs at home
- Take photos
- Tag your Chabad page
- Automatic entry

**Next Morning:**
- Review all tagged photos
- Select random winner (or judge on creativity)
- Post winner announcement with:
  - Winner's name
  - Their photo
  - Today's prize photo
  - Encouragement for tonight's lighting

**Why This Happens:**

- Facebook algorithm shows rabbi that Chabad is tagged
- Creates awareness in other communities
- Soft outreach to unaffiliated

**Strategic Value:**
- Extends your reach
- Non-confrontational visibility
- Social proof
- Algorithm boost

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Q: What if same people win repeatedly?**
A: Limit to one win per family, or weight selection toward new participants

**Q: What if no one tags us?**
A: Seed it with your own volunteers/staff/family. Once a few do it, others follow.

**Q: How do we verify they actually lit?**
A: Trust and assume good faith. Contest is about engagement, not policing.

**Q: What about privacy concerns?**
A: People choose to participate. They control what they post. You're not creating content, just receiving tags.

## Final Thoughts

**Why This Works:**

1. **Low Barrier** - Take photo, tag, done
2. **Daily Engagement** - 8 nights, 8 chances
3. **Social Proof** - See others participating
4. **Fun** - Raffle element adds excitement
5. **Authentic** - People actually lighting menorahs
6. **Viral** - Spreads organically
7. **Cross-Community** - Reaches other temples' members

**The Genius:**
You're not asking people to come to an event or commit time. You're asking them to share something they're already doing (hopefully). The contest gives them extra motivation to actually light AND to share publicly.

**Result:**
> "Locally it has gone viral, people we don't even know are tagging us so they can be part of the contest"

**Bottom Line:**
For a few hundred dollars in prizes, you get massive engagement, visual proof of impact, viral spread, and the nachas of watching your community light menorahs. Highly recommended!
