### Step 2: Search

**Directive:**
> "Search the house with a candle on Thursday night, April 21st. Then burn what you find on Friday morning."

## For Shluchim: Using This Format

### Structure Analysis

**12 Steps Work Because:**
- Manageable chunks
- Clear progression
- Each step discrete
- Comprehensive coverage
- Not overwhelming

**Order Makes Sense:**
1. Physical preparation (clean, search, buy/sell, cook)
2. Intellectual preparation (text, master seder, brush up)
3. Social preparation (kids, inspiration, invite)
4. Participation (guest at seder)
5. Spiritual essence (liberation)

---

### Customization

**Adapt to Your Community:**
- Change specific dates
- Update contact information
- Add local resources
- Modify links to your content

**Your Details:**
- Rabbi and rebbetzin names
- Organization name
- RSVP email/phone
- Local seder details

---

- 4 weeks before Passover
- As single comprehensive email

**Single Email:**

- Comprehensive reference
- Save and return to
- Printable checklist

**Email Series:**
- One step every 2-3 days
- Builds momentum
- Not overwhelming
- Regular engagement
