# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > Chanukah Events > Event Ideas] Chanukah Bus Delivers Joy to Senior Citizens

## Program Overview

Children of Norfolk, Virginia spread Chanukah joy to senior citizens at assisted living centers throughout the town, thanks to shluchim **Rabbi and Mrs. Levi and Rashi Brashevitzky**.

### What Made This Unique

The **Chanukah Party Bus** transported the children to their various destinations.

---

## Event Timeline

### 1. Pre-Departure (4:30 PM)
- Light dinner at the Chabad House
- Menorah lighting ceremony

### 2. Boarding the Bus
- Each child receives a **special Chanukah Party Kit**
- Children board the Chanukah Party Bus

### 3. Activities on the Bus
- Variety of Chanukah games and activities
- Chanukah video screening
- Chanukah music
- Using items from party kits

### 4. Stops at Three Assisted Living Centers

**At Each Location:**
- Rabbi Brashevitzky lights the Menorah
- Children sing Chanukah songs
- Dancing around the room
- Handing out Chanukah gelt and donuts to the seniors

---

## Things to Do and Games to Play on the Chanukah Party Bus

### 1. Song Practice
Practice singing the songs which you will sing with the seniors

### 2. Interactive Chanukah Story Telling
- Use puppets to tell the Chanukah story
- Have children make motions with their hands
- Children shout out lines, etc.

### 3. Chanukah Q&A Review
- Chanukah Question and Answer Review
- Distribute treats for correct answers

### 4. Video Screening
Watch a Chanukah video:
- Professor Pellah's Place
- Lights
- Miracle Lights
- Maccabees, etc.

### 5. Chanukah "Pass Up" Game
**How to Play:**
- Two sets of seat rows race each other
- Pass requested items to the front seat of the bus
- Examples: a candle, a receipt, a yellow shoelace, a latke, etc.
- **Tip:** Include some items in the Chanukah Party Kits as most children will only have their coats with them

### 6. Chanukah Bingo
- Use laminated Bingo boards with dry erase markers
- *Source: Mrs. Nechama Dina Hecht, Greenfield, Massachusetts*

### 7. "Broken Telephone" with Chanukah Phrases
- Classic telephone game with Chanukah-themed phrases
- *Source: Mrs. Nechama Dina Hecht, Greenfield, Massachusetts*

### 8. Chanukah Trivia
**How to Play:**
- Two sets of seat rows take turns
- Answer "yes" or "no" Chanukah questions
- Hold up "yes" and "no" cards
- **Tip:** Include "yes" and "no" cards in the Chanukah Party Kits
- *Source: Mrs. Nechama Dina Hecht, Greenfield, Massachusetts*

### 9. Chanukah Music
Listen to Chanukah music throughout the journey

### 10. Scavenger Hunt Clues
If your Chanukah Bus will be making multiple stops:
- Hide a slip of paper at each location
- Clue indicates where the next stop will be
- *Source: Mrs. Sarah Alevsky, New York, New York*

## Implementation Tips

### Planning Checklist

- [ ] Book party bus
- [ ] Coordinate with assisted living centers
- [ ] Prepare Chanukah Party Kits
- [ ] Print game materials (Bingo cards, yes/no cards, etc.)
- [ ] Purchase treats (gelt, donuts, etc.)
- [ ] Select Chanukah videos
- [ ] Create music playlist
- [ ] Prepare puppets for storytelling
- [ ] Plan scavenger hunt clues (if applicable)
- [ ] Brief children on songs and activities
- [ ] Send parent information and permission slips

### Safety Considerations

- All children must be accompanied by an adult
- Ensure bus has adequate seating and safety features
- Check assisted living center accessibility
- Plan for dietary restrictions
- Have first aid kit available
- Emergency contact information for all participants

### Engagement Strategies

- **Variety:** Mix active and quiet activities
- **Participation:** Ensure every child has a role
- **Inclusivity:** Activities suitable for different ages
- **Connection:** Help children understand the meaning behind the activities
- **Joy:** Keep the atmosphere fun and uplifting

### For the Seniors

- **Advance Notice:** Inform assisted living centers in advance
- **Accessibility:** Ensure performance area is accessible
- **Volume:** Consider hearing needs of seniors
- **Timing:** Choose appropriate time of day
- **Comfort:** Provide seating for seniors
- **Participation:** Invite seniors to sing along or share memories
