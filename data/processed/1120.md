## Service Timeline

### 7:40 PM - Hot Food Preparation

**MAKE PLATTERS** of hot food from sternos

**Important:** Remove all pans from fire before serving

**Items to Prepare:**

1. **Potato kugel** on 8 rectangle trays
   - 10 pieces per tray
   - With plastic fork

2. **Vegetables** in 8 bowls
   - With serving spoon

3. **Chicken** on 8 larger rectangle trays
   - About 8 pieces per tray
   - With plastic fork

4. **Meat** on the chicken tray
   - About 10 slices per tray

---

### 8:00 PM - Traditional Items Service

**TIMING:** As soon as people sit down from washing their hands

**BRING OUT:**

1. **LETTUCE**

2. **APPLE MIXTURE**
   - Add spoon before serving

3. **HORSERADISH**
   - Add spoon before serving

---

### 8:12 PM - Eggs Service

**Wait 10 minutes** (from 8:00)

**BRING OUT:**
- **EGGS**

---

### 8:15 PM - First Course Service

**Wait for cue**

**SERVE FIRST COURSE:**

1. **FISH PLATTERS**
   - Add forks before serving

2. **CUCUMBER SALAD BOWLS**
   - Add spoon before serving

3. **BEET SALAD BOWLS**
   - Add spoon before serving

---

### 8:25 PM - Clear First Course

**Wait 10 minutes** (from 8:15)

**CLEAR FIRST COURSE:**
- Including all other small bowls on the table

---

### 8:30 PM - Second Course Service

**Immediately** (after clearing first course)

**SERVE SECOND COURSE:**

1. **ROAST PLATTERS**

2. **CHICKEN PLATTERS**

3. **KUGEL PLATTERS**

4. **VEGETABLE PLATTERS**

---

### 8:45 PM - Clear Second Course

**Wait 15 minutes** (from 8:30)

**CLEAR SECOND COURSE**

---

### 8:50 PM - Dessert Service

**Immediately** (after clearing second course)

**SERVE DESSERT:**
- **CAKE PLATTERS**
- Add forks before serving

---

### 9:00 PM - Clear Dessert

**Wait 10 minutes** (from 8:50)

**CLEAR DESSERT**

---

### 9:05 PM - End of Service

**At the end of the service:**

**CLEAR TABLES COMPLETELY**

### Critical Timing Patterns

**Pattern Analysis:**

**Service Intervals:**
- 8:00 - Traditional items (lettuce, horseradish, apple)
- 8:12 - Eggs (+12 minutes)
- 8:15 - First course (+3 minutes / wait for cue)
- 8:25 - Clear (+10 minutes)
- 8:30 - Second course (immediately after clear)
- 8:45 - Clear (+15 minutes)
- 8:50 - Dessert (immediately after clear)
- 9:00 - Clear dessert (+10 minutes)
- 9:05 - Final table clearing (+5 minutes)

**Pattern:**
- Traditional items → 12 min → Eggs → 3 min → First course
- 10-minute eating interval
- Immediate clear and serve
- 15-minute eating interval for main course
- 10-minute dessert interval
- 5 minutes final clearing

**Total Service Time:** 1 hour 25 minutes (7:40 PM prep to 9:05 PM complete)

---

### Scaling This Model

**For 8 Tables (As Written):**
- 16 bowls/platters for items with "2 per table"
- 8 platters for items with "1 per table"
- Total traditional item bowls: 48 (16+16+16)
- Total course platters/bowls: 8-16 per course

**To Scale Up:**
- Multiply platters/bowls per table added
- Add staff proportionally (1 waiter per 3-4 tables)
- Consider multiple sterno stations
- May need staggered service

**To Scale Down:**
- Fewer total items
- Single server possible for 4 tables
- Simplified timing
- Family-style instead of individual table service

---

### Integration with Seder Ritual

**Why These Timings:**

**8:00 - Traditional Items:**
- Corresponds to karpas (vegetable dipping)
- People just washed hands (urchatz)

**8:12 - Eggs:**
- Part of traditional Seder plate items
- Used during Maggid

**8:15 - First Course (wait for cue):**
- Not clock-based but ritual-based
- Flexible for Seder progression
- "Cue" from Seder leader

**8:30 - Main Course:**
- Corresponds to Shulchan Orech (meal)
- After all Maggid/rituals complete
- Longer interval (15 min) reflects main meal

**8:50 - Dessert:**
- Before afikoman
- Or after afikoman depending on custom
- Keeps evening flowing
