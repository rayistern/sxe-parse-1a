## How to Adapt This Template

### Structure to Keep

**1. Enthusiastic Opening**
- Exclamation points
- Energetic language
- Celebratory tone

**2. Numbers First**
- Lead with impressive statistics
- "All of which are records"
- Quantify everything

**3. Break Down by Audience**
- Campus
- Seniors
- General community
- Public presence

**4. Include Testimonials**
- Quote from partner organization
- Specific and powerful
- Shows external validation

**5. Name Dignitaries**
- Municipal officials
- Media personalities
- Community leaders

**6. Links to Media**
- Videos
- Photos
- Full press releases

**Bottom Line:**
This press release format works because it leads with impressive numbers, breaks down impact by audience, includes powerful testimonials, and maintains an enthusiastic tone throughout. Adapt the structure to your community size and keep the energetic style. Send while Chanukah is still fresh in people's minds, and use it as both celebration of success and foundation for next year's growth. The numbers prove impact, the stories create connection, and the enthusiasm is contagious.
