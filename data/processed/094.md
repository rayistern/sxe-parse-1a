## Legal vs. Practical

### Know the Difference

**Legal Right:**
- What you're entitled to
- Constitutional protection
- Court precedent
- Theoretical permission

**Practical Reality:**
- What you can actually do
- Community relationships
- Political considerations
- Long-term presence

**Balance Both:**
- Assert rights when necessary
- But preserve relationships
- Pick battles strategically
- Think long-term
