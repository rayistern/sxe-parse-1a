## The Concept

### Background

In our area, the firemen go around to neighborhoods before December 25 giving out candy canes.

We have in our Chabad House a Fire Chief who asked if we could do something for Chanukah.

---

## Implementation

**Setup:**
- Put a Menorah on a Fire Truck
- Go around to neighborhoods giving out Chanukah Gelt and Dreidels

**Coordination:**
- Told people that we were coming around
- Set up a route
- Had people get their neighbors to come out to meet us

**Time:**
- Had two hours on the Fire Truck
- Able to visit six neighborhoods

---

## Results

**Attendance:**
> "In some neighborhoods we had over 100 people waiting for us to come."

**Impact:**
- Impressive sight with lights flashing
- Chanukah music playing over the loudspeakers
- Kiddush Hashem
- People loved it (who previously had only seen something like this for Dec. 25)

**Firemen Response:**
> "The firemen loved it and said that for next year we need to plan on doing it for more than one night"

**Outreach:**
> "It got us to meet a bunch of people we never would have met."

---

## Recommendation

**For Other Communities:**

If you have a similar system with firemen going around before December 25, **ask them if they would be willing to do something for Chanukah as well.**

---

## Additional Note from Sicha

**Important Halachic Point:**

> "I saw this in a sicha today, where the Rebbe says that an announcement should be made by the public lighting, that one is not yotze!"

**Meaning:**
At public menorah lightings, announce that this public lighting does not fulfill one's personal obligation to light at home.

**Why This Matters:**
- Ensures people understand they must light at home
- Prevents confusion about fulfilling mitzvah
- Educates about proper observance
- Maintains halachic integrity

## Key Takeaways

**What Worked:**
1. Fire department partnership
2. Neighborhood route planning
3. Advance notice to residents
4. Visual/audio impact (lights, music)
5. Giveaways (gelt, dreidels)
6. Meeting people in their neighborhoods

**Success Factors:**
- Fire Chief relationship
- Piggybacking on existing tradition (candy cane distribution)
- Community loved seeing familiar trucks for Chanukah
- Firemen wanted to do more next year
- Reached unaffiliated Jews where they live

**Halachic Responsibility:**
- Make announcement that public lighting doesn't fulfill personal obligation
- Educate about home lighting requirements
- Maintain proper halachic standards
