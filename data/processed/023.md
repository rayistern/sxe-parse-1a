## License for Parade

### When Required

According to many local ordinances, one must obtain a permit before a parade.

---

## Recommendation: Get a Permit

**If possible, instead of trying to get around this requirement, we recommend you get a permit.**

### Benefits:

**Safety:**
- Helps with the safety of the parade

**Police Coordination:**
- May make it easier to obtain a police escort

**Professional Appearance:**
- Makes the parade much more "official"
- Helps it run smoother

---

## Alternative: Police-Escorted Motorcade

### If Getting a Permit is Not Feasible

You can call it a **police-escorted motorcade/procession** similar to a funeral and get around some requirements that way.

**Authority:**
Transit police may have the authority to escort a procession without a permit.
