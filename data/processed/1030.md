## Games

### 1. Hamantash Hunt

**Group Size:** Big or Small

**Time:** Prep 1 hour, Activity 1 hour

**Concept:** Scavenger hunt to gather hamantaschen ingredients from around neighborhood or Chabad House

---

#### Ingredients Needed (Basic Recipe)

- 4 cups flour
- 4 eggs
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- A pinch of salt
- 1 cup of oil
- 3 tablespoons of warm water
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 1 cup sugar

---

#### Setup Options

**Community-Wide Hunt:**
- Choose 8 community members to volunteer
- Each receives one ingredient and next clue
- Station in different places
- Create sequential clues
- Teams or all together
- Arrange transportation

**Chabad House Hunt:**
- Hide ingredients around building
- No transportation needed
- Easier to manage
- Still engaging

---

#### Setting Up the Hunt

**Hiding Tips:**
- Not too easy or too hard
- Color code clues for each team
- Don't hide clue in place it points to!

**Organization Method:**
1. Label envelopes with hiding place names (Kitchen, bima, library)
2. Cut clues into strips
3. Mix up each team's strips (different paths)
4. Number each strip (Clue #1, #2, etc.)
5. In each envelope, place FOLLOWING clues
6. Hide envelopes at specified locations
7. First clue handed out at start
8. Test run recommended

---

#### Sample Clues

1. "If you're in a hungry mood, go here first and find some food." (Kitchen)
2. "This is where we read and roll, the parchment from the Torah scroll." (Bima)
3. "Java Java Java!" (Coffee machine)
4. "If you want to learn and grow, turn the page, get in the know." (Library/bookcase)
5. "Add some kindness to your days! Drop one in when you're on your way." (Pushka)
6. "Take a walk and step outside, this is where you go to ride." (Parking lot)
7. "Time to chill, time to think; please go here for a cold drink." (Soda machine)
8. "This place is packed with sounds of Torah and Aleph Bais every Sunday." (Hebrew school)
9. "You're almost at the very end! This is where you hang with friends." (Teen lounge)
10. "The final clue. The final prize. Hiding behind the place where you look to see your eyes." (Behind a mirror)

### 3. Post-it Game

**Group Size:** Large

**Time:** 14 minutes

**Materials:** Post-it notes, pens

**How To:**
1. Everyone writes name on Post-it, sticks on chest
2. Partner with someone, introduce yourself, state 3 facts
3. Partners switch name tags
4. Find new partner, introduce with OLD partner's name and facts
5. Continue switching identities
6. Circle up, state last person's facts
7. That person corrects if wrong

**Debrief:** "On Purim we wear costumes and change our identities!"

---

### 4. Masquerade Maze

**Group Size:** Large

**Time:** 12 minutes

**Materials:** Post-it notes or name tags, pens, tape

**Setup:**
- Circle formation
- Everyone writes Purim costume name on tag
- Facilitator redistributes randomly
- Place tag on back of person to your right

**How To:**
1. On "go," find your own name tag on someone's back
2. Avoid others finding tag on YOUR back
3. Grab your tag when found, place on chest
4. Stay in game until you find yours AND person whose name was on your back finds theirs

---

### 5. Purim Story Rap Off

**Duration:** 10 minutes

**Group Size:** Any

**How To:**
- Split into teams
- Each team picks song (preferably rap) with catchy beat
- Rewrite as Purim song
- Perform as rap
- Girls: spoken word (not singing)
- Optional: Perform Purim story while rapping if can't create new lyrics

---

### 6. Draw on Your Head - "V'Nahafochu"

**Materials:** Sturdy paper plates, pens

**Instructions:**
1. Put paper plate on head
2. Draw without looking (following instructions)
3. Trace your hands
4. Draw 2 hamantaschen in one hand
5. Draw gragger in other hand
6. Write CTEEN

**Scoring:**
- 2 points if hamantaschen inside hand
- 2 points for each hand with 5 fingers
- 3 points if hamantaschen same size
- 1 point if CTEEN all same level

**Highest points wins!**

## Crafts

### 1. Purim Photo Booth Props

**Duration:** 45 minutes

**Group Size:** Large

**Materials:**
- Photo prop templates
- Printer paper
- Matboard or cardstock
- X-acto knife or boxcutter
- Scissors
- Hot glue gun
- Wooden sticks

**Prep:**
- Print templates on printer paper
- Set up photo booth with good lighting
- Assign teen photographer

**How To:**
1. Trace templates onto colored/patterned matboard
2. Cut with scissors (large shapes) and X-acto (details)
3. Hot glue to wooden sticks (along edge so stick doesn't show)
4. Let dry
5. Place in box/basket next to photo booth
6. Take fun pictures!

---

### 2. DIY Masquerade Masks

**Materials:**
- Mask base
- Scissors
- Lace or other fabric
- Spray adhesive
- Feathers
- Rhinestones
- ¼" dowel rod (12" length)
- Colorful ribbon
- Glue gun

**How To:**
1. Determine mask shape
2. Arrange fabric covering until satisfied with pattern
3. Spray back of fabric with adhesive
4. Attach piece by piece to mask
5. Let dry
6. Add embellishments (feathers, rhinestones)
7. Wrap stick with ribbon diagonally
8. Secure ends with hot glue
9. Attach stick to mask with hot glue
10. Cover attachment with fabric scrap

### 3. S'more Hamantashen

**Graham Cracker Dough:**
- 2½ cups plus 2 Tbsp flour
- 1 cup dark brown sugar, packed
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¾ tsp salt
- 7 Tbsp margarine (frozen, cubed)
- ⅓ cup honey
- 5 Tbsp almond/soy milk
- 2 Tbsp vanilla

**Filling:**
- 12 Tbsp marshmallow fluff
- 2-3 oz finely chopped chocolate

**Note:** Chill dough 2 hours or overnight

### 4. Stained Glass Hamantashen

**Yields:** 4 dozen

**Special Ingredient:** 25 hard candies or lollipops

**Concept:** Crushed candy melts in oven creating "stained glass" effect in center

**Ingredients:**
- ½ cup butter/margarine, softened
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt

**Method:** Crush candy separately by color, spoon onto dough centers before folding

**Bake:** 6-8 minutes at 350°F until candy melted

### 5. Hamantaschen Sushi!

**Creative Twist:** Triangular onigiri (rice balls) styled as hamantaschen

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups warm cooked sushi rice
- Nori strips (1"-1½")
- Fillings of choice
- Toasted sesame seeds

**Method - Using Rice Mold:**
1. Rinse mold, fill halfway with rice
2. Make indent in center
3. Add filling (squeeze out liquid)
4. Cover with more rice
5. Close lid and press
6. Release from mold
7. Wrap nori around sides
8. Use paper triangle template to sprinkle sesame seeds on top

**Alternative:** Hand-form triangles

**Rice molds:** Available at Asian markets or Amazon
