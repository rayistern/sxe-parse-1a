## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Purpose:** Simple Passover desserts

**Format:** Recipe cards

**Recipes:** (1) Ices - three layer or one layer, (2) Nut Cake

---

## Recipe 1: Ices (Three Layer or One Layer)

### Base Ingredients

- **¾ cup sugar**
- **1 ¾ cups orange juice**
- **½ cup lemon juice**

---

### Fruit Options (Add Either):

**Option A: Mango**
- 4 mango

**Option B: Pineapple**
- 1 pineapple

**Option C: Papaya**
- 1 papaya

**Option D: Kiwi**
- 8 kiwi

---

### Instructions

1. **Blend** sugar, orange juice, lemon juice, and chosen fruit

2. **Freeze**

3. **Blend again** - it gets creamy

4. **For Single Layer:**
   - Serve after second blend

5. **For Layered Version:**
   - Freeze again after blending
   - Make second flavor
   - Layer on top of first
   - Repeat for third layer if desired

---

### Tips for Layering

**Popular Combinations:**
- Strawberry + Mango + Kiwi
- Pineapple + Mango + Kiwi
- Any three contrasting colors

**Technique:**
- First layer must be fully frozen before adding next
- Smooth surface for even layering
- Use clear container or bundt pan for visual effect
- Can add thin layer of orange juice between for separation

## Recipe 2: Nut Cake

### Ingredients

- **12 eggs**
- **1 ¼ cups sugar**
- **3 cups almond, crushed**

---

### Instructions

1. **Beat eggs and sugar** for at least 10 minutes
   - This is critical for texture
   - Should be light and fluffy
   - Color will lighten significantly

2. **Fold in crushed almonds**
   - Gently fold, don't overmix
   - Maintain air in egg mixture

3. **Bake at 325°F** for 1 hour 10 minutes
   - Until golden brown
   - Toothpick should come out clean
   - Top should be firm to touch

---

### Tips for Nut Cake

**Crushing Almonds:**
- Food processor until fine
- Not too fine (some texture good)
- Can use almond flour/meal
- Ground almonds from store work well

**Beating Eggs:**
- Use stand mixer if available
- Full 10 minutes minimum
- Don't skip this step
- Creates cake structure (no flour)

**Serving:**
- Delicious plain
- Can dust with powdered sugar (not Passover) or cinnamon
- Serve with berries
- Pair with sorbet/ices above

**Ices:**

- **Only 3 ingredients** (incredibly simple)
- **Gluten-free naturally** (no flour at all)
- **Elegant** (suitable for Seder dessert)
- **Pareve** (versatile)
- **Not too sweet** (sophisticated flavor)
- **Make ahead** (day before fine)
- **Slices beautifully** (holds shape well)

### Scaling for Events

**Ices for 50 People:**
- Make 3-4 bundt pans with 3 layers each
- Or 2 large 9x13 pans per flavor
- Total: 6-8 pans
- Multiply base recipe by 6-8
- Start 3 days before event

**Nut Cake for 50 People:**
- Recipe makes approximately 16 servings
- Need 3-4 cakes
- Triple or quadruple recipe
- Bake in multiple pans simultaneously
- Make 1-2 days before (improves with sitting)

### Flavor Variations

**Ices:**

**Beyond the Four Listed:**
- Strawberry (3 cups)
- Raspberry (4 cups)
- Blueberry (4 cups)
- Mixed berry
- Peach (4 medium)
- Passion fruit (pulp of 8)

**Texture Adjustments:**
- More sugar = softer ice
- Less sugar = harder, icier
- More fruit = creamier
- Banana addition = extra creamy

---

**Nut Cake:**

**Almond Variations:**
- Half almonds, half walnuts
- Pecans instead of almonds
- Mix of different nuts
- Add 1 tsp almond extract

**Flavor Additions:**
- 1 tsp cinnamon to almonds
- Zest of 2 oranges
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder (for chocolate version)

---

### Time Management

**Ices Timeline:**

**3 Days Before Event:**
- Make and freeze first layer

**2 Days Before:**
- Blend first layer (creamy)
- Re-freeze
- Make and freeze second layer

**1 Day Before:**
- Blend second layer
- Layer on first
- Make and freeze third layer

**Day Of:**
- Blend third layer
- Layer on second
- Keep frozen until service

---

**Nut Cake Timeline:**

**2 Days Before:**
- Can make cake
- Wrap well when cool
- Store at room temperature

**1 Day Before:**
- Best time to make
- Flavor develops
- Texture sets

**Day Of:**
- Slice and plate
- Add garnishes
- Serve room temperature

---

### Common Issues

**Ices:**

**Problem: Too Hard/Icy**
- Solution: More sugar or more fruit next time
- Fix: Let sit at room temp 10 minutes before serving

**Problem: Not Creamy After Second Blend**
- Solution: May need more powerful blender
- Fix: Partially thaw before blending

**Problem: Layers Mix Together**
- Solution: Ensure first layer completely frozen
- Fix: Freeze each layer longer

---

**Nut Cake:**

**Problem: Cake Falls/Deflates**
- Solution: Beat eggs full 10 minutes
- Also: Fold almonds gently (don't mix hard)
- Check: Oven temperature accurate

**Problem: Too Dense**
- Solution: Beat eggs longer
- Also: Don't overmix after adding almonds

**Problem: Dry**
- Solution: Don't overbake
- Fix: Serve with sauce or cream
