### When There IS a Problem

**Sanctioning Positions:**
> "It is only if by your presence you might be sanctioning their position as rabbis by doing panels together etc.. and only with rabbis getting perspectives of the so called three branches. By doing so you are tacitly agreeing that there can be a different peirush to the Torah than the Halacha."

**Where the Problem Lies:**
- Doing panels with Reform rabbis
- Being on programs about "three branches"
- Any format that legitimizes Reform rabbinic authority
- Tacit agreement to "different interpretations" of Torah

---

### When There's NO Problem

**This Case:**
> "Here there is none of that."

**Why It's Different:**
- Just a public menorah lighting
- Not a panel or discussion
- Not presenting "three perspectives"
- Not legitimizing Reform rabbinate
- Simple community event

## Key Distinctions

### JCC is NOT Reform

**Important Understanding:**
- JCCs are generally not Reform-affiliated organizations
- Community centers, not synagogues
- Neutral communal space
- Different from Reform temple

**Verification:**
Check www.uahc.org to see if specific JCC is Reform-affiliated

---

### Event Type Matters

**Problematic:**
- Panel discussions on "Jewish perspectives"
- Three branches of Judaism programs
- Anything implying equal validity of movements
- Legitimizing Reform clergy as "rabbis"

**Generally Acceptable:**
- Community menorah lighting
- Cultural event
- Holiday celebration
- Neutral communal gathering

---

## Decision Framework

### Questions to Ask

**1. Who is Involved?**
- JCC president (lay person) ✓ Generally OK
- Reform rabbi ⚠️ Requires careful assessment

**2. What is the Format?**
- Community menorah lighting ✓ Generally OK
- Panel/discussion with Reform clergy ✗ Problematic

**3. How is it Framed?**
- Community holiday event ✓ OK
- "Three branches perspective" ✗ Not OK
- Your menorah, they attend ✓ OK
- Joint program with equal billing ⚠️ Depends

**4. What's the Message?**
- Celebrating Chanukah together ✓ OK
- Legitimizing different Torah interpretations ✗ Not OK

## Possible Approaches

### Option 1: Yes, With Parameters

**If You Partner:**
- You lead/organize
- Your menorah, your rabbi
- They attend and help promote
- Clear it's a community (not Reform) event
- No joint billing with Reform rabbi
- Avoid language of "partnership between movements"

---

### Option 2: Polite Decline

**If You Don't Partner:**
- Thank them for interest
- Invite them to attend your event
- Offer to help them separately
- Maintain cordial relations
- Don't burn bridges

---

### Option 3: Alternative Collaboration

**Middle Ground:**
- They promote your event
- Use their mailing list
- JCC space (if appropriate)
- They attend, you organize
- No official "partnership"

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

**Never:**
- Joint program with Reform rabbi as co-equal
- Panel on "perspectives on Chanukah"
- Anything implying Reform is legitimate stream
- Format that legitimizes Reform clergy
- Language of "three branches"

**Warning Signs:**
- They want their rabbi to speak
- Suggest "different perspectives"
- Want equal billing
- Frame as interfaith/inter-movement dialogue
