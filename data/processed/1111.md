# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Splitting of the Sea - 7th Day Teaching

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Teaching for 7th day of Passover

**Source:** Shluchim Exchange forum

**URL:** http://www.shluchimexchange.com/en/forum/viewtopic.php?t=546162

**Purpose:** Explain significance of splitting the sea miracle

**Audience:** Classes, divrei Torah, 7th day events

---

## The Question

**What We Celebrate:**
> "On the seventh day of Passover, we celebrate the miracle of the splitting of the sea."

**The Deeper Question:**
> "In Kabbalah, as well as in Chassidus, the question is asked as to why the necessity for such a grand miracle."

**Simple Explanations Insufficient:**
> "Were it to be just to save the Jewish people, or to kill the Egyptians, G-d had many other ways to do this."

---

## The Miracle Will Repeat

**Isaiah (-11:15):**
> "And the Lord shall dry up the tongue of the Egyptian Sea, and He shall lift His hand over the river with the strength of His wind, and He shall beat it into seven streams, and He shall lead [the exiles] with shoes."

**The Question:**
> "What does splitting water and passing through it have to do with the redemption from Egypt and then the final redemption?"

---

## Connection to Receiving Torah

### G-d's Promise to Moses

**When Told of Exodus:**
> "When G-d told Moses that G-d was going to take the Jewish people out of Egypt, G-d said, 'When you take the people out of Egypt, you will worship G-d on this mountain.'"

**Meaning:**
> "Everything which happened from leaving Egypt until receiving the Torah at Mt. Sinai, was happening either to bring the Jewish people out of Egypt, or to bring them to receiving the Torah."

**Purpose of Crossing the Sea:**
> "Crossing through the sea was for the purpose of bringing us to receiving the Torah."

---

## When Moshiach Comes

**Mishneh Torah, Laws of Teshuvah, Chapter 9, Law 2:**
> "He [Moshiach] will teach the entire nation and instruct them in the path of G-d."

**Then Too:**
> "The crossing of the waters will be in preparation of us receiving this new and deeper form of Torah knowledge."

---

## Two Levels: Hearing vs. Seeing

### Moses - Power of Hearing

**What Moses Brought:**
> "In Kabbalah we are taught that Moses brought us the power of hearing G-d within the Torah, as Moses said (-Deuteronomy 4:1), 'And now, O Israel, listen to the statutes.'"

**Moses's Request and Limitation:**
> "When Moses asked (-Exodus 33:18-22), 'Show me, now, Your glory!,' G-d responded with, 'You will not be able to see My face, for man shall not see Me and live.'"

---

### Moshiach - Power of Seeing

**Higher Level:**
> "Moshiach will bring us a higher level in which we will see G-d within His Torah, as the verse states (-Isaiah 40:5), 'And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh together shall see that the mouth of the Lord spoke.'"

**Therefore:**
> "When Moshiach comes, we will again go through the split waters."

---

## Sea Creatures vs. Land Creatures

### Kabbalistic Meaning

**Sea Creatures:**
> "According to Kabbalah and Chassidus, [sea creatures] represent the Hidden Worlds, such as the angels, which live within a conscientiousness of G-d being their life-force, with no Evil Inclination."

**Land Creatures:**
> "[Land creatures] represent the Revealed Worlds, in which we have an Evil Inclination, and often aren't conscious of G-d being our sole life-force."

---

### Hidden vs. Revealed Worlds

**Hidden Worlds:**
- Heaven
- Spiritual realms
- Conscious of G-d constantly
- No evil inclination

**Revealed Worlds:**
- Physical world (ultimate)
- Have evil inclination
- Often not conscious of G-d as sole life-force

---

## G-d's Desire: Torah in Physical World

**Specific Intent:**
> "G-d's desire was specifically to give the Torah to us physical people, souls within bodies, within a physical world."

**Superiority of Physical Mitzvot:**
> "Even more so, our sages tell us that the spiritual mitzvot that were performed by our forefathers are considered like a passing fragrance, while our physical mitzvot are the essence of Torah and mitzvot."

**Ultimate Experience:**
> "Thus, the desire of G-d is that we have the ultimate experience of G-d conscientiousness specifically here in the physical world."

---

## Soul and Body

**Another Layer:**
> "In some teachings, our soul represents the Hidden World, while our body represents the Revealed World, and G-d's desire is that the soul descend into the body, and only then receive the ultimate revelation, through G-d giving us the Torah."

---

## Breaking Down the Barrier

**What Had to Happen:**
> "However, in order for this to happen, the barrier between Heaven and Earth, between the Hidden World and the Revealed World, would have to come down."

**The Process:**
> "The land creatures of the Revealed World would have to first be able to walk through waters of the Hidden World, purifying them and opening them up to hearing G-d at Mount Sinai, and then again to be purified and opened by crossing the waters, in order to seeing G-d, when Moshiach comes."

---

## For Shluchim: Using This Teaching

### 7th Day Application

**Perfect For:**
- 7th day of Passover classes
- Evening of 7th day farbrengen
- Day of 7th day study session
- Pre-Shavuot connection

**Why 7th Day:**
- Celebrates splitting of sea
- Connects Exodus to Sinai
- Links past redemption to future
- Prepares for Shavuot (receiving Torah)

---

### Teaching Structure

**Opening (5 minutes):**
- Question: Why such grand miracle?
- G-d had other ways to save/destroy
- Must be deeper purpose

**Development (10-15 minutes):**
- Connection to receiving Torah
- Everything between Exodus and Sinai
- Crossing sea prepared us
- Moshiach will repeat (Isaiah)

**Core Concepts (10-15 minutes):**
- Moses: Hearing G-d (Deuteronomy 4:1)
- Moshiach: Seeing G-d (Isaiah 40:5)
- Sea creatures: Hidden Worlds (angels, no evil inclination)
- Land creatures: Revealed Worlds (us, evil inclination)
- G-d's desire: Torah in physical world

**Application (5-10 minutes):**
- Breaking down barrier
- Land creatures walk through waters
- First time: Prepared to hear (Sinai)
- Second time: Prepared to see (Moshiach)
- We are that process today

---

### Discussion Questions

**Understanding the Miracle:**
1. Why wasn't a simpler miracle sufficient?
2. What's unique about passing through water?
3. How does physical action prepare for spiritual revelation?

**Hearing vs. Seeing:**
1. What's the difference between hearing and seeing G-d?
2. Why was Moses limited to hearing?
3. How will Moshiach enable seeing?

**Hidden vs. Revealed:**
1. What are examples of Hidden Worlds today?
2. How do we experience Revealed Worlds?
3. Why is physical world superior despite evil inclination?

**Personal Application:**
1. How do we break barriers in our lives?
2. Where do we need to "cross waters"?
3. What prepares us to receive Torah more deeply?
