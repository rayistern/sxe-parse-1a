## Activities & Entertainment

### Wine Tasting

**Italian Connection:**
- Italy renowned for wine
- Sophisticated activity
- Adult-oriented
- Educational element

**Setup:**
- Multiple Italian wine varieties
- Tasting notes provided
- Sommelier or knowledgeable guide
- Small pours
- Cheese/bread accompaniment

**Purim Connection:**
- Wine central to Purim celebration
- "Ad d'lo yada" tradition
- Festive drinking
- Elevated experience

---

### Glass Blowing

**Italian Tradition:**
- Murano glass famous
- Venice specialty
- Artistic demonstration
- Cultural education

**Implementation Options:**

**Demonstration:**
- Professional glass blower
- Live performance
- Explain technique
- Audience watches
- Q&A session

**Hands-On Workshop:**
- Small group participation
- Simple project
- Supervised by professional
- Take-home creation
- More expensive

**Practical Considerations:**
- Requires special equipment
- Safety concerns
- Heat/fire involved
- Professional needed
- Higher budget item

---

### Mafia Game

**Italian-American Connection:**
- Social deduction game
- "Mafia" theme from Italian-American culture
- Popular party game
- Engaging for groups

**How to Play:**
- Players assigned roles (Mafia, citizens, detective)
- Night and day phases
- Mafia "eliminates" citizens
- Citizens try to identify Mafia
- Discussion and voting
- Exciting and interactive

**Why It Works:**
- No props needed
- Large group game
- Creates excitement
- Social interaction
- Mystery element (Purim = hidden miracle)

---

### Travel to Italy Game

**Concept:**
- Board game or quiz format
- Journey through Italian regions
- Trivia about Italy
- Cultural education

**Possible Elements:**
- Italian geography questions
- Famous landmarks
- Italian Jewish history
- Food trivia
- Art and culture
- Language basics

**Format:**
- Team-based
- Individual competition
- Family-style play
- Prizes for winners

---

### Mimes

**Italian Pantomime:**
- Classic Italian street performance
- Silent comedy
- Physical humor
- All ages entertainment

**Performance Ideas:**
- Purim story as pantomime
- Interactive with audience
- Comedy sketches
- Character entertainment
- Photo opportunities

**Why Mimes:**
- Visual humor (no language barrier)
- Engaging for kids
- Can be done by volunteers
- Minimal props
- Flexible timing

**Lower Budget:**

- Travel to Italy quiz (DIY)

**Moderate Budget:**
- Wine tasting (moderate - depends on wines)

## Props and Materials

- Tasting note cards

**Glass Blowing:**

**Mafia Game:**
- Role cards (can be homemade)
- Nothing else needed!

- Small prizes

## Safety Considerations

### Wine Tasting

**Responsible Service:**
- Monitor consumption
- Provide transportation info
- Non-alcoholic options
- Food with alcohol
- Age verification

---

### Glass Blowing

**Safety First:**
- Professional only
- Maintain distance
- Proper ventilation
- Fire extinguisher nearby
- Clear safety perimeter
- Supervise children closely
