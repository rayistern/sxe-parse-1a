**Bottom Line:**
Free DIY building plans available on Shluchim Exchange for both car menorah (mobile/parade use) and 6-foot wooden menorah (permanent/semi-permanent installation). Building your own offers significant cost savings ($250-500 materials vs. $800-2,500 commercial), complete customization (size, style, materials), and community engagement opportunity (volunteer build day, skill sharing, team bonding). Wood menorah requires basic carpentry skills (measuring, cutting, drilling, finishing) and takes 3-4 days including finishing/drying time. Car menorah needs mechanical and electrical skills for secure vehicle mounting, wind-resistant design, and safe attachment that won't shift while driving. Typical materials for wood version: 4x4 posts, 2x4 boards, plywood, hardware, paint/sealant ($250-400 total). Safety critical for both construction (eye protection, proper tools) and installation (stable base, weather resistance, public codes). Consider group build project for faster completion, shared skills/costs, and educational value. Document with photos and notes for improvements next year. Alternative if DIY not feasible: borrow, rent, buy used, or start small and upgrade later—but DIY investment pays off in subsequent years through reuse with only minor repairs needed.
