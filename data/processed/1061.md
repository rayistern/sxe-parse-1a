## Note: Purim Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Operational guidelines for Purim outreach (variant version)

**Language:** English

**Purpose:** Practical reference for Shluchim conducting Mivtza Purim

**Note:** This is a variant of File 1059 with similar content but slightly different wording and organization

## General Preparations

### Timeline

**30 Days Before Purim:**
- Begin all mivtza purim preparations
- Advance planning essential
- Coordinate resources

**Key Preparations:**
1. Ba'al koreh for Megillah
2. Ba'al koreh for Torah reading on Purim day (if possible)
3. Mishloach manos packages
4. Coins for matanot l'evyonim
5. Purim guide

---

### Universal Reach

**Target Audience:**
> "Every jew, including children that may not have reached the age of chinuch, should be given the opportunity to fulfill the mitzvas of purim."

**Priority Locations:**
- Orphanages
- Old age homes
- Hospitals
- Prisons
- Army bases

**Rationale:**
- Reach isolated populations
- Bring mitzvot to those in need
- Maximum communal impact

---

### Connection to Other Mivtzoim

**Opportune Time:**
- Remind about other mivtzoim
- Specifically Mivtza Torah
- Specifically Mivtza Tefillin
- Purim atmosphere conducive to engagement

**Overarching Goal:**
> "All details in the shlichus of spreading torah, yiddishkeit and chassidus, need to be permeated with this point - how this leads to kabolas moshiach tzidkeinu."

---

## Special Emphasis: Mishloach Manos and Matanot L'evyonim

### Why These Mitzvot Are Critical

**Rebbe's Teaching:**
> "Special emphasis should be placed on the mitzvahs of matonos loevyonim and mishloach manos, being that they are fundamental to the theme of purim and at the same time most neglected."

**Paradox:**
- Easiest to fulfill
- Most fundamental
- Yet most neglected

**Why Neglected:**
- Yetzer hara specifically interferes
- People assume they're less important
- Focus shifts to more "exciting" mitzvot
- Youth especially neglected

---

### Matanot L'evyonim - Simple Fulfillment

**Rebbe's Example:**
- Even children can fulfill easily
- Give 2 cents to 2 evyonim
- 1 cent to each
- *Nowadays: 5 cents to each*

**Key Message:**
- Extremely accessible
- No excuse not to fulfill
- Remove all barriers
- Focus on participation, not amount

---

### Mishloach Manos - Simple Fulfillment

**Rebbe's Example:**
- Even children can fulfill easily
- Apple and candy
- Or piece of cake and soda or juice
- Simple items sufficient

**Key Message:**
- Don't overcomplicate
- Two basic items enough
- Fulfillment over extravagance
- Accessible to all

---

## Megillah Reading Challenge

### Common Problem

**Reality:**
- Megillah reading is highlight for many
- Also the most difficult mitzvah
- Ba'al koreh often pressured to lein fast
- Words swallowed or mispronounced
- Result: No one is yotze

**Note in Document:**
> [הלשון "לוותר" צ"ע. אף שאין יוצאים וכו הרי כדאי לכתוב בלשון חיובי שחייבים להשתדל שישמעו כל מילה ואם הזמן והמקום אין מאפשרים יצא שכרו בהפסדו. או משהו כזה]

**Translation of Note:**
- The language "to forgo" is questionable
- Even though not yotze, better to write positively
- Must try hard that everyone hears every word
- If time and place don't allow, reward is in the effort itself

---

### Better Approach

**If Quality Can't Be Ensured:**
- May be better to forgo Megillah reading
- Focus on other mitzvot instead
- Invalid reading worse than no reading
- People can fulfill elsewhere

**However:**
- Make maximum effort for quality
- Slow down despite pressure
- Invest in proper ba'al koreh
- Every word must be heard

---

## Megillah Reading - Detailed Halachot

### Night vs Day Choice

**If Choosing One:**
- Preferable: day option
- Allows other mitzvot proper fulfillment

---

### Night Reading Timing

**Primary Time:**
- From tzeis hakochavim
- Until alos hashachar
- Should NOT read after alos
- Exception: B'shas hadchak godal until netz hachama

**Early Reading Leniency:**
- If absolutely cannot read after tzeis
- Can rely on opinions:
  - After shkia
  - Even from plag hamincha (in shas hadchak)
- Read with bracha

**Important Detail:**
> "According to the Alter Rebbe (and the Gra) plag is earlier than what is often written in calendars."

---

### Who Should Wait

**Shliach and Family:**
- Anyone able to wait till after tzeis
- Should have in mind NOT to be yotze
- Even if need to eat beforehand (after fast)
- Then hear properly later

**Eating Before Megillah:**
- If being yotze while after fast
- Or difficult to read without eating
- Weak person can eat snack

**Snack Definition:**
- Any non-alcoholic drink
- Fruit or vegetable
- Less than k'beitza of mezonos
- If insufficient: eat more if designate shomer (someone to remind)

---

### Day Reading Timing

**Primary Time:**
- From netz until shkia

**Before Netz Leniency:**
- Can begin after alos

**After Shkia:**
- If reading after shkia
- Or estimate will finish after
- Read without bracha

---

### Brachos When Already Yotze

**Standard Procedure:**
- Ask listener to stand and say brachos for everyone
- If not possible: say yourself
- Or have person repeat after you like by tefillin etc.

**Same Applies:**
- If reading only for women
- But for women: bracha is "lishmoa megilla"
- If said "al mikrah megila" - still yotze

---

### Bracha After Reading

**Requirement:**
- Only if minyan present
- *Yish omrim:* Say without shem hamalchus
- [ובכף החיים]
- Women can be counted for this

---

### Multiple Readers

**If Needed:**
- Reading can be divided
- More than 1 ba'al koreh
- Helpful for stamina
- Maintains quality

---

### Technology

**NOT Valid:**
- Microphone
- Telephone
- Zoom
- And the like
- Must be direct voice

---

### Doubtful Listeners

**If All Listeners:**
- Doubt whether will listen to whole reading
- Due to illness, tiredness etc.
- Brachos should NOT be said

---

### Separation Requirements

**Private Home - One Family:**
- No need for separation concerns

**Public Place:**
- Work place, school and the like
- Should ensure separate seating

**Shul or Chabad House:**
- Any official program
- MUST have mechitza

---

## Matanot L'evyonim - Detailed Halachot

### Tzedaka Boxes

**If No Evyonim to Give To:**
- Prepare tzedaka boxes
- Distribute later

**Emphasis to Community:**
- Preferable: money reach evyon on Purim itself
- Easy nowadays via internet
- Halachically: yotze by putting in pushka for later delivery

---

### Minimum Amount

**Basic Requirement:**
- Value of prutah (approximately 5 cents)
- To each evyon

---

### Special Locations

**Prisons or Special Institutions:**
- Not allowed to handle money
- Explain: Yid won't keep money
- Will immediately insert into pushka

**If They Don't Agree:**
- Have someone else pick up money for yid
- Acquire it for him
- Have yid make you personal shliach
- Give money for him

---

### Where Money Goes

**Actual Evyon:**
- Should be given to real poor person
- Not to other worthy institutions
- Exception: If mosad makes meals for needy

**Practical Setup:**
- 2 boxes:
  1. Matanot l'evyonim (divided among evyonim)
  2. General donations

**If Shliach Is Evyon:**
- No sufficient set salary
- No sufficient set donations
- Minimal needs not met
- Considered evyon
- May use money for family

---

### Timing

**Primary Time:**
- Until shkia

**B'dieved:**
- Until tzeis

---

## Mishloach Manos - Detailed Halachot

### Everyone Participates

**Important:**
> "It is important to remember that the mitzvah is for every yid to give mishloach manos. Therefore packages should be prepared which people can give one to another."

**Emphasis:**
- Not just receiving
- Active giving
- Enable participation
- Provide packages

---

### Minimum Requirements

**Contents:**
- 2 types of food, OR
- 2 types of drink, OR
- One food and one drink
- May be same bracha

**Size Requirements:**
- Value of prutah
- Size of k'zayis
- *If needed:* Can be k'zayis combined
- Should look presentable (choshuv) package

---

### Items Requiring Tevila

**If Gifting:**
- Recipient will toivel (if aware)
- Make sure no food touches keli
- Let recipient know to toivel

**If Won't Toivel:**
- Have someone pick up keli on recipient's behalf
- Acquire it for him
- Then toivel for him

---

### Gender Guidelines

**Basic Rule:**
- Man gives to man
- Woman gives to woman

**Giving to Woman:**
- Especially if not married
- Give on behalf of wife
- Encourage her to give to another woman
- If needed: she can give to you for your wife

---

### Family Dynamics

**Sons or Daughters Living at Home:**
- Supported by parents
- Letatchilah: give to friends
- Also yotze by giving to parents (if food belongs to them)
- But parents NOT yotze giving to them

**Brothers and Sisters:**
- Yotze giving to each other
- If food belongs to them

---

### Children and Adults

**Children to Children:**
- Yotze (boy to boy, girl to girl)

**Adult to Child:**
- Should NOT give to child under bar/bas mitzvah
- Adult not yotze

---

### Timing

**Primary Time:**
- Until shkia

**B'dieved:**
- Until tzeis

---

## Seudas Purim

### Mincha First

**Requirement:**
> "Mincha must be davened before the seuda."

**Non-negotiable**
- Plan accordingly

---

### Timing Strategy

**Schedule:**
- A while before shkia
- Noticeable part eaten before shkia
- Continue (farbrenging) into night

---

### Separation

**Requirement:**
> "The seuda and farbrengen must be with total separation between men and women."

**Complete separation essential**

---

### Meal Content

**Universal Custom:**
> "The minhag by all Yidden is to fulfill this mitzvah with a seuda consisting of bread and meat, and this is mentioned in sichos as matter of fact, and you should not change and 'separate from the tzibur' without a specific heter."

**Traditional meal:**
- Bread
- Meat
- Don't deviate without permission

---

### If Running Late

**Minimum Fulfillment:**
- Try to wash and eat
- Bit more than k'beitza (or at least k'zayis)
- Of bread (and meat)
- Before shkiah
- Have in mind to continue meal elsewhere

---

### Alternative Timing

**Morning Option:**
> "Most people wash in the afternoon, one could wash and eat a seuda in the morning if one wants to."

**Valid approach**
- If concerned about time
- Morning seudah acceptable

---

### Don't Forget

**Essential:**
- Remember to recite Birchas Hamazon
- Remember to daven Maariv
