## Main Themes Covered

### Personal Connection to Rebbe's Father
- Reb Levi Yitzchok's imprisonment and golus
- Chamisha Asar Bishvat as his day of liberation/freedom
- First time experiencing relative freedom after 11 months
- Deep joy and simcha on this date

### Rebbe's Observance of the Date
- Starting from 5731, Rebbe farbrenged annually on this date
- Previously only farbrenged when fell on Shabbos
- Change coincided with publication of Likkutei Levi Yitzchok
- Rebbe revealed: "Eppes Hot mir Geshtuped" to farbreng from the Ohel

### Major Topics Discussed on This Date
1. **Hiskashrus**: First official letter as Nasi (5711)
2. **New Mivtzoim**: Launch of Mivtza Torah (5731), Sheva Mitzvos (5743), Children's rooms (5747)
3. **Geulah and Moshiach**: Powerful sichos demanding geulah
4. **Chidushei Torah**: System of "Leshitoso" in learning

### Historical Significance
- Rebbe's father freed from strict surveillance on this date (5740)
- Anniversary commemorated by Rebbe through annual farbrengens
- Elevation of parents' importance in Lubavitch
- Integration into Chabad calendar
