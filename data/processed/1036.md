## South American Theme Possibilities

### Geographic Regions

**Brazil:**
- Carnival connection (perfect Purim parallel!)
- Samba music and dancing
- Colorful costumes
- Beach/tropical theme
- Christ the Redeemer imagery
- Amazon rainforest elements
- Soccer/football culture
- Caipirinha mocktails

**Argentina:**
- Tango dancing
- Gaucho cowboys
- Pampas grasslands
- Buenos Aires elegance
- Mate tea service
- Beef/asado BBQ
- European-influenced architecture

**Peru:**
- Machu Picchu theme
- Incan culture
- Llamas and alpacas
- Colorful textiles
- Pan flute music
- Ceviche (kosher version)
- Ancient ruins aesthetic

**Colombia:**
- Coffee culture
- Colorful architecture (Cartagena)
- Cumbia music
- Emerald green decor
- Flower festivals
- Diverse landscapes

**Chile:**
- Wine regions
- Pacific coastline
- Moai statues (Easter Island)
- Andes mountains
- Unique geography (long thin country)
