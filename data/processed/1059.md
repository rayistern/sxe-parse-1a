# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Mivtza Purim - Comprehensive Guidelines (English)

## Note: Purim Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Operational guidelines for Purim outreach

**Language:** English

**Purpose:** Complete reference for Shluchim conducting Mivtza Purim

---

## General Preparations

### Timeline

**30 Days Before Purim:**
- Begin all preparations
- Planning and organization
- Resource gathering
- Team coordination

**Key Preparations Include:**
1. Ba'al koreh for Megillah
2. Ba'al koreh for Torah reading (Parshat Zachor/Amalek)
3. Mishloach manot packages prepared
4. Coins for matanot l'evyonim
5. Purim guide/informational materials

---

### Universal Outreach

**Target Everyone:**
- Every Jew without exception
- Including children not yet of chinuch age
- Babies and toddlers can fulfill mitzvot
- No one too young or too old

**Priority Locations:**
- Old age homes
- Hospitals
- Prisons
- Orphanages
- Army bases

**Rationale:**
- Reach isolated Jews
- Bring joy to difficult situations
- Fulfill mitzvot where most needed
- Maximize impact

---

### Connection to Other Mivtzoim

**Opportune Time:**
- Promote Mivtza Torah
- Promote Mivtza Tefillin
- Purim opens doors
- Festive atmosphere conducive to engagement

**Broader Mission:**
> "All details in the shlichus of spreading Torah, yiddishkeit and chassidus, need to be permeated with this point — how this leads to kabolas moshiach tzidkeinu."

---

## Mishloach Manot and Matanot L'evyonim

### Special Emphasis

**Why These Mitzvot Matter:**
- Fundamental elements of Purim
- Easiest to fulfill
- Most neglected (paradoxically)
- Yetzer hara interferes specifically here
- Even those meticulous in other mitzvot may neglect these
- Youth especially neglected

**Rebbe's Statement:**
- Great merit in promoting these
- Publicize their importance everywhere
- Share how easy they are to fulfill

---

### Matanot L'evyonim - Easy Fulfillment

**Rebbe's Example:**
- Even children can fulfill
- Give 5 cents to 2 evyonim
- 5 cents to each evyon
- Total: 10 cents fulfills mitzvah

**Modern Application:**
- Adjust for inflation
- Make it accessible
- Remove barriers
- Emphasize simplicity

---

### Mishloach Manot - Easy Fulfillment

**Rebbe's Example:**
- Even children can fulfill
- Give apple and candy
- Or piece of cake and soda/juice
- Simple, accessible, achievable

**Key Point:**
- Don't over-complicate
- Two items sufficient
- Can be very basic
- Focus on mitzvah, not extravagance

---

## Megillah Reading Considerations

### Common Challenge

**For Many People:**
- Megillah reading is highlight
- Most difficult mitzvah to execute properly
- Ba'al koreh often pressured to read quickly
- Words swallowed or mispronounced
- Result: No one is yotze

**Consequence:**
- Invalid reading
- Mitzvah not fulfilled
- Wasted effort
- Disappointment

---

### Quality vs Quantity

**Better Option If Necessary:**
- Focus on other mitzvot (mishloach manot, matanot l'evyonim)
- Skip Megillah reading if quality can't be ensured
- Invalid reading worse than no reading
- People can fulfill mitzvah elsewhere

**However:**
- Make every effort for proper reading
- Invest in good ba'al koreh
- Slow down despite pressure
- Quality paramount

---

## Megillah Reading - Detailed Guidelines

### Timing: Night vs Day

**If Choosing Between Night or Day:**
- Preferable to choose day reading
- Allows other mitzvot to be fulfilled properly
- Logistically easier for many

---

### Night Reading Timing

**Proper Time:**
- From tzeis hakochavim (nightfall)
- Until alos hashachar (dawn)
- Should NOT read after alos
- Exception: B'shas hadchak gadol until netz hachama

**Leniencies for Early Reading:**
- If absolutely cannot read after tzeis
- Can rely on opinions allowing:
  - After shkiah (sunset)
  - Even from plag hamincha (in great need)
- Read with bracha

**Important Note:**
- According to Alter Rebbe (and Gra)
- Plag hamincha earlier than often written in calendars
- Check accurate halachic times

---

### Who Should Wait

**Shliach and Family:**
- Should wait until after tzeis if possible
- Have kavanah NOT to be yotze at early reading
- Can eat something after fast ends
- Then hear Megillah properly

**Eating Before Megillah:**
- Lichatchila wait until after Megillah
- If being yotze a while after fast
- Or if weak person difficult to read without eating
- Can eat snack beforehand

**Definition of Snack:**
- Any non-alcoholic drink
- Fruit or vegetable
- Less than k'beitza of mezonot
- If insufficient: can eat more if designate shomer (someone to remind) to read/hear Megillah after

---

### Day Reading Timing

**Proper Time:**
- From netz hachama (sunrise)
- Until shkiah (sunset)

**Leniency Before Netz:**
- Can begin after alos hashachar
- With bracha

**After Shkiah:**
- If reading after shkiah
- Or estimate will finish after shkiah
- Read WITHOUT bracha

---

### Brachos When Already Yotze

**If Already Fulfilled Obligation:**
- Or will be yotze later
- Ask one of listeners to stand and recite brachos for everyone

**If Not Possible:**
- Say brachos yourself
- Or have listener repeat word-for-word after you
- Like by Mivtza Tefillin and similar

---

### Brachos for Women

**Women's Bracha:**
- "Lishmoa Megillah" (to hear Megillah)
- Not "al mikra Megillah"
- If said "al mikra," still yotze

**Bracha After Reading:**
- Only if minyan present
- Women can be counted toward minyan for this
- Some opinions: without Shem U'Malchus if only women

**Rebbe's Custom (Minhagei Ha'admor):**
- Recite blessing even when reading privately

---

### Multiple Readers

**If Necessary:**
- Reading can be divided among multiple ba'alei kriah
- Helpful for stamina
- Share the burden
- Maintain quality

---

### Technology and Megillah

**NOT Valid:**
- Microphone
- Telephone
- Zoom
- Any electronic transmission
- Must hear live voice directly

---

### Doubtful Listeners

**If All Listeners:**
- Such that doubtful they'll listen to entire reading
- Due to illness, fatigue, etc.
- Do NOT say brachos

---

### Mechitza Requirements

**Private Home - One Family:**
- No need for separation concerns
- Family setting, informal

**Multiple Families or Public Place:**
- Workplace, school, etc.
- Should ensure separate seating

**Shul, Chabad House, Official Program:**
- MUST have mechitza
- No exceptions
- Proper separation required

---

## Matanot L'evyonim - Detailed Guidelines

### Tzedaka Boxes

**If No Evyonim Present:**
- Prepare tzedaka boxes
- Money distributed later
- Still valid

**Emphasis to Community:**
- Preferable: money reaches evyon on Purim day itself
- Easy nowadays via internet
- Halachically valid: putting in pushka for later distribution

---

### Minimum Amount

**Basic Requirement:**
- Value of prutah (approximately 5 cents)
- To each evyon (2 evyonim minimum)

---

### Special Locations (Prisons, Institutions)

**Money Handling Restrictions:**
- Some places forbid cash handling
- Explain: money won't stay with person
- Will immediately go into tzedaka box

**If They Don't Agree:**
- Someone else picks up money on behalf of prisoner
- Acquires (zocheh) it for him
- Prisoner designates you as shliach
- Give money on his behalf

---

### Where Money Goes

**Actual Evyonim:**
- Should go to poor people
- Not other charitable institutions
- Exception: If mosad makes meals for needy

**Practical Setup:**
- Two boxes:
  1. Matanot l'evyonim (divided among evyonim)
  2. General donations

**If Shliach is Evyon:**
- No sufficient set salary
- No sufficient regular donations
- Minimal needs not met
- Considered evyon
- May use money for family

---

### Timing Emphasis

**Until Shkiah:**
- Primary time for mitzvah
- Until sunset

**B'dieved Until Tzeis:**
- Can fulfill until nightfall
- Not ideal but valid

---

## Mishloach Manot - Detailed Guidelines

### Everyone Gives

**Remember:**
- Mitzvah is for every Yid to give
- Not just receive
- Prepare packages people can give one another
- Enable active participation

---

### Minimum Requirements

**Contents:**
- 2 types of food, OR
- 2 types of drink, OR
- 1 food and 1 drink
- May be same bracha

**Minimum Size:**
- Value of prutah (per item, or combined b'shaas hadchak)
- Size of k'zayis (per item, or combined b'shaas hadchak)
- Should look presentable/important (choshuv)

---

### Items Requiring Tevila

**If Gifting Utensil:**
- Recipient will toivel it (if aware)
- Ensure no food touches keli
- Inform recipient to toivel

**If Recipient Won't Toivel:**
- Have someone else acquire (hagbaha) keli for recipient
- Then toivel it for him

---

### Gender Guidelines

**Basic Rule:**
- Man gives to man
- Woman gives to woman

**Giving to Woman:**
- Especially if unmarried
- Give on behalf of your wife
- Encourage her to give to another woman
- If needed: she can give to you for your wife

---

### Family Dynamics

**Children Living at Home:**
- Supported by parents
- Lechatchila: give to their friends
- Also yotze by giving to parents (if food belongs to them)
- BUT parents NOT yotze by giving to dependent children

**Siblings:**
- Yotze by giving to each other
- If food belongs to them

---

### Children and Adults

**Children to Children:**
- Yotze (boy to boy, girl to girl)

**Adult to Child:**
- Adult NOT yotze by giving to minor (under bar/bas mitzvah)

---

### Timing Emphasis

**Until Shkiah:**
- Primary time for mitzvah
- Until sunset

**B'dieved Until Tzeis:**
- Can fulfill until nightfall
- Not ideal but valid

---

## Seudas Purim

### Mincha First

**Must Daven Mincha:**
- Before seudah
- Non-negotiable
- Plan accordingly

---

### Timing Strategy

**Schedule Seudah:**
- Sufficient time before shkiah
- Noticeable part eaten before sunset
- Continue (farbrengen) into night
- Split across day/night boundary

---

### Separation Requirements

**Complete Separation:**
- Men and women
- Seudah and farbrengen
- No exceptions
- Proper mechitza

---

### Meal Content

**Universal Jewish Custom:**
- Seudah with bread and meat
- Mentioned in sichos as matter of fact
- Should NOT change or "separate from tzibur"
- Need specific heter to do differently

---

### If Running Late

**Minimum Before Shkiah:**
- Wash and eat at least k'beitza of bread (or k'zayis minimum)
- And meat
- Have kavanah to continue meal elsewhere
- Fulfill basic obligation before sunset

---

### Alternative Timing

**Morning Option:**
- If concerned about running late
- Can wash and eat seudah in morning
- Valid approach

---

### Don't Forget

**Essential:**
- Birchas HaMazon
- Daven Maariv
- Easy to overlook in simcha

---

## Mivtzoim Extension

### For Those on Mivtzoim

**Can't Attend Full Seudah:**
- Still fulfill mishte v'simcha
- Eat a hamantash
- Say l'chaim
- Brief but meaningful
- Connects to Purim joy
