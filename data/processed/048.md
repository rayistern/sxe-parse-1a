## Key Situations Covered

**Situation One: Teaneck Menorah**
- Rebbe's instruction: "Bitchila kosavti lo shelo lehadlik eish hamachlokes"
- Senator's advice to back off
- Rebbe's response: "V'harei boze hatzedek itoy"
- Result: Backed off, worked it out next year

**Situation Two: Mayor Koch in New York**
- Rabbi Butman wanted to stand strong
- Rebbe wrote back: Don't start a Machlokes
- Result: Got different public space

**Key Principle:**
> "Clearly, while the Rebbe defends the right to put up a public Menorah, he doesn't want us to pick fights about them."
