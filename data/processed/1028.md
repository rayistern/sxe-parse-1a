## Response 1: Panoply Game

### Shneur Zalman Teitelbaum, Morris Park, NY

**Posted:** Wed Feb 25, 2015 23:23 pm ET

---

### Game Description

**Name:** Panoply

**Format:** Team-based trivia with variety

**Setup:**
> "it's a form of trivia with all types of questions, not just regular questions. each table is a team and gets papers to fill out the answers."

---

### Question Categories

**Variety of Types:**
- What company is this logo?
- Who said this quote?
- Play a piece of music and ask for part of lyrics or name of composer
- True or false?
- In what city in Israel is [landmark]?
- Etc.

**Key Feature:** Not just traditional trivia - multiple question types keep it interesting

---

### Structure

**How It Works:**

**1. Each Category Has Multiple Questions:**
> "each of these categories could have 10-15 questions"

**2. Paper Per Table:**
> "a paper on each table to fill the answers"

**3. Turn In and Score:**
> "the papers are turned in, marked and scored and the winning table gets a prize"

---

### Execution

**Volunteers Needed:**
> "works best with a volunteer or team of volunteers planning and running it"

**Advance Preparation:**
- Create question categories
- Develop 10-15 questions per category
- Prepare answer sheets
- Organize answer key
- Recruit scorers

---

### Request to Community

> "If anyone ever did this and has a list of questions please post!"

### Game Description

**Source:** JLI (Jewish Learning Institute) game cards

**Implementation:**

**Placement:**

### Benefits

**For Mixed Tables:**

- Flexible timing
- Conversation starter
- Thoughtful engagement

**For Program:**
- Self-directed activity
- Leader can facilitate
- Creates meaningful moments
- Educational content

## Detailed: Panoply Game Implementation

### Creating Questions

**Logo Category:**
- Show 10 company logos
- Mix Jewish and general
- Vary difficulty
- Include local businesses

**Quote Category:**
- Famous quotes
- Mix Jewish and general
- Historical and contemporary
- Include Rebbe's quotes

**Music Category:**
- Play song snippets
- Ask for lyrics or composer
- Mix Jewish and general
- Include Purim songs

**True/False:**
- Historical facts
- Jewish law questions
- Purim story details
- Community trivia

**Geography:**
- Israeli cities
- World Jewish communities
- Historical locations
- Modern Israel

---

### Sample Questions

**Logos (Show Image):**
1. What company is this? [Coca-Cola logo]
2. What Jewish organization? [Chabad logo]
3. What Israeli brand? [Osem]

**Quotes:**
1. "Don't ask what your country can do for you..." [JFK]
2. "If I am not for myself, who will be for me?" [Hillel]
3. "A little light dispels much darkness" [The Rebbe]

**Music (Play Clip):**
1. Name this composer [Beethoven]
2. What song is this? [Hava Nagila]
3. Complete the lyrics: "Mishenichnas Adar..."

**True/False:**
1. Esther was originally named Hadassah [True]
2. Purim is celebrated on the same date everywhere [False - Shushan Purim]
3. We read Megillah three times [False - twice]

**Geography:**
1. In what city is the Kotel? [Jerusalem]
2. Where is Chabad headquarters? [Brooklyn, NY]
3. What city never sleeps? [Tel Aviv... or NYC]

## Detailed: JLI Discussion Cards

### Types of Questions

**Values-Based:**
- "What's a value you learned from your parents?"
- "What Jewish value do you most admire?"
- "How do you define success?"

**Personal Reflection:**
- "What are you grateful for today?"
- "What's a lesson you learned this year?"
- "What inspires you?"

**Jewish Content:**
- "What's your favorite Jewish holiday and why?"
- "What Jewish memory stands out from childhood?"
- "What mitzvah speaks to you most?"

**Light and Fun:**
- "If you could have dinner with anyone in history, who?"
- "What's your hidden talent?"
- "What would you do with a million dollars?"

---

### Implementation Tips

**Preparation:**
- Print on nice cardstock
- Different question per person
- Under each plate
- Or in cloth napkin

**Facilitation:**
- Announce during meal
- Invite table sharing
- Circulate to listen
- Ask individuals to share with group

**Timing:**
- Between courses
- Natural conversation time
- Not during speeches
- Relaxed atmosphere

## Detailed: Mitzvahnaire Game

### Game Format

**Based On:** "Who Wants to be a Millionaire"

**Questions:** Jewish/mitzvah-themed

**Progressive Difficulty:**
- Start easy
- Get harder
- Final question very challenging

**Lifelines:**
- Ask the Rabbi
- Poll the Audience
- Call a Friend (another table)

---

### Sample Questions

**$100 Question:**
- "How many nights does Chanukah last?"
- A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10

**$1,000 Question:**
- "What does 'Purim' mean?"
- A) Lots  B) Joy  C) Queen  D) Victory

**$10,000 Question:**
- "In what year did the Rebbe arrive in America?"
- A) 1939  B) 1941  C) 1943  D) 1945

**$100,000 Question:**
- "Which tractate of Talmud deals primarily with Shabbat?"
- A) Berachot  B) Shabbat  C) Eruvin  D) Pesachim

---

### Participant Selection

## Choosing the Right Game

### Consider Your Audience

**Mostly Families:**
- Panoply (team-based, all ages)
- Mitzvahnaire (entertaining, educational)

**Mostly Adults:**
- JLI cards (meaningful discussions)
- More complex Panoply

**Large Event:**
- Mitzvahnaire (whole group)
- Panoply (manageable at tables)

**Intimate Gathering:**
- JLI cards (personal sharing)
- Simple trivia

---

### Logistics

**Time Available:**
- 15 minutes: Mitzvahnaire segment
- 30 minutes: Panoply throughout meal
- Ongoing: JLI cards

**Volunteer Capacity:**
- Panoply: Need scorers
- Mitzvahnaire: Need host
- JLI cards: Minimal staff

**Budget:**
- Panoply: Low (paper, printing)
- JLI cards: Low to moderate (purchase cards)
- Mitzvahnaire: Low (use Exchange version)

---

## Combining Approaches

### Multi-Game Strategy

**During Meal:**
- JLI cards for table discussions

**Between Courses:**
- One round of Mitzvahnaire

**Throughout:**
- Panoply answer sheet available
- Turn in before dessert
- Announce winner at end

**Benefits:**
- Variety
- Multiple engagement styles
- Appeals to different personalities
- Maintains energy
