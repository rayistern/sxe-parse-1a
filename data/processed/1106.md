# [Misc > For Writer > _5 Chanukah > General] Passover Seder Outline - Monica Study Group 2016

## Note: Passover Content (Not Chanukah)

**Resource Type:** Complete Seder guide for study group

**Year:** 2016

**Audience:** Monica study group (women's group)

**Purpose:** Interactive Seder with depth for each of 15 steps

**Format:** Step-by-step guide with stories, discussion questions, personal applications

---

## Opening Notes

**Preparation Reminders:**
> "Pesach guest who walked off with the bone - prepare your guests, prepare yourself for the night, make it interactive"

**The Mitzvah:**
> "The mitzvah of the night is V'higadta L'vincha and you shall tell your child, this is a night for children and the child inside of us to ask questions and break free from anything that limits us"

---

## How? The Seder - Organized Plan of Action

**Definition:**
> "Seder - organized plan of action"

**Purpose:**
> "The seder consists of 15 steps and each step is part of a process in becoming a free person."

**Symbolism:**
> "The moon take 15 days to grow to its fullest, pesach occurs on the 15th day of the month and the seder has 15 steps as guide for us to grow and reach personal freedom."

---

## 1. Kadesh (Sanctification)

### Details
- First of 4 cups
- Customary to have others pour for you - "we are like free people tonight"
- 3.4 ounces minimum requirement
- Try to drink all at once
- Leaning to the left

### Meaning of Sanctification

> "In the words of Kiddush we say that - '...sanctify also comes from the word - to separate (like groom and bride)'"

**Teaching:**
> "The Seder begins by recognizing in order for me to accomplish whatever I want to tonight, I have to first separate myself from my mundane, hum drum life, who am I, what is my mission on this world"

### Identity Question

**Ask:** "Who are we?"

**Answer:**
- A neshama
- A child of Avraham
- Story of Yankele the ganiv [thief]

> "I have the ability to be free from the limits of the natural world, where only our logic seems to function. Our g-dly soul is the first step to freedom."

---

## 2. Urchatz (Washing)

### Details
- 3 times on right, 3 times on left
- No blessing
- "This is how they did it in the temple"
- About to eat wet vegetable

### Meaning

> "The conclusion we have just made is something that we have resolved in our minds. However, to be of value, it needs to be brought into the reality of our physical existence."

**Symbolism:**
> "Idea of washing hands - hands represents the way we interact with the physical world"

---

## 3. Karpas (Vegetable in Salt Water)

### Details
- Dipping a veggie into salt water
- Say blessing HaAdamah
- Have in mind the maror you'll eat later
- "Eat up! You won't be getting some for a while!"

### Purpose

**Teaching:**
> "The Talmud teaches us that the custom of karpas has no reason. It is just there to provoke a question. Why are we dipping a vegetable in salt water?"

**Surface Explanation:**
- Salt water reminds us of tears shed while slaves in Egypt

**Essential Reason:**
- Simply to make us wonder
- Provoke questions

**Educational Value:**
> "More than we need to teach our children to learn, we need to teach them to ask questions. Brilliant book of Talmud is also q and a form"

**Freedom Message:**
> "In order to be free you have to challenge and question your previous assumptions. We have this bizarre ritual just to break us out of our comfort zone and ask, 'Hey', what and why am I doing what I do? Karpas your questions are an important part of becoming free."

### Discussion Question

> "Avodas perech - man did work of women did work of men - question: were you ever given a job that was pure torture simply because you didn't feel it suited your style, personality"

---

## 4. Yachatz (Breaking the Middle Matzah)

### Details
- Smaller piece - leave on table, "this is the poor man's bread" (they don't eat whole food)
- Larger piece for afikomen - hide or give
- Sefardim: Custom to tie it under kids' arms like they left Egypt

### Meaning

> "Breaking the middle matzo teaches that part of becoming free requires of us to break and shatter our comfortable and familiar habits."

**Slave Mentality:**
> "It's easier to take a person out of slavery than to take slavery out of a person"

**Story:** Beggar with brass door knockers

### The Pain of Change

> "We are married to our slave ideas and its hard to give them up - in this process there will be a breaking. I must expect that many ideas and beliefs that I am emotionally connected to will get shaken up - which can be painful."

### Pause for Reflection

> "Pause for a moment - what is an area of life, the voice of the pharaoh within you that may be limiting? A bad habit you have fallen into or way of thinking that you know is not helping?"

**Activity:** Watch freedom video

---

## 5. Maggid (Telling the Story)

### Readiness

> "After completing the first four steps, we are ready. Ready to delve into a story of the supernatural. Ready to remember leaving the Egypt of the past. Ready to leave the Egypt of our time."

### Chabad Custom - 4 Questions

**Note:**
> "Read about the custom first - value of customs, they keep our children tied to our tradition."

**Campus Observation:**
> "I see on campus, you could have a sign up for prayer, shake the lulav... but a bowl of chicken soup or an opportunity to make challa, something wakes up inside and they are comfortable to get involved Jewish - continue the customs of your FAMILY"

### How to Say Haggadah

- "Say the haggadah in a joyful tune"
- "Involve children"
- "Say it in first person, when we were slaves in Egypt..."

### 4 Sons

**Why the Order?**
- Chacham is near Rasha - so he can influence
- Rasha first - "because at least he has passion, the worst is apathy - I'm cool, couldn't care less!"

**Discuss:**
- Advantage of each son

**5th Son:**
> "Rebbe didn't want to leave an empty chair, fill the chair!!"

### Dayenu - Gratitude

[Express gratitude]

## 6. Rachtzah (Washing with Blessing)

### The Pattern

> "The order of the Seder is: Transcend, and then purify. Rise to a higher spiritual level, and draw that higher level into your physical life. Then do it again. Rise to a still higher level and draw it into your life. It is a process that goes on forever. It will only end when we reach the highest possible level of G-d. And He is infinite!"

**Continual Growth:**
> "Growing and learning never ends - the more you know the more you desire to learn some more"

---

## 7. Motzi (Blessing on Bread)

### Purpose

> "Our goal is not just to believe in miracles. We must also seek to see G-d in the process of the natural world."

### Story

**Boy from Hebrew School:**
> "If I would tell you..."

### Miracle Glasses

> "Pesach we recognize that miracles are the foundation of our nation - let's not dilute that! We need to wear miracle glasses and see them around us, big and small."

**Examples:**
- Monica called me
- I had a baby
- Time passed and then our group came together

> "Call it nature, call it a miracle! Nature is also miraculous"

**Personal Example:**
> "Since I have become pregnant Sarelka has become so independent. I guess, this is part of the miraculous system that G-d set up to have children slowly become independent."

---

## 8. Matzah (Eating Matzah)

### The Mitzvah of the Night

> "Eating Matza - the mitzvah of the night. This is what the seder is leading up to - what it's all about."

### What Matzah Represents

> "Matzo is food of faith and food of healing second night and we want to internalize it. The humility that matza represents is the first step in leaving our personal Egypt's and forging a relationships with G-d."

**Tangible Mitzvah:**
> "We don't just talk or meditate about humility but we tangibly eat it! And rid our homes, to the EXTREME of everything else."

### Amount and Care

> "Very careful to eat the right amount - it's spiritual medicine - make sure we eat the right amount: 1/2 to 3/4 matza"

**Personal Story:**
> "My grandfather the past couple of years, with the few teeth he had left and had huge issue with swallowing would painstakingly eat the matzo"

### Details
- Eat leaning to the left
- "Decorating pillow cases"

---

## 9. Maror (Bitter Herbs)

### Purpose

> "Having thought about the freedom from the limitations of the mind, we must now remember what psychological slavery is like."

**Examples:**
- Poor self esteem
- Desire to please

### Details
- Eat enough about small egg
- Wrap in romaine
- Dip but do not liberally apply the charoset

---

## 10. Korech (Sandwich)

### Meaning

> "The point is not to have the faith when everything is proceeding as planned. It is when there are obstacles, that we must rise to the occasion."

**Stories:**
- Fruma and Chanchie
- Shliach who bought the sunglasses and sunscreen on Lag B'Omer

---

## 11. Shulchan Orech (The Meal)

### Why Eat a Meal at Seder?

> "Why do eat a meal at a seder? If the point of seder is spiritual exercise of freedom why eat? Go to synagogue and pray!"

**Answer:**
> "Because every spiritual advance has to be bound to the physical - we don't leave our physical self at home, attend synagogue, have a spiritual exercise and return. We bring our physical side with us and imbue it with the ideas of seder night."

### Integration

> "The freedom that we have experienced tonight must not remain something which we feel only during holy-pursuits. We must weave it into all aspects of our lives. So we eat."

### Custom

> "Custom to start meal with boiled egg - remember egg is a sign of mourning and reminds us of the temple that was destroyed and sacrifices that we used to be able to bring there"

**Share 3 recipes**

---

## 12. Tzafun (Hidden - Finding the Afikomen)

### The Question

> "From where is the power going to come to leave mitzrayim today? Not from outside ourselves."

**Answer:**
> "Hidden within every Jew is the perfect faith in the supernatural and in G-d's infinite power. All we have to do is find it."

### After the Seder Experience

> "Hopefully after experiences much of the seder, eating matza, remembering how bitter it is to be slaves to anybody besides we g-d the hidden spark within every Jew comes out and we feel close to G-d in this world"

### Modern Application

**Story:** Shimona Tzuernuk - Coca Cola

> "From where do we find the power to fight a million dollars a minute of values? Believe in the soul. Nourish the soul with kosher food, torah, mitzvos, halacha, see the soul in others"

**Teen Example:**
- Prom
- "But to me..."

---

## 13. Berach (Blessing After Meal)

### Timing
- Pour cup before bentching
- Drink after

### Two and Two

> "We have drunk two cups celebrating our miraculous past. Now, we will drink two cups celebrating our miraculous future."

---

## 14. Hallel (Praise)

### Opening the Door

**First:** Open door for Eliyahu Hanavi

**Story:** Melamed and Eliyahu Hanavi, giving away salary etc.

> "For all this we praise G-d with Hallel."

---

## 15. Nirtzah (Acceptance)

### Waiting for G-d

> "Wait for G-d to accept our seder - to finish the process of freedom, G-d needs to do his part to liberate us"

> "Was it the best seder? It was the best one for this year 2016"

### Divine Partnership

> "Ultimately, we cannot truly leave Egypt on our own. After completing the Seder, we turn to G-d and ask him to accept our efforts and liberate us."

### Study Group Note

> "Conclude with note from kinus - lady said to stay as rebbetzin, I can do that most of the time but I remember when Shaya diagnosed, it came out in study group, crying because we have something special here, feel open a sense of trust, open arms whenever you need a hug so to remind myself and each of us to keep what comes out here private and maintain the special space this is."

### Conclusion

> "We end off our seder with L'shanah haba'ah b'Yerushalayim!!!"

## Additional Days

### Second Days of Pesach
- We light candles

### 7th Day
- Not celebrating downfall of Egyptians
- Remembering miracle of splitting of the sea

**Night that Sea Split:**
- Leave our doors unlocked
- Decorate synagogues with water/flowers
- Stay up the whole night as G-d did when we passed over
- Remember the song we sang at the sea

**Women's Faith:**
> "Only women had tambourines - reflection of the faith of their time - in the depth of despair, they packed the tambourines, they KNEW that G-d would save them"

**Our Generation:**
> "Our generation is the reincarnation of the souls of that generation - as women, we need to lead with the absolute knowledge and faith that the redemption will happen in our days as well"

### Second Day - Day of Moshiach

> "Mashal of preg [pregnancy], plane, engage only taking you to the next step. Redemption of Passover was only a step to take us to the redemption that we will experience TODAY in OUR TIMES"

**Baal Shem Tov:**
> "Instituted the Moshiach's Feast with matza and 4 cups of wine, leads into the end of Passover"

**Personal Note:**
> "Day of Moshiach - also my grandfather's yartzeit"
