## The 14 Guidelines (Hebrew and English)

### 1. Location

**Hebrew:**
> א. מצוה גדולה להדליק נרות חנוכה ברחובות קריה, "במקום הציבורי והמרכזי ביותר".

**English:**
> "It is a great Mitzvah to light Chanukah lights in the thoroughfares of the city 'in the most public and central location'."

**Source:** Sicha of 6th day of Chanukah 5747; and elsewhere

---

### 2. Seven Noachide Laws

**Hebrew:**
> ב. יש לנצל את המעמד לעורר גם על דבר קיום ז' מצות בני נח.

**English:**
> "The occasion of the public Menorah lighting should be utilized to also highlight the Sheva Mitzvos Bnai Noach."

---

### 3. Does Not Fulfill Personal Obligation

**Hebrew:**
> ג. טוב להכריז שאין אדם יוצא ידי חובתו בהדלקה זו, ואשר כל אחד מצווה להדליק נר חנוכה בביתו.

**English:**
> "It is important to inform those present that the public Menorah lighting does not fulfill one's personal obligation to light Chanukah lights and that it is incumbent upon each individual to light Chanukah lights at home."

**Source:** Sefer Hasichos 5747 vol. 1 p. 172; Hisvaduyos 5747 vol. 2 p. 98

**Halachic Basis:** Rema (Shulchan Aruch 671:7) - One does not fulfill obligation with synagogue candles

---

### 4. Nevertheless, We Make a Brocho

**Hebrew:**
> ד. ואף על פי כן, מברכים על הדלקה זו, וכדין ההדלקה שבבית הכנסת.

**English:**
> "Nevertheless, we recite a Brocho at the public lighting, similar to the Halacha regarding the lighting of Chanukah lights in a Shul etc."

**Source:** Extensive discussion in Nesivim BeS'dei HaShlichus, pp. 242+

**Yaavet'z Quote (cited in sources):**
> "For this mitzvah of Chanukah lights is different, certainly its Brocho is essential, because its entire purpose is for Pirsumei Nissa (publicizing the miracle), and if one lights without a Brocho, one accomplishes nothing, for the observer will think he is lighting for his own needs... therefore there is no place to light without a Brocho."

---

### 5. Women Do Not Make the Brocho

**Hebrew:**
> ה. מדין צניעות, אין לכבד אשה לברך על ההדלקה.

**English:**
> "For reason of Tznius we do not honor a woman with making the Brocho."

**Source:** Megillah 23a; Shulchan Aruch (and Shulchan Aruch HaRav) Orach Chaim 282:3 (5)

---

### 6. Multiple People Can Be Honored

**Hebrew:**
> ו. יכולים לכבד כמה אנשים בהדלקת הנרות. שכל אחד ידליק נר אחד והדומה. אכן בזה האופן, הרי את ההכרזות - את מי מכבדים באיזה נר - יש לעשות לפני הברכות, שלא יהוו ההכרזות וכו' הפסק בין הברכה וקיום המצוה.

**English:**
> "A number of individuals may be honored to each kindle a Chanukah light. However, when doing so all the announcements of the names etc, should be made prior to the Brocho so as not to make a Hefsek (interruption) between the Brocho and kindling of all the lights."

**Practical Application:**
- Can honor multiple people
- Each lights one candle
- **IMPORTANT:** Announce all names BEFORE blessings
- Prevents interruption between brocho and mitzvah

---

### 7. Non-Jews and the Shamash

**Hebrew:**
> ז. אין לתת לנכרי להדליק נרות המצוה. ורבים נוהגים לכבדו בהדלקת השמש.

**English:**
> "A non-Jew should not be honored to light a Chanukah light. Instead, many will honor a non-Jew with kindling the Shamash – which is acceptable."

**Halachic Basis:** "Hadlaka Oseh Mitzvah" - the lighting itself performs the mitzvah (Orach Chaim 671:1)

**Practical Solution:**
- Non-Jews cannot light the main lights
- CAN honor them with lighting shamash
- Common practice

---

### 8. Height Limit

**Hebrew:**
> ח. גובה המנורה לא יעלה על עשרים אמה, היינו שתחתית הלהבות תהא תוך עשרים אמה מן הקרקע.

**English:**
> "The height of the Menorah should not exceed twenty cubits from the ground, i.e. the lowest part of the lights should be within twenty cubits of the ground."

**Source:** Shulchan Aruch 671:6; Pri Megadim Mishbetzos Zahav 5

**Measurement:** 20 cubits ≈ 30-40 feet (depending on which cubit measurement used)

**Key Point:** LOWEST part of flames must be within 20 cubits

---

### 9. Real Flames, Not Electric

**Hebrew:**
> ט. במקום שאפשר להדליק להבה ממש, אין להדליק בנרות חשמליות, כי על נרות של חשמל אי אפשר לברך.

**English:**
> "Whenever it is possible, one should use real flames for the public Chanukah lights, rather than using electric lights - as a Brocho cannot be recited on electric lights."

**Sources:**
- Shu"t Yitzchak Yoreh Deah 120
- She'arim Hametzuyanim BeHalacha 139:4
- Encyclopedia Talmudit vol. 18, entry "Chashmal"
- Igros Kodesh vol. 10 p. 345

---

### 10. Gas/Butane Lights

**Hebrew:**
> י. אף נר של גאז (butane) אין לברך על הדלקתו.

**English:**
> "Likewise, a Brocho should not be recited on lights which are fueled by gas or butane."

**Rationale (from sources):**
- Rav Moshe Feinstein zt"l ruled one doesn't fulfill obligation with gas
- With gas lights, small flame burns above gas canister
- Flame is surrounded by mesh covered in powder that glows when heated
- Observer doesn't see actual flame, only glowing powder
- Therefore problematic for brocho

---

### 11. Kerosene Lights

**Hebrew:**
> יא. רבים נוהגים להדליק נרות הללו בנפט, ויש להם על מה שיסמוכו.

**English:**
> "Many kindle the public menorah using kerosene lights, and there's sufficient Halachic basis for that."

**Source:** Piskei Teshuvos (beginning of 673) discusses this

**Considerations:**
- Some permit in times of need
- Concern about smell causing people to extinguish - not applicable outdoors
- Concern about thick wick being like "avukah" (torch) - varies by setup

**Conclusion:** "Yesh lahem al ma she'yismoch" - there's what to rely on

---

### 12. Minyan Preferred But Not Required

**Hebrew:**
> יב. יש להשתדל שההדלקה תהא בנוכחות עשרה מישראל. ואם אין עשרה, יכול להדליק בברכה.

**English:**
> "Effort should be made to kindle the public Menorah in the presence of at least ten people. However, when that is not achieved, a Brocho may nevertheless be recited."

**Source:** Magen Avraham 671:10 and other commentaries there

**Practical Application:**
- TRY for minyan
- But don't cancel if fewer
- Can make brocho either way

---

### 13. Timing: Plag HaMincha and Duration

**Hebrew:**
> יג. זמן ההדלקה הכי מוקדמת היא מפלג המנחה. אך צריך לתת די שמן שידליק עד חצי שעה תוך הלילה.

**English:**
> "The earliest time that the Menorah may be lit is Plag Hamincha, but there should be sufficient fuel for the lights to last at least 30 minutes past nightfall."

**Source:** Orach Chaim beginning of 672

**Calculation:**
- **Plag HaMincha** = 1¼ hours (sha'os zmaniyos) before sunset
- **Must burn until:** 30 minutes after nightfall (tzeis hakochavim)

**Why This Matters:**
"That is why R. Shmuel Butman needs to fly in a helicopter on Friday" - to light early enough before Shabbos while ensuring it burns 30 minutes into night

---

### 14. Gender Separation

**Hebrew:**
> יד. כבכל אירוע ציבורי, יש להשתדל שתהא הפרדה בין גברים לנשים.

**English:**
> "As with all public events, effort should be made to achieve gender separation."

**Source:** Shulchan Aruch HaRav 315:3

**Rebbe's Language (cited):**
From Seder HaHora'os for Birchas HaChamah: "צריך להיות כפשוט אנשים בפני עצמם ונשים בפני עצמן" - "It must be as is obvious, men by themselves and women by themselves"

**Note in sources:** There it's simpler as each person makes their own brocho, unlike here. "V'yalu'i"

---

### 15. Daily Lighting (Bonus)

**Added note:**
> "With the menorah it should be lit/turned on every day, and not all at once"

**Meaning:** Add one light each night progressively, don't light all eight at once

## Additional Halachic Discussion: Shehecheyanu

### Forum Question

**Scenario:**
Person lighting public menorah on Monday (not first night) has not yet lit at home this Chanukah. Should he say Shehecheyanu out loud?

**Answer:**
> "Being that the lighting he is doing 'b'tzibur' (in public) is totally unrelated to his personal obligation to light, what difference would it make whether or not he lit a menorah yet? Even after he lights the Public menorah he will still be obligated to light a menorah at home with a bracha, and if it is the first time this year (at home) he should also say a Shehecheyanu."

**Conclusion:**
- Public lighting ≠ personal obligation
- If it's past first night, NO Shehecheyanu at public lighting
- Will say it at home when lights for first time
- "Neither out loud or quietly" at public lighting after first night

## Practical Summary for Shluchim

### Must Do

1. ✓ Most public location possible
2. ✓ Announce: doesn't fulfill home obligation
3. ✓ Make brocho (like shul lighting)
4. ✓ Real flames if possible (not electric)
5. ✓ Lowest flames within 20 cubits
6. ✓ After Plag HaMincha
7. ✓ Fuel for 30 min after nightfall

### Should Do

1. ○ Highlight 7 Noachide Laws
2. ○ Try for minyan (10 men)
3. ○ Gender separation
4. ○ Use kerosene if can't use candles/oil

### Don't Do

1. ✗ Don't let woman make brocho publicly
2. ✗ Don't let non-Jew light main lights
3. ✗ Don't announce names after brocho
4. ✗ Don't use electric lights with brocho
5. ✗ Don't use gas/butane with brocho

### May Do

1. △ Honor multiple people (announce before brocho)
2. △ Honor non-Jew with shamash
3. △ Light without minyan (still make brocho)
4. △ Use kerosene (with brocho)

## Special Situations

### Multiple Honorees

**How to structure:**
1. Announce ALL names first
2. "We will honor [names] to light..."
3. THEN make brochos
4. THEN each person lights their candle
5. No talking/announcing between brocho and last candle

---

### Non-Jewish Dignitaries

**Problem:** Want to honor but can't light main lights

**Solution:**
- Honor with lighting shamash
- "Rabim nohagim" - many do this
- Acceptable and common
- Dignitary participates meaningfully

---

### Fuel Types Priority

**Best to Worst:**
1. **Olive oil** (ideal)
2. **Other oils** (kosher)
3. **Wax candles** (acceptable)
4. **Kerosene** (has what to rely on)
5. **Gas/Butane** (don't make brocho)
6. **Electric** (don't make brocho)

---

### Friday Afternoon

**Challenge:** Must light before Shabbos but must burn 30 min into night

**Rav Butman's Solution:** Helicopter lighting early

**Alternatives:**
- Light as late as possible before Shabbos
- Use LOTS of fuel
- Calculate exact times carefully
- Some use Thursday night public lighting instead

## Common Questions Addressed

### Q: Can we make brocho if fewer than 10 people?
**A:** Yes. Try for minyan but brocho is valid either way.

### Q: Can woman light the menorah?
**A:** She can light, but should not make the brocho publicly (tznius).

### Q: Can mayor/non-Jewish dignitary light?
**A:** Not the main lights, but can honor with shamash.

### Q: Electric menorah okay?
**A:** If real flames possible, no. Can't make brocho on electric.

### Q: What about gas lights?
**A:** Brocho should not be recited. Use kerosene instead.

### Q: How high can menorah be?
**A:** Lowest part of flames must be within 20 cubits (~30-40 feet).

### Q: What time to light?
**A:** Earliest: Plag HaMincha. Must burn 30 min past nightfall.

### Q: Multiple people lighting - when announce names?
**A:** BEFORE brochos, not between brocho and lighting.

**Bottom Line:**
These authoritative guidelines from Vaad Rabbonei Lubavitch address the most common halachic questions about public menorah lightings. Key takeaways: Use real flames when possible, make brocho (like shul), announce it doesn't fulfill home obligation, honor non-Jews with shamash only, announce all honorees before brocho, light after Plag with fuel for 30 min past nightfall, and try for minyan but not required. When in doubt about fuel type, kerosene has what to rely on, but gas/electric should not have brocho. These aren't just suggestions - they're halachic requirements and best practices from the central Chabad rabbinic authority.
