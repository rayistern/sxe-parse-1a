## Class Content Overview

### Introduction: Two Names, One Person

**Biblical Text:**
> "And he [Mordechai] had brought up Hadassah, that is Esther..." (Esther 2:7)

**Central Question:**
- Why does Esther have two names?
- What is the significance of each name?
- How do these names reflect different aspects of her identity?

---

### Talmudic Interpretations

**Three Explanations (Megillah 13a):**

1. **"Esther was her proper name"**
   - Hadassah: Named after the myrtle
   - Righteous called myrtles
   - Reference: "He stood among the myrtle trees" (Zachariah 1:8)

2. **"Hadassah was her name" (R. Judah)**
   - Esther: From "astir" (concealed)
   - She concealed her identity
   - "Esther did not make known her people or her kindred" (Esther 2:20)

3. **"Hadassah was her name" (R. Nehemiah)**
   - Esther: Nations called her after Istahar (planet Jupiter)
   - Universal appeal
   - How gentiles perceived her

---

### Kabbalistic Framework

#### Sefirat Malchut - The Emanation of Kingship

**Unique Quality:**
- Only emanation that descends to lower world
- First nine emanations remain in their world
- Malchut bridges worlds
- Becomes inner essence of lower world's crown

**Two Dimensions:**
- **Ocean (Yam):** Kingship in its own world, hidden
- **Land (Yabasha):** Kingship revealed in lower world

**Parallel to Esther:**
- **Hadassah:** Kingship in righteous world (G-dliness)
- **Esther:** Kingship concealed in exile

---

### Application to the Soul

#### Israel and Jacob - Head and Heel

**Two Names for Jewish People:**

**Yisroel (Israel):**
- Letters: Yud-Shin-Reish-Aleph-Lamed
- Rearranged: "Li Rosh" = "My Head" / "Unto Me a Head"
- Essence of soul remaining above
- G-dly world connection

**Yaakov (Jacob):**
- From "okev" (heel)
- Ray of soul descending into body
- Physical world manifestation
- Lower level

**Parallel to Hadassah/Esther:**
- Hadassah = Israel = Head = Soul Essence
- Esther = Jacob = Heel = Soul's Ray in Body

---

### Key Message: Unity of Soul

**Biblical Teaching:**
> "Hadassah, that is Esther"

**Meaning:**
- The elevated soul essence (Hadassah)
- IS ONE WITH
- The soul's ray in the body (Esther)
- Not separate or disconnected
- Complete unity despite levels

**Practical Implication:**
- Even in lowest state (exile/body)
- Soul retains connection to essence
- G-dliness present always
- No true separation from G-d

---

## Three Loves of Shema

### Framework from "Shema Yisrael"

**"Love G-d with all your heart, soul, and might"**

Three types of love:

---

### 1. Love of the Heart

**Based On:**
- Understanding how G-d fills the world
- G-d is the life of creation
- Meditation on G-d as source of existence

**Result:**
- Love G-d for being my life
- Closeness through understanding
- G-d immanent in creation

**Level:** Light of Esther (revealed, based on creation)

---

### 2. Love of the Soul

**Based On:**
- Understanding G-d's infinite magnitude
- All creation is tiny ray of Infinite Light
- G-d infinitely transcends creation

**Result:**
- Willing to sacrifice physical life
- Love for Infinite transcends love for finite
- Mesirut nefesh (self-sacrifice)

**Level:** Still Light of Esther (related to creation)

---

### 3. Love "With All Your Might"

**Based On:**
- Focus transcends creation entirely
- Direct perception of G-d's Essence
- Core of G-dly Soul (Spark of Creator)
- Beyond understanding or meditation

**Result:**
- Pure love from soul's essence
- Not dependent on intellectual comprehension
- Innate connection to G-d

**Level:** Light of Hadassah (soul's essence)

---

## Exile and Concealment

### When Lights Are Extinguished

**In Times of Exile:**
- Light of G-d concealed
- Difficult to perceive G-dliness
- Both Love of Esther and Love of Hadassah seem unavailable
- Darkness magnified by indulgence in physical desires

**Human Condition:**
- Egocentric pleasure-seeking
- Disconnection from spirituality
- Loss of revealed love
- Spiritual darkness

---

### Beyond Light: Potency of Soul

**Deeper Than Light:**
- Light = Revealed, felt, experienced
- Potency = Latent, hidden, unfelt
- True Esther experience = Potency

**Potency of Soul:**
- Capacity for total self-sacrifice
- Not driven by understanding or love
- Comes from Spark of Creator within
- Activated specifically in darkest exile

**Five Names of Soul:**
1. Nefesh
2. Ruach
3. Neshama
4. Chaya
5. Yechidah (deepest - total unity with G-d)

**Beyond Yechidah:**
- Essence beyond names
- Spark of Creator (not created essence)
- Source of Potency

---

### Paradox of Darkness

**In Darkest Times:**
- Persecution or assimilation
- Complete concealment
- Essence Potency crushed out of soul
- Esther Potency in Esther Times!

**Result:**
- Greatest Hadassah Light emerges
- From deepest Esther Potency
- Hidden essence revealed through pressure

**Hence:** "Hadassah, that is Esther!"
- Esther (concealment/potency) produces greatest Hadassah (revelation/light)

---

## Torah as Empowerment

### The Word "Uman"

**Verse:** "And he [Mordechai] had brought up [uman] Hadassah, that is Esther..."

**"Uman" Meaning:**
- Nursling
- Refers to Torah
- Proverbs 8:30: "I was a 'uman' (nursling) beside Him..."

**Torah's Power:**
- Divinity of Torah-study
- Brings forth Esther Potency
- Activates Spark of Creator
- Especially in darkest exile times

**Mechanism:**
- "Strengthens G-dly Soul and weakens Animalistic Soul"
- Torah study empowers spiritual essence
- Simple Torah reading has profound effect

---

### Practical Application

**In Our Darkest Times:**
- Specifically during assimilation
- When spirituality seems distant
- Simple Torah study has power
- Reading Torah brings forth soul's essence

**Not Dependent On:**
- Deep understanding
- Emotional connection
- Spiritual feeling
- High level of observance

**Simply Through:**
- Opening a Jewish book
- Reading Torah text
- Studying any Jewish teaching
- Activates deepest potency

---

## Conclusion of Class

### Key Takeaways

**1. Two Dimensions of Soul:**
- Hadassah = Essence, Light, Israel, Head
- Esther = Ray, Potency, Jacob, Heel
- Both are one unified soul

**2. Beyond Revelation:**
- Potency deeper than Light
- Concealment can produce greatest revelation
- Esther's concealment is her power

**3. Power of Exile:**
- Specifically in darkest times
- Essence Potency activated
- Greatest light from greatest darkness

**4. Torah's Role:**
- Simple Torah study
- Brings forth Esther Potency
- Which reveals Hadassah Light
- "Uman" (Torah) connects Hadassah and Esther
